Goggle options

Scratch prototypes for Wren's night-vision goggle (in use, over the eyes). Same scale throughout, sized from head_kit.py and SPEC head proportions. Flat shapes only: this is for silhouette and proportion, not the final model.

Coordinates in mm: eye line at y = 0, eye socket plane at z = 0, forward is +z. Head: SPEC 4 proportions (breadth 158, length 195, vertex to chin 229) plus the hood. The current goggle uses head_kit.py exactly (H_HALF 50 / 23 / 24, CUP_L 14, lens barrels r 13.2, 7.5 long at x = ±32, the shroud lip 94 × 43, 7 mm proud). A, B and C keep the two lenses, the band, the per-player glow and the runtime (goggle.ts) unchanged. Never three lenses, no copied housing (G5, G6, G69); B's optics are square so they stay clear of Moth's round barrels.
