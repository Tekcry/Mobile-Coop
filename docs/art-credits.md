## Motion capture (Mixamo, mixamo-retarget)

Animations from Mixamo (mixamo.com, Adobe), royalty-free for commercial use; retargeted onto the game's own rigs; raw files not redistributed.

Approved by Michael 2026-10-25 (docs/mixamo-retarget/BRIEF.md A1-A11). The raw FBX files stay out of git (they are in
`C:/NightShift/scratch/mixamo/clips/` and its `pack-*` subfolders, outside the repo); only clips converted onto Wren are committed and bundled. The
Mixamo skeleton is never used in the game. Source labels per clip: `src/anim/mocapSources.ts`. Rows are added before
each clip module is committed. A clip is `mocap_<slug>`: `src/anim/clips/mixamo/<slug>.ts` (keyed) or
`src/anim/clips/mixamo/trav_<slug>.ts` (traversal channel clip). (mixamo-retarget I9) An Anim Studio only clip sits in
`src/anim/clips/mixamo/studio/<slug>.ts`. The packs, as the site names them: "Pistol/Handgun Locomotion Pack"
(`pack-pistol/`, 20 files), "Pro Rifle Pack" (`pack-rifle/`, 49), "Shooter Pack" (`pack-shooter/`, 15), "Action Adventure
Pack" (`pack-action-adventure/`, 22); a pack clip's title cell starts with its pack's name.

Licence sentence (Mixamo FAQ, read 2026-10-26 by the orchestrator): you can "use both characters and animations royalty free for personal, commercial, and non-profit" projects. Source: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html.
