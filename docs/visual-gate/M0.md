## Why renders sat out the converge cap (vg5t triage)
The unsteady renders (M0: m-f-gallery, card-m-a5-kiosk-1..3, cp-s1-b, cp-s3-c at Epic; VG5: m-a8-vestibule,
m-f-gallery, cp-s1-b, card-m-a5-kiosk-3 at Epic) end with a max-cell delta of 0.008-0.017 after 35-63 reads: not a
slow settle (it would fall towards 0) but a shimmer that never stops. A diff of the same view between the two runs puts
it on sub-pixel bright edges (the gallery railing, desk edges in cp-s1-b): TAAU jitter at Epic native resolution flips
those pixels every frame and moves 1-11 of the 144 cells by ~1/255. Low (0.67 render scale) never shows it. A longer
budget would not help; vg5t's converge takes a "noisy-steady" exit (the 0.9 quantile of the cells under the tolerance,
the worst cell under 0.03), which ends these at the usual 0.6-2 s.
