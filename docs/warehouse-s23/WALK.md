# Warehouse sections 2 and 3 - walk guide

Greybox prototype (no art), for Michael to judge the direction. Branch `feature/warehouse-s23`; once merged, /preview/.
**Menu:** Sector Ops > Warehouse (any difficulty), solo or co-op. All three sections are rebuilt; section 1 also has a
lamp pass so its routes read without night vision.

**Light, all three sections.** Try a run with night vision off. Stealth routes and takedown spots sit at 0.40-0.55 on
the meter: you can see where you are going, but guards only see you fully above about 0.6. Objectives and staffed work
areas are brighter (to 0.8); dark pockets are 0.30 or less. Each room has a wall switch that kills its lamps.

## Section 2 - storage hall (start: the C2 lobby; objective: the case in the cage; exit: the C3 office door)
1. **Ground:** behind the west staged pallets, up aisle A1, over the cage's south fence, take the case at the shelving,
   back over, cross aisle, A3, the north gangway to the office door.
2. **High:** the ladder on R2N in the cross aisle, along the rack tops, the jump over A2 (1.8 m), drop into the cage,
   out by the stock ladder, hand over hand along the sprinkler mains, drop at C3.
3. **VNA:** up A2 to the ladder south of the beam sensor (it trips on the floor, not on a rack top), R1N's top, drop
   north of the beam, in at the issue gate past the cage officer.
- Loud: shoot the lane lamp, force the cage gate.

## Section 3 - assembly hall, mezzanine, offices (start: C3; objective: the terminal in the production office on the deck; exit: the fire lobby)
1. **Ground / ledge:** along the conveyor's south side, the dark parts store, climb the forklift, mantle onto the deck,
   hang and shimmy under the stair-head laser, the office door behind the clerk; back out, drop into the east bay, round
   the plant room to the lobby.
2. **High:** the walkway ladder by the west wall, the crane walkway, the lobby and plant roofs, grab the header pipe,
   hand over hand along the main, drop to the deck, in through the open window; back the same way, the lobby hatch.
3. **Service:** the manager's office (unlocked when C3 seals), past the fitter at the press, the plant room's west door
   (the engineer's panel), unscrew the grate, crawl the duct, out of the grille behind the clerk; back down the duct.
