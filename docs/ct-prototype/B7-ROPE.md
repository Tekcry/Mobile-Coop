# B7 - two-person rope (2026-10-19)

## Built
- **Two-person rope (CT 1.4 Belay Line, G130 / G151 / G178: the rope-climb exception to G110).** STANDING at an edge FACING a drop (the B5 drop probe: 1 m ahead, no floor within 2.2 m under it), Co-op (RB / F) drops a rope (`ropeOffer`, wire mode `brace` + sub bit 4, braced pose). Crouched there stays the B5 hang-down; standing without a drop stays the B4 throw. Prompt "Co-op: drop a rope for your partner (crouch: hang-down)". Polite refusals (G151): no ground under the edge ("The rope does not reach the ground here"), a drop under 2.2 m ("Too low for a rope: just drop down"), over 6 m (the rope's length).
- **Climbing.** The partner below (within 2 m across of the holder, 1.8-6.4 m under them) sees "Co-op: climb the rope (two-person rope)", presses Co-op at the foot and hangs on the rope facing it (`ropeClimb`, wire mode `boost` + sub bit 4, pose `climb` with a cadence from the height). Own left stick: up 1.0 m/s, down 1.6 m/s, between the floor and the top. At the top (feet 1.5 m under the edge) the climber tops out over the edge to a spot 0.8 m beside the holder (0.9 s, `climbUp` pose; falls back to just behind the holder when neither side has floor).
- **Ending / never stranded.** Topping out frees the holder. Co-op or B from either while climbing = let go: the climber slides down the rope to the floor at 3 m/s (no fall, no harm) and the holder is free. A holder who stops holding (death, disconnect, lost message) lets the climber slide down after 1.5 s; a lost end reply frees the holder after 3 s. Offers time out after 20 s.
- **Host-authoritative on the team-move rail (B5 pattern).** The climber sends `tmove` kind `rope` with the rope foot it found in front of the holder (`lx`, `gy` = edge y, `lz`); the host checks both MoveStates (holder offering the rope, climber below in reach, foot within 1.6 m of the holder at their height) and sends `tstart` kind `rope` with `l`; the pair is kept until a `hangEnd` (`pull` = topped out, `drop` = let go), answered by `tend` with `k` to both. The climber's height is its own (kinematic, no correction, no rubber-banding); remotes pose it from MoveState.
- **Station 7 rebuilt.** The placeholder hold console, wire strip and rope post are gone. The wall is a solid 4.4 m, 1 m thick wall across the whole yard with no door (no gate; the airlock waits for both). Flow: crouch + Co-op leg-up one; they pull up onto the wall top, turn back to face the partner, stand and Co-op to drop the rope; the partner climbs it and tops out; both drop down the far side. A far-side return stair (three blocks, 1.1 m rises, against the south fence) lets a player alone on the far side climb back to the top (never stranded). Panel and radio text say what to do.

## Stubs / not done
- No rope mesh is drawn (the rope is implied by the holder's brace and the climber's climb pose). No bespoke holder pose (the leg-up brace crouch, not anchoring with the rope round the back).
- The climb is a kinematic team move, not the solo `rappel` attach (that needs a level anchor; a runtime anchor was out of the time box). The climb pose is the ladder `climb` clip without grips on a rope; the top-out uses `climbUp`.
- The holder offers only facing the drop (the facing direction is probed); the claimed foot is bounded by the host, not re-raycast. Throw over wire / pipe (station 7's other CT lessons) not built.

## Try it
`https://tekcry.github.io/Mobile-Coop/ct/?net=local&autostart=ctcourse&mode=course` in two tabs (the first hosts). At station 7 (the high wall with no door): one crouches by the wall and presses RB / F; the other runs in and presses RB / F (leg-up), then Y / Space to pull up onto the wall. On top, turn to face your partner below, stand (not crouched) at the edge and press RB / F: the rope is down. The partner walks to the wall under you and presses RB / F, then pushes the left stick up to climb; at the top they climb over beside you. Both drop down the far side and go into the airlock. Also try: the holder pressing RB / B mid-climb (the climber slides down safely), the climber pressing B (lets go), crouching at the edge instead (the hang-down), and the far-side stair to climb back up.
