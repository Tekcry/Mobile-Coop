# B5 - hang-down hack (2026-10-19)

## Built
- **Hang-down (CT 1.5 Drop Line, G130 / G151 / G180).** Crouched at an edge FACING a drop (a roof edge or a hatch; looked for 1 m ahead, no floor within 2.2 m under it), Co-op (RB / F) offers the hang-down instead of the leg-up (`hangOffer`, wire mode `brace` + sub bit 2; braced crouch pose). Prompt "Co-op: offer a hang-down (lower your partner)". The partner within 1.6 m sees "Co-op: be lowered head-first (hang-down)", presses Co-op and is lowered head-first over the edge (0.8 s) at the hang point, held by the feet (`hanging`, wire mode `boost` + sub bit 2, body turned over: rig tumble PI).
- **Holder controls the height.** The holder's left stick moves the head 1.0-2.5 m under the edge (0.8 m/s; start 1.5; never closer than 0.3 m to the floor). The depth rides on the holder's MoveState `s`; the hanging player eases to it locally (1.2 m/s), so the wire's steps never snap the body (no rubber-banding).
- **Ending.** Co-op from either player = pulled back up beside the holder (0.9 s, lands crouched). B from the holder = released: the partner turns feet-down and drops to the floor under the hang, lands crouched (no harm: under 3 m, and the game has no fall damage). A holder who stops holding (death, disconnect, lost message) lets the partner drop after 1.5 s; a lost end reply frees the holder after 3 s. Never stranded.
- **Acting while hanging.** The interact button (A / E) works on anything within the game's normal reach of the hanging body: the hack.
- **Host-authoritative on the team-move rail.** The partner sends `tmove` kind `hang` with the hang point it found in front of the holder (`lx`, `gy` = edge y, `lz`); the host checks both MoveStates (holder offering, partner in reach and level, hang point within 1.6 m of the holder at their height) and sends `tstart` kind `hang` with `l`; the pair is kept until `tmove` kind `hangEnd` (target 0 pull / 1 drop) from either, answered by `tend` with `k: 'pull' | 'drop'` to both.
- **Station 5.** The annex roof has a 0.55 m skylight hatch over the back room (behind the grid wall; narrower than a body, so nobody walks or drops in, ledges suppressed at its rim). Striped frame and a striped holder square beside it (the tell). The hack lever placeholder is replaced by the **Hack panel** low on the back room's south wall (1.2 m up, under the hatch; device id `sw-s5-hack`, gate `g-s5-grid` unchanged): reached hanging only once lowered past about 2.3 m. Flow: leg-up + relay onto the roof, roof plates (front door), crouch on the striped square facing the hatch, Co-op; the partner Co-op; holder pushes the stick down; the hanging partner presses A on the panel, the grid door opens; Co-op pulls them up; both drop off the roof and go through. A partner dropped into the back room can press the panel from the floor and walks out through the opened grid.
- **Remote view.** Remotes pose the hanging player from MoveState (`boost` + sub 2 = tumble PI, the same rig path as the inverted pipe hang) and the holder's brace (`brace` pose). Partner blocker parked while hanging (as the boost).

## Stubs / not done
- No bespoke animation: the hanging body is the standing / idle rig turned over (tumble PI); the holder uses the leg-up brace pose, not lying at the edge. The pull-up and drop flip the body at once (half way / at the start).
- No inverse crouch (hide), no upside-down neck snap or pistol use while hanging, no up / down callouts.
- The hang-down needs the holder crouched and FACING the drop (only the facing direction is probed); crouched elsewhere stays the leg-up. A hang point the partner claims is bounded by the host, not re-raycast.
- G44 (guards see poorly above) is not part of this slice (no guards at station 5).
- The kinematic hanging body ignores collision (it passes the narrow hatch like the throw's flight).
