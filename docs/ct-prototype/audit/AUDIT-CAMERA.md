# Audit: CAMERA (2026-10-20)

Covered: explore camera (distance, height, offset, pitch, horizon, follow, recentre), look rate, wall / door pull-in (N1), crawl and duct, ADS rifle / pistol / crouched, shoulder swap, scope, back-to-wall and peek, traversal cameras, takedown / grab / door cameras.
Clips: new 105509, 105624, 105718, 105845, 110038, 110341, 110522, 110714, 111234, 111335, 111731, 111908, 112102; old 051606, 051709, 053525, 055649 (plus `docs/locomotion/research/ct-measure-camera.md` and `ct-measure-walls-doors-combat-vision.md`, rechecked where noted). Frames: `pc/ct-work/frames/camera/`.

Method: CT frames are 2420x1668 (aspect 1.45). Distances assume 75 deg horizontal FOV across that width (f = 1577 px, vertical 55.7 deg), hero 1.8 m; good to about +-15 %. Screen positions are fractions of width (x) / height (y) from the top-left, read off a 10 % grid. Our numbers come from `pc/ct-work/ours/*-ots.png` and `docs/ct-prototype/shots/f/*-ots.png` (16:9, rendered at pitch 0, which anim-sheet forces) and from `src/config/camera.ts`. Measurement was cut short by the usage limit: rows marked "estimate" were not frame-stepped.

## 4. Clipping and believability risks in our renders
- `shots/f/aim-ots`, `aimcrouchpistol-ots`: the head is cut by the left edge and the gun dominates the frame (row 2).
- `walk-ots`, `sprint-ots`: head-to-waist framing hides the legs, so the walk, run and stop work cannot be seen in play in the open yard (row 1).
- `ours/traverse/*.png` use the proving script's own side camera (`scripts/anim-cleanup/traverse-shots.mjs` `cam`), not the game camera, so they say nothing about the traversal framing. In `ladder-climb-1.4` and `drainpipe-1.6` that camera sees Wren through a translucent wall. In `duct-1.0` the duct is never entered (`kind: none`, KICK VENT prompt): flag for the traversal audit.
- During takedowns CT itself clips through the hero (112428 @10-10.5); our `applyBodyFade` hide is better, keep it.

## 5. Not measurable from the footage
- True FOV: no ruler with a known distance in a normal-vision clip. If CT is 75 deg horizontal at 1.45 aspect (vertical 55.7), our 75 deg at 16:9 shows 16 % less height; matching CT's vertical view needs about 86 deg horizontal at 16:9. Every distance above scales with this assumption. Decide with Michael after fixes 1-2.
- The CT rule that picks the open vs tight distance (zone data or geometry), and its blend time: no clip crosses from a corridor to an open room with a steady camera.
- Look rate and acceleration: Michael orbits by hand and input is unknown; only a peak rate could be read.
- Door-open camera and grab / carry cameras: high or top-down because Michael orbited (110936, 052441); no fixed rule visible beyond "normal follow camera".
- Sprint FOV change: none seen at 1-2 fps; not frame-stepped.
