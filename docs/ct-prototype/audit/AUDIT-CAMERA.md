## 0. Method and Michael's answers
- CT frames are 2420x1668 (aspect 1.45). I read screen positions as fractions of width (x) and height (y) from the top-left, using a 10 % grid burned into each frame. Distances assume CT's field of view is 75 deg across the width (f 1577 px, vertical 55.7 deg) and the hero is 1.8 m tall; they are good to about +-15 %. Frame-stepped tiles are named `s<clip>_*_from<s>.jpg`; their `pts` labels count from the `-ss` point.
- Ours: `pc/ct-work/ours/*-ots.png` and `docs/ct-prototype/shots/f/*-ots.png` (16:9; anim-sheet forces pitch 0) and the constants in `src/config/camera.ts`. `pc/ct-work/ours/traverse/*.png` come from the proving script's own camera (`scripts/anim-cleanup/traverse-shots.mjs`, `cam`), not the game camera. So for traversal I compare the `ATTACH_FRAMING` numbers only.
- **Michael's answers (2026-10-20):**
  - Explore shoulder offset: "Match CT (0.15 m)".
  - FOV: "Match CT (86)".
  - "Footage wins" over G103 / F1a.
  - Every row below already applies these answers.
- **FOV 86 means:** 86 deg horizontal at 16:9 gives 55.3 deg vertical, which is CT's 55.7. Every vertical position and size below therefore carries over 1:1. Horizontal positions on a 16:9 screen are those of CT's 1.45 frame rescaled: for an angle a off-axis, x = 0.5 - tan(a) / 1.865. Constants: `CAMERA.fov` 75 -> **86**; `settings.ts` default `video.fovH` 75 -> **86**; add a load migration that maps a saved 75 to 86, the same way `adsLook` does.

## 4. Clipping and believability risks in our renders
- `shots/f/aim-ots` and `aimcrouchpistol-ots`: the head is cut by the left edge and the gun dominates the frame (row 2).
- `walk-ots`, `jog-ots` and `sprint-ots`: head-to-waist framing hides the legs, so the stride and stop work cannot be seen in play outdoors (row 1).
- `ours/traverse/*`: the proving camera sees Wren through a translucent wall in `ladder-climb-1.4` and `drainpipe-1.6`. In `duct-1.0` the duct is never entered (`kind: none`, KICK VENT prompt). Pass both to the traversal audit; they say nothing about the game camera.
- After rows 1 and 7, check that the 3.3 m boom still clears the yard's container rows: the sphere cast handles it, but the pull-in will happen more often.
