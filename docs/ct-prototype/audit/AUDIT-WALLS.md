## Clipping and believability risks in our renders
- `ours/traverse/wall-slide-0.6.png`: the slung rifle lies along the back while the back is held <= 3 cm off the face (`wallRootPush`): the rifle must be inside the wall. CT avoids it because the crouched pose is side-on (item 1) and the standing torso turns (item 3). Until then, push the rifle sling to the open side or add the rifle's depth to the standoff.
- `ours/walledgeaim-ots.png` 0.6-1.1 s: after aiming at a wall edge the camera ends with the wall's near face covering about 40 % of the frame and Wren dim behind it (camera at or inside the face plane). Check the sphere cast against the wall segment being attached to.
- `docs/ct-prototype/shots/f/walledgelook-back.png` 0.0-0.2 s: feet about 1 m apart (one leg straight out to the side) on a stance change; CT never exceeds about 0.6 m.
- `ours/traverse/wall-crouch-0.8.png`: flat-back kneel facing the camera, the "gorilla" read Michael flagged for the free crouch; item 1 replaces it.
- Door open with no step-in (item 4): from up to 1.6 m away and on the swing side the leaf (about 1 m wide) can sweep through the hero; the step-in spot removes this.
- Carry (item 7): hands IK to the knees with an upright spine reads as a weightless body; the 25 m/s drop impulse throws the body.
- Hostage (item 11): at `GRAB.hold` 0.32 m the two leg sets can cross; needs an anim sheet before shipping.
