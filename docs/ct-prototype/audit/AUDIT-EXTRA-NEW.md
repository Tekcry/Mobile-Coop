## Clipping and believability risks
- Goggle touch with a rifle slung: the hand path to the head must clear the back-slung gun's stock on the right shoulder (check carry slot yaw in `carrySlots.ts`); aimed or aiming state: block the touch.
- Roll from the crouch-run: the 2.3 m roll needs the same clear-space probe as the standing roll (`traversal.ts:476`); crawl exit must not push the head through the duct lip (row 9 path end 0.4 m past the opening).
- Top mount (row 6): turning 180 deg at the rim puts the heels over the drop; keep the feet 0.15 m inside the rim until k 0.33. Shaft arms-out pose (row 13) must stay inside a 1 m shaft: hand wall contact only on one side.
- Hang sway (row 8) must not swing the feet into the wall (limit toward the wall to 0, as TRAV #5).
- Our renders for these moves were not reviewed in this pass (no ladder-mount or crawl renders exist in `ours/traverse/`).
