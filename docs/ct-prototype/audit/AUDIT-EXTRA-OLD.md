## Clipping and believability risks
- Run jump at `LEAP.vy` 5.6 is a 0.87 m hop with the arms and legs of a gait: with the CT numbers (row 1) a tuck clip is mandatory or the long 0.95 s float shows straight legs.
- Landing from a leap at run pace into `fallProbe` grab range (0.75 m) can snatch a drainpipe in the air: check the CT 0.95 s flight clears a pipe 3 m ahead.
- Enemy auto-close (row 6) must not shut on the player in the doorway: `clearance` 0.75 m also applies to the player.
- Crouch spin hand IK (row 5) near a wall: clamp the floor hand to free floor (no hand inside geometry).
