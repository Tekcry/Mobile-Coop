## 2. Missing entirely
- Kneel straight from a crouched stop (item 3): exists only behind a 2.3 s delay; prototype: kneel on the stop, rise into the next crouch step (105624 @ 54.7-55.0 shows him rising straight into a crouch walk in ~0.3 s).
- Landing arms (item 18): new arm channels in LAND.
- Leg-locked turning mode for free movement (item 1): a planner mode `stride: 'facing'` that keeps feet in the root frame (CT style, feet allowed to scrub) instead of world-locked.

## 3. Clipping and believability risks in our renders
- `crouchrun-side.png`: rear knee on the floor every stride (knee drag / floor clip); hands near the floor.
- `turn-side.png` 0.3-0.5 s: feet splayed wide, knees bowed inward; reads as legs left behind.
- `stop-side.png`: deep lunge held ~1 s after a jog stop.
- `walk-side.png`: bent-knee walk at 1.35 m/s.
- `crouch-front.png` 0.2-0.4 s: wide squat before the kneel.

## 4. Not measurable from the footage
- World speeds in the new clips: the camera was orbited by hand and there is no fixed ruler near the feet; speeds stay from the old scroll measures.
- Walk cadence: the high behind view (105509 @ 2-4.4) does not show foot contacts reliably at 12.5 fps.
- Bank angle in degrees: top-down only (110117), foreshortened.
- Noise meter as a gear signature in the new clips: the HUD sample positions did not line up with the pips reliably (not used).
