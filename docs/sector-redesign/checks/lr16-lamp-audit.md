# LR16c lamp placement audit + LR16-2 framing audit

Generated by `npm run lamp-audit` (`scripts/lamp-audit.mjs`; logic `src/world/lampAudit.ts`, unit `tests/lampAudit.test.ts`).
Do not edit by hand: re-run. LR16d moves lamps strictly from the flagged-lamp table; a re-run must show 0 flags outside the decided rows (`DECIDED` in the script; LR16dx).

## Method

- Maps: `LISTED_MAP_IDS` + `warehouseOps`, `ctcourse*`, `bunker*`, `embassy`, `dustDepot`, `courselab` (LR16 plan). Missing (skipped): none.
- Each map is built headless (`LevelBuilder`, as the unit tests do); its canonical light shapes (what the lamp bake sees) are ray cast. Every fixed light of kind `lamp` or `spot` is audited, switched-off ones too (they can be switched on).
- Lamps: 16 horizontal rays + 8 rays tilted 45 deg down find the nearest wall face (|n.y| < 0.5, normal facing the lamp); **wall dist** is the perpendicular distance to that face. Flag when < 1 m (the photometric near radius) and the face is within the lamp's reach (a dead fitting, reach 0, is never flagged: no light of it reaches a wall).
- Spots: flag when the cone axis meets a wall within 0.6 m (**wall dist** is that axis distance).
- Wall fittings (bulkheads, `base: 'wall'`) skip their own mounting wall (face within 0.45 m behind them along their facing) and may only slide along it. **Signs** (a wall fitting in exit-sign green, or one reaching <= 1.2 m: exit signs, indicators) are never moved off their wall: when the glow reaches a face, `dim` = the reach (and authored radius) that stops 0.05 m short of it.
- **Move**, height kept: `push` = slide in plan along the face normal until the face is 1.2 m away (spots: the axis distance); else `centre` = toward the room's centre line across that wall (midway to the face opposite, 40 m scan), only as far as needed (the smallest 0.05 m step past the push that clears) and never past the line. A target keeps 1 m in plan from every other lamp, and 0.3 m between the fittings' bodies (one moved earlier in the table: at its target). A move is valid when the path is clear, the light stays as clear of solids as before (or 0.1 m), a ceiling-mounted lamp still has a ceiling within 0.8 m over it (**ceil gap** = new hanger gap), and the flag clears. `none` = neither works: the note says why and gives the centre point tried; re-site by hand.
- **Source**: the lamp's data entry (a `[x, z, ...]` table row or a `x: .., z: ..` line, preferring files on its build call stack); `(call)` = the coordinates are computed, the line is the call that adds it.
- Framing (LR16-2): standing spots every 6 m in plan on every floor with 1.9 m headroom (roofs and yards included; **sky** = open sky over the head), eye 1.6 m, 4 yaws (0 = +z, 90 = +x). Each view casts 16 x 8 rays (70 deg wide, -40 to +10 deg pitch, 25 m) and reads `LightField.levelAt` 5 cm off each hit (static light: ambient + moon + baked lamps, doors shut). Lit > 0.45, dark < 0.28, dim between. **No pool**: lit share < 5%; **no dark**: dark share < 5%. Views seeing under 24 points are left out.
- Levels are this branch's (before the LR16b soft edges and the LR16g re-level, which lifts pools): read a map-wide "no pool" as the level, a room-level one as placement. A map whose ambient sits over the dark line (`theme.lightLevel` > 0.28 with no darker zone) has "no dark" everywhere.

## Framing per room (LR16-2)

Views grouped by the HUD room at the spot (the map's `rooms`, on the spot's storey); views on open-sky spots outside every room (yards, roofs) are one row. Lit / dim / dark: the mean shares over the room's views. Verdict: **no pool** when every view in the room has no pool ("nothing to read"), **no dark** when every view has no dark ("nowhere to hide"), else the flagged views are listed as `(x, y, z) yaw` (feet; every view in `node scripts/lamp-audit.mjs --json`), at most 8 per room.
