## Design (one sentence)
A pendant's live offset (`swx, swy, swz`) is a pure function of (its kicks, sim time); the meter (`LightField`), the
shader (data-texture texel t0 + `spheres`) and the fittings read rest + offset; visibility stays the rest-pose bake
(audit `lr16-0-ct-audit.md:81`: error acceptable under 0.3 m); only a kick crosses the wire.

## Files
1. NEW `src/world/lampSway.ts` (pure, no Babylon): `SWAY` constants, `SWAY_PENDANTS` table, `claimHangers`, `SwaySet`.
   - `SWAY = { maxAmp: 0.25 /* m, cap < 0.3 */, tau: 5 /* s, amplitude e-fold */, kickShot: 0.35, kickBump: 0.25,
     kickBlast: 0.7 /* m/s at the centre, linear to 0 at */, blastReach: 6, graze: 0.6 /* m, a round's closest
     approach to the fitting centre */, near: 1.5 /* m, its impact point */, bumpReach: 0.35, minGap: 0.2 /* s per
     pendant, host */, minAmp: 0.005, slots: 4, still: 0.01, tol: 0.05 }` (each with its reason in a comment).
   - Physics: bifilar hang (two parallel cables/rods): the body translates, stays level, so the cone axis (t2) never
     tilts. `L` = mean claimed hanger length, `w = sqrt(9.81 / L)`, `wd = sqrt(w^2 - 1/tau^2)`. Kick i (t_i, A_i,
     heading h_i): `off = sum A_i e^-((t-t_i)/tau) sin(wd (t-t_i)) (cos h_i, sin h_i)`, `swy = (swx^2+swz^2)/(2L)`.
     A kick of speed v asks `A = v / w`; added at t0 it is capped `A = min(A, maxAmp - E(t0))`, `E` = sum of live
     envelopes, so `|off| <= maxAmp` always; `< minAmp` is dropped (no event). `slots` kicks per pendant in a
     Float64Array ring; full: replace the one with the smallest envelope at t0; an envelope `< 0.002` m retires.
   - Kicks (host only): `shotKick(ax..bz)`: closest approach d of the segment to the fitting centre `< graze` ->

     `blastKick(x,y,z)`: within `blastReach`, away from the blast. `bumpKick(pts, n)`: a head point within `bumpReach`

## Perf limits
No allocation in `tickSway`, `shotKick`, `blastKick`, `bumpKick`, `writeSway`, the rig update (allocPerCall = 0 B).
CPU: <= 9 pendants x 4 kicks x (exp + sin) a fixed step, `shotKick` <= 9 segment tests a round; `lampInto` +3 adds.
GPU: no new sampler or shader; at most one data-texture upload a frame (as today); 2 small meshes (<= 9 + 18 instances).

## Tests (`tests/lampSway.test.ts`, NEW)
- `pendulum: period 2 pi / wd, envelope e^-1 at tau` - zero-crossing spacing pi/wd within 2 %; |off| at t0 + tau
  envelope within 3 % of A/e; `swy` = (swx^2+swz^2)/2L.
- `never above maxAmp` - 30 kicks (shots + blasts at 0 m) in 2 s: max |off| <= 0.25 over 20 s at 1 ms.
- `determinism: host kicks -> events -> client registry` - offsets equal (`toBe`) at 200 times; tick step 1/60 vs
  1/30 gives the same value at shared times.
- `shotKick` - through the fitting: A = kickShot / w along heading; 0.7 m away and impact 2 m away: no kick; impact
  1 m away: away from the impact; second kick within minGap dropped. `blastKick`, `bumpKick` scale and cap.
- `versions` - `tickSway` moves `swayVersion`, never `version`; a still pendant writes nothing after settling.
- `light parity` - open-room LightField fixture (`tests/lightField.test.ts` pattern): level at P + off with the lamp
  swung equals level at P at rest (1e-9); `contribution` agrees with `lampInto`.
- `ctcourse table` - `ctcourse.build`: >= 4 pendants, each claims 2 hangers, claimed boxes `visible === false`,
  `canonicalLightSet` unchanged by `applyMapSway`; campusOps / warehouseOps / warehouse: none.
- `picker` - `LampSlotPicker`: a held moving lamp keeps its slot against a nearer still lamp past `minHold`;
  `refreshEvery` 1 while moving, back to 2 when still.
- `alloc` - the hot calls above 0 B per call. Protocol: `sway` event and `sw-` target parse, clamp, reject garbage.

## Acceptance
A shot within 0.6 m (or impact within 1.5 m), a grenade within 6 m or a head bump swings the pendant <= 0.25 m; it
settles in ~15 s; fitting, cables, pool, char shadow, meter and guards move together; host and client agree with no
per-frame wire; non-ctcourse maps byte-identical (no pendant); tsc, lint, tests green.
