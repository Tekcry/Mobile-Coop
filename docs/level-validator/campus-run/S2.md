## Campus-audit complaints (`origin/feature/campus-audit:docs/campus-audit/BRIEF.md`)

1. **Rooms randomly placed - not found as a pattern.** The shell is a 48 x 36 rectangle on the 0.5 grid; cores sit at opposite
   corners (SW x -42..-39.4, z -28..-22.4; NE x 0..2.6, z 0..5.6); the west block is four 14 m wide rooms (10 / 8 / 8 / 10 deep);
   rooms group by function off the atrium. Two minor misplacements are named: F10 (lift lobby) and F3 (L1 HUD boxes).
2. **Hallways too small - found: F1, F2, F6.** The ring strips (2.45), the CR2 landing (0.7) and the foyer lane (2.35). The wing
   (5.3 clear) and the C2 bridge (3.7) are inside the sheet.
3. **No space when doors open - not found.** All 31 door leaves inside the footprint sweep a 1.2 m quarter circle that overlaps no body-level
   collider and no other door's sweep (closest pairs: (-7.5,-21) and (-4.5,-23) 1.95 m, (-7.5,-21) and (-10,-22.5) 2.15 m). Every
   door on a corridor-like space swings away from it: the ring-strip doors (z -22.5) swing south into the rooms. The core doors and
   the facilities / board room doors open out into the 3.65-3.8 m strip, leaving about 2.45 m. The 32nd, C2's near door (4,-14.6), swings into the 3.7 m bridge
   along z -14.6, leaving 2.4 m (checked by hand: the bridge is outside the raster). The only tight spot is the 1.2 m landing (F5).
4. **Out-of-bounds reach - not found.** Outer walls are full height with no gap; the goods door (-44,-16) and the C1 far door are locked
   and unbreachable (:367, campusS1.ts:284). The 1.1 m roof parapet and the 0.9 m roof-light rim (:526-542) might be
   mantled by `pickTraversal` (height up to 1.8 with free air on top, `player/movement.ts:134-141`), but whether that leaves the building or
   drops into the void is engine behaviour, not provable from collision data alone: not listed, a candidate for the V6 or e2e run.
5. **Poor flow - found in part.** From the C1 door to the C2 door is 28.8 m straight; the SPEC routes walk 123 m (stealth), 99 m (loud) and
   174 m (high) including stairs. That is the loop round the atrium by design; the pinches are F1 and the two 1.2 m landing doorways (F7).
   The foyer reaches the hall through three gates on one camera line or by the post and mail rooms; L2 only by two fire stairs 45 m apart.
