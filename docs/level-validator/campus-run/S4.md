## 1. Modelling decisions (read before the findings)

- **Every space that holds a guard is typed `encounter`.** That is why small rooms (lobby, battery room, colonnade corridor,
  security room, NOC, north corridor) carry the rule of three and shadow / vantage targets, and fail them. Ten encounter
  spaces. If the lead prefers "encounter = large play space only", re-type those six and move their guards; the
  coverage ERR in section 6 then changes (1 of 4 instead of 1 of 10).
- **The hall is one space** (x -4..34, z 22..42). The five hot-aisle pods are internal obstacles: both ends of a pod open
  into the same hall, so as spaces they would be dead ends by construction. The 1.2 m hot aisles are in the hand findings.
- **The cable basket network is one space** (`s4-baskets`, B-N 29.5 m as the spine). Branches B1, B2, B3, B-cage and the
  landing are folded in.
- **Entry `s4-dock`, exit `s4-cc`.** SPEC route order: C3 far door (24,16) -> dock -> ... -> cage -> UPS room ->
  colonnade -> C4. The file holds only the outgoing spine link (C4, `s4-cc` -> `s5-lobby`, id guessed from `S5_ROOMS`
  `campusS5.ts:188`). The incoming C3 link (S3 exit -> `s4-dock`) belongs to the S3 file; the lead reconciles the ids.
- **Keypad doors, the armoured mantrap door and alarm-stage locks are plain `door` links** (no lock field). The 2.0 m yard
  gate (36.5..38.5, z 16) is locked and unbreachable (`campusS4.ts:466`), so it is not a link.
- No split is built in S4, so no `split:` / `overwatch:` / `assist` tags. CR4 and CR5 are single-route co-op moves.

## 5. Michael's campus complaints (docs/campus-audit/BRIEF.md, origin/feature/campus-audit)

- **Rooms randomly placed: not found as random; two defects.** The plan is function-ordered (service block west, dock
  south, hall centre, security and NOC north-west, riser north-east, compound east). Defects: the lobby / mantrap leftovers
  (finding 6) and the dock with no way into the hall except through the lobby / mantrap or round the compound (finding 5).
- **Hallways too small: found.** Findings 1 and 3 (service corridor 1.75 clear, colonnade corridor 2.2 clear, hot aisles
  1.2, cold aisles 2.0, a 0.8 m slot). The north corridor (6 m) and the hall are not small.
- **No space when doors open: found, visual only.** Finding 2 (leaf sweeps into the 1.75 m service corridor, the 2.2 m
  colonnade corridor, the pod and cage strips). Not a collision: open leaves have no body.
- **Out-of-bounds reach: not found.** `tests/campusS4.test.ts:632` floods the nav from the start (ladders as links, the
  C3 / C4 / yard-gate doors shut) and asserts it stays in the footprint plus the C3 and C4 boxes; my edge read agrees: deck
  screens 2.0 on the N, S, W edges (:477-479), the east deck parapet 1.1 drops only into the walled compound inside the
  footprint (:480-481), compound walls 2.8 (:463-465), the hatch is railed on three sides (:458-460), the basket
  penetration (:400, x 31.55..32.65, y 4.4..6.4) leads only to the landing. Not proven either way: whether a player can
  clear the 1.1 east parapet; it would land in the compound, not outside.
- **Poor flow: found, two points.** The dock funnel and alarm lock (finding 5), and the colonnade vestibule doors with
  the objective-locked C4 (finding 9). The hall itself has seven doors (three west, one south, one east, two north).
