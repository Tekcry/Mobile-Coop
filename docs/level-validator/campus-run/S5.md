## 5. What the schema could not express
- CR6 and CR7 are "one goes, one holds" moves, not two legs that rejoin: no `split:` tags, no `assist` target (it needs a split id), no overwatch tag. Modelled as `pair_gate` / `team_rope` links with the partner's action in `reason`.
- Guards in non-encounter spaces (hub, utility): the `guards` key is encounter-only, so 9 guards are in `note`.
- Open boundaries: parapets 1.1 m and balustrade gaps have no "open edge" kind; modelled as `drop` links (inferred) and open `door` gaps.
- Door leaf swing / clear width after opening, sight lines through glass or a camera as a vantage `from`, and a space spanning storeys (hall void and gallery, the core) have no field; one space per surface.
- Who owns the C4 spine link between two section files is not stated; none is in this file.
