- `PropPlacement` / `FacilityProps.add`: optional `flag` (the shared course flag the device follows; `CtDevices` reads it,
  the renderer never does) and `lampOff` (the lamp colour shown, lit, while the device is off).
- `PropRender.paint`: a `lamp` instance whose device is off and that has `lampOff` shows `lampOff` at `PROP_LAMP_GAIN`, still
  scaled by its circuit (`groupLit`). Every other case is unchanged (a plain device lamp still dims to `PROP_DARK`).
- `CtDevices`: the constructor collects `flagLamps` once from `layout.facilityProps` (device + flag, deduped by id,
  `on: -1`). `applyJam` (host `fixedUpdate`, client `apply` / `frame`) reads `shared(flag)` and calls
  `facilityProps.setDevice(id, on)` only on a change (the first pass always). Allocation-free; gameplay never reads it.
  `debug().flagLamps` = `[{ id, on }]`.
- No wire, `PROTOCOL_VERSION`, save, grant, card, top-up or `*.light.json` change. No laser / panel / camera behaviour change.
