## 1. Connection modes by platform at launch (2005)

| Platform | Same-screen split | LAN / System Link | Online co-op |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xbox | Yes (vertical split, Agent 1 left / Agent 2 right per PRIMA; a forum user says split orientation follows the dashboard widescreen setting - UNCERTAIN) | System Link (2 consoles for co-op) | Xbox Live |
| PC | Disputed - see note | LAN | Yes, via ubi.com / GameSpy-backed lobby |
| PS2 | Yes | No co-op over LAN (PS2 LAN/online exists for Versus only) | No |
| GameCube | Yes | No | No |

- Xbox: split-screen, System Link and Xbox Live co-op. [VERIFIED - PRIMA connection table + MN 2005 + Co-Optimus listing via search snippet + Wikipedia game article]
- System Link on Xbox: up to 4 consoles / 4 players for Versus, max 2 for co-op, 1 player per console. [VERIFIED - Team XLink wiki + Wikipedia "List of Xbox System Link games"]
- PS2 and GameCube: co-op is split-screen only. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]. A Feb 2005 preview (GamesFirst) already said PS2 would lose online play but keep split-screen co-op, and that GameCube would ship without the Versus mode. [SINGLE SOURCE - GamesFirst; preview, so pre-release] Taken together: [VERIFIED - PRIMA + GamesFirst] for "PS2 co-op had no online".
- PS2 Versus: FW says the PS2 multiplayer is basically a Pandora Tomorrow-style versus with a few new CT maps. Team XLink lists the PS2 game as LAN-capable (that is Versus), and a community still plays PS2 Versus online via XLink Kai (YouTube 2026, Facebook "splintercellonlineps2" plug in a Steam thread). No evidence of PS2 co-op over network. [SINGLE SOURCE each - treat PS2 network co-op as absent]
- PC split-screen - CONFLICT: PRIMA's table lists PC as "Split-screen, LAN, Online" and says split-screen is supported by all platforms. Steam users (2015 thread, co-op.gg listing) say the PC version has LAN/online only, no split-screen; community uses Nucleus Co-op or DxWnd hacks to fake split-screen. Steam store system requirements mention "joystick for Solo and Co-op modes". [UNCERTAIN - leaning "no native PC split-screen" (two independent player sources vs one guide that may have generalised)]

## 2. Co-op menu modes by platform
- Xbox/PC: Story (missions in order, each unlocks next), Mission (replay missions in any order), Elite (Mission mode where firearms start with no ammo and the knife is usable; available after Story completed). [VERIFIED - XBM + PRIMA; Elite details in systems_rules.md]; Elite unlocked by clearing co-op Story [VERIFIED - PRIMA + Cheat Index]
- PS2/GC: missions in fixed order, each split into 4-5 areas, progress saved at the end of each area, any unlocked area replayable (functionally like Mission mode). [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA] FW sub-level pages agree missions are split into 4-5 parts [VERIFIED for the split - PRIMA + FW].

## 3. Mission lists and structure

### Xbox/PC (7 missions incl. DLC)
1. Training Facility (Fort Meade) 2. Panama (PCA building) 3. Seoul ("Seoul 3") 4. Chemical Bunker (Nampo) 5. Train Station (Grand Central NYC) 6. Nuclear Plant (Yongbyon) 7. UN Headquarters (NYC). [VERIFIED - FW + Wikipedia "seven-mission story mode" + Elder-Geek "seven missions"]
- FW (English) says Nuclear Plant and UN HQ swap order in the PC version (UN HQ 6th, Nuclear Plant last on PC). The Russian Fandom wiki calls UN HQ the 7th mission "on Xbox and PC". On PC both are only in Mission mode (patch notes), so "order" may just be list order. [UNCERTAIN - conflict]
- Pre-release (Feb 2005) material spoke of only 4 co-op levels. [SINGLE SOURCE - GamesFirst preview] The retail disc had 5 (Training + 4), DLC added 2. [VERIFIED - FW + IGN wiki ("five co-op missions, two more as free DLC")]

### PS2/GameCube (different build)
Team Training -> Seoul (Streets, Rooftops, Gardens, Fish Market, Cyber-Cafe) -> Chemical Bunker (Silo, Laboratories, Cryogenic Chamber, Storage Room, The Docks) -> Train Station (Train Repair Station, Hydro Tunnel, Subway, The Station) -> Panama (The Alley, East Wing, Archives, Server Room). [SINGLE SOURCE - FW] (PRIMA has separate "PS2/GCN" co-op walkthroughs with Panama sub-areas e.g. "Get into the archives in the administration building", consistent [VERIFIED for Panama being split])
- Order differs: on Xbox/PC Panama is the first real mission; on PS2/GC it is the last; PS2/GC has no Nuclear Plant / UN HQ. PRIMA's Xbox walkthrough calls Chemical Bunker the "third COOP mission" and its PS2 walkthrough the "second", matching FW's orders. PS2 manual's end screen speaks of results in "all four missions". [VERIFIED - FW + PRIMA + PS2M]
- Layouts are reworked/smaller on PS2/GC (general statement for SC1-3 PS2/GC builds by a Steam user; FW says 3DS remake is based on the PS2/GC build). [UNCERTAIN for co-op specifics]
- PS2/GC loadouts per FW: both agents carry SC-20K (60), SC Pistol (40), 1 flash grenade, 2 adrenaline syringes, sticky camera in some areas. [SINGLE SOURCE - FW]
- PS2/GC sub-levels fail on 3 alarms; Seoul: Gardens has a 4-minute timer. [SINGLE SOURCE - FW]

## 4. Co-op DLC (Nuclear Plant, UN Headquarters)
- Announced 12 May 2005 by Ubisoft together with Versus map "Steel Squat": free, for PC and Xbox (Xbox Live); Squat end of May, co-op maps "shortly thereafter". [VERIFIED - Shacknews 12 May 2005 + Gaming Nexus 12 May 2005 (press release)]
- 13-14 July 2005: Steel Squat released; co-op maps "in the coming weeks"; Ubisoft blurb: Nuclear Plant = claustrophobic passages + open spaces, loud machinery, puzzles; UN HQ = "no room for kills or alarms". Polar Base versus map promised for end of summer. [VERIFIED - WorthPlaying 13 Jul 2005 + VideoGamer 14 Jul 2005]
- Xbox Live release: Major Nelson post 25 Aug 2005 - two free downloads continuing and ending the co-op story; playable in split-screen, System Link and Xbox Live; only in Mission and Elite mode; you must play through Coop Story on Elite to see the maps first, after which they are selectable in Coop Mission mode. Post edited to say there may be content issues. [SINGLE SOURCE - MN (official)]
- GameSpot follow-up ("New content available for Chaos Theory") reported the Xbox downloads as unusable at first (maps not shown on level select) and Ubisoft investigating. [SINGLE SOURCE - via search snippet only; GameSpot 403 to fetch] Whether/how it was fixed: GAP.
- PC delivery: patch v1.05. US patch 1.00->1.05 dated 19 Oct 2005 (77.2 MB); notes: two new co-op levels UN Headquarters and Nuclear Plant, selectable in "Create Game" screen in "Mission" mode; not accessible in Story mode; no "Use native languages" sound option on these maps. [VERIFIED - gamepressure patch page + ModDB patch listing snippet] EU had a 1.04->1.05 patch "adds two new co-op missions". [SINGLE SOURCE - search snippet of ModDB/softpedia]
- PC "Elite unlock" requirement for the DLC: not stated in PC patch notes (they just say Mission mode). [GAP]
- Original Xbox Live shut down 15 April 2010, so DLC can no longer be bought; preservation installers (Harcroft/xbox-scene) exist for modded Xbox (Nuclear Plant 2090 blocks, UN HQ 1990 blocks; includes title update). [VERIFIED for date - xbox-scene + Wikipedia BC list citing Marc Whitten letter]
- Xbox One/Series backward compatibility (10 June 2019): both co-op mission packs (described by Microsoft as "split-screen co-op missions") plus Polar Base and Steel Squat map packs released free, delivered automatically via content updates. [VERIFIED - Xbox Wire 10 Jun 2019 + GamesHedge + Wikipedia BC list "Includes all DLC"]

## 5. Server shutdowns and online status
- Original Xbox Live: ended 15 April 2010. [VERIFIED - xbox-scene + Wikipedia (Whitten letter)]
- PC: GameSpy-backed services died 2014 (GameSpy shutdown, May 2014 general fact; Steam users cite "2014"); Ubisoft then (re)hosted / ended CT online in 2016 - sources disagree on month: April 2016 (PCGamingWiki + Steam guide, via search snippets) vs 7 Oct 2016 (MobyGames, via search snippet). Several Steam users: "servers shut down 2016, LAN only now". [UNCERTAIN on exact date; VERIFIED that official PC online co-op is dead]
- Steam store and Microsoft store text today: "online features for this game are no longer supported. Multiplayer and co-op modes are no longer functional." [VERIFIED - Steam store page + Microsoft display catalog (fetched 2026-10-17)]
- N-Gage Arena (not used for CT multiplayer anyway) closed 2010. [SINGLE SOURCE]

## 6. Current community ways to play co-op (2026)
- Xbox (original hardware): System Link tunnelling via XLink Kai is supported (Team XLink: co-op yes, max 2). Title Updates must match: Xbox 360 digital copy lacks the updates, Xbox One/Series digital includes them, so 360 cannot link with One/Series; One and Series can link; on original Xbox updates can be added/removed to match. [VERIFIED - Team XLink wiki + Wikipedia System Link list (same caveat)]
- Xbox (Insignia revival service): CT main title (US-056, 55530038) and the separate Versus executable (US-065, 55530041) are listed with matchmaking; leaderboards exist for both; Insignia page shows no details. Whether co-op matchmaking specifically works was not confirmed. [SINGLE SOURCE - insignia.live listing; co-op function UNCERTAIN]
- Xbox One/Series X|S: plays the original-Xbox version via BC with DLC; local split-screen co-op works; System Link between One/Series works; no online. [VERIFIED for BC + DLC; local/system-link by inference from Team XLink notes]
- Xbox 360: on 360 BC list and sold digitally as an Xbox Originals title (announced by Major Nelson 15 Apr 2008 per Wikipedia citation; FW infobox "21 April 2008 (XBLA)"). [VERIFIED - Wikipedia BC list + FW]
- PC (Steam app 13570 / Ubisoft Connect): online lobby is gone; co-op works via LAN menu (Coop -> LAN -> Create Game) over virtual LAN tools - Radmin VPN (most recommended), ZeroTier One (needs manual IP, interface metric 1), Hamachi (gateway/metric hack, unreliable), Tunngle (closed 30 Apr 2018), Evolve (closed). Both players need their own copy. [VERIFIED - multiple Steam threads 2015-2024 (independent posters, one site - counted with Elder-Geek/co-op.gg as partial second sources)]
- Known PC co-op problem: frequent desync/disconnects (e.g. during the training hacking minigame, ~1 min into Panama). Multiple users traced it to mismatched configs, especially the ThirteenAG widescreen fix/forced FOV installed on only one PC; fix = identical configs/same FOV or remove the widescreen mod on both. [VERIFIED - two separate Steam threads, different posters]
- A 2024 Steam user claims co-op works on both Steam and Ubisoft Connect versions and that Nucleus Co-op split-screen supports only the Steam version. [SINGLE SOURCE]
- PC Versus has a community "Enhanced SCCT Versus" patch (GitHub Joshhhuaaa) with custom LAN master server/direct IP; it is for Versus, not co-op. [SINGLE SOURCE - search snippet + Steam user]
- PC patching: base game -> 1.05 recommended; Ubisoft support (2023) advises running the 1.05 patch as admin and applying it directly from base game (patches.ubi.com). [SINGLE SOURCE - Ubisoft rep in Steam thread]
- GameRanger support for CT: no source found. [GAP]
- PS2: Versus playable online via XLink Kai (community); co-op is split-screen only so remote co-op not possible. [UNCERTAIN - inferred]

## 7. Other versions
- PS3 Classic Trilogy HD (2011, port of the PC version): no co-op and no versus; adds manual saves, 720p, stereo 3D, trophies. [VERIFIED - FW HD page + IGN wiki note ("PS3 HD Remastered disc does not have Co-op")]
- Splinter Cell 3D (3DS, 2011, remake based on PS2/GC build): no multiplayer modes; a reviewer (GamingXP) called missing multiplayer its biggest drawback. [SINGLE SOURCE - FW citing GamingXP]
- Mobile (Java ME, Gameloft, 2005): 2D single-player flip-screen game, 8 missions; no co-op mentioned. [SINGLE SOURCE - FW]
- N-Gage (Mar/Apr 2005) and Nintendo DS (Jun/Jul 2005), Gameloft: own co-op campaign of 5 parts: Rooftops, Breakdown, Corruption (one ARGUS Corporation HQ mission in New York, 3 parts) and Prison Rescue, Escape (Cuba prison rescue of Colonel Geoff Trason, 2 parts). Players are "Shadownet Hacker" (blue goggles; hacks computers, unlimited sticky shocker, chaff/flashbangs; cannot short-circuit electric panels) and "Shadownet Assault" (SC-20K, Five-seveN, frags, sticky cams, night/thermal vision; cannot hack). [SINGLE SOURCE - FW mission pages]; "five original co-op missions" on DS [SINGLE SOURCE - GamesRadar DS review]
- N-Gage co-op is local Bluetooth, two players; N-Gage Arena not used for co-op or versus (Arena only for top-10 scores/replays); Versus up to 4. [SINGLE SOURCE - Gaming Target N-Gage review]
- DS co-op over local wireless; GameSpot review says multiple cartridges needed. [SINGLE SOURCE each - via search snippets]
- N-Gage/DS co-op uses classic 3-alarm abort and checkpoint saves. [VERIFIED - FW + GamesRadar DS review (checkpoints and "three strikes" alarms)]


## 8. Platform-specific extras and quirks
- PS2 menus: "Solo/Co-op" (offline; co-op = Training, Campaign, Options, Hall of Fame) vs "Multiplayer Online" (Play on Ubi.com, Play LAN, Tutorials) - online is Versus only and needs broadband + network adaptor + memory card. [SINGLE SOURCE - PS2M]
- GameCube: no Versus mode at all; GBA link cable feature (commands / sticky-camera feed on GBA, solo). [VERIFIED for no Versus - GameRevolution GC review + GamesFirst preview]
- GameCube co-op reportedly needs both game discs to reach every mission; missions are short and cannot be saved mid-way. [SINGLE SOURCE - Classic Co-op 2017; disc count UNCERTAIN]
- Xbox 360 (BC disc or Xbox Originals digital): no online co-op (original Xbox Live gone); digital 360 copy lacks title updates, so it cannot System Link with One/Series copies. [VERIFIED - IGN wiki note + Team XLink]
- Xbox One/Series: 4x-16x resolution boost through BC emulation; all DLC included. [VERIFIED - Xbox Wire 2019 + Wikipedia BC list]
- Original Xbox title update shipped with Steel Squat (July 2005) fixed light replication between host and clients and other Versus bugs. [VERIFIED - WorthPlaying + VideoGamer]
- PC 1.05: DLC maps lack the "Use native languages" sound option. [SINGLE SOURCE - patch notes]
- FW-RU Seoul page marks an objective wording change "[Version 1.05]" (destroy Jong's boat engine), implying 1.05 also touched base co-op text on PC. [SINGLE SOURCE - FW-RU]

## 9. Cheats / unlocks for co-op missions
- Xbox: entering the profile name COOPA22COOL unlocks all (co-op) levels. [SINGLE SOURCE - multiplayer.it (search-engine summaries of cheatcc/supercheats echo it, but those pages were 403 to me)]
- PS2: at the Solo/Co-op menu (after picking an offline profile) hold L1+L2+R1+R2 and press Square x5, then Circle x5 -> all solo and co-op missions unlocked; sites mark it unverified/"try the other order". [VERIFIED - ChapterCheats + multiplayer.it + IGN wiki ("Unverified")]
- GameCube: at the Solo/Co-op menu hold L+R and press X x5, Y x5 -> all levels. [SINGLE SOURCE - multiplayer.it]
- Elite unlock (Xbox): finish co-op Story. [VERIFIED - XBM + cheat sites]
- PC: no co-op unlock cheat found; profile .ini can bind console cheats (invisible, invincible, ammo, fly, playersonly). [SINGLE SOURCE - IGN wiki PC cheats]

## 10. Widescreen / patch issues affecting co-op (PC)
- Community widescreen fix (ThirteenAG "widescreen fix", forced FOV) installed on only one machine causes co-op desyncs/disconnects; both players must run identical configs/FOV, or remove the fix. Reported independently in two Steam threads (2014 thread updated later; 2019 "[Solved]" thread). [VERIFIED - two Steam threads]
- Patch 1.05 installer can fail ("could not retrieve installation folder"); Ubisoft advises admin rights and patching from base game. [SINGLE SOURCE - Steam/Ubisoft rep 2023]
- Steam "Single-player" feature tag only; store says MP/co-op no longer functional, but LAN co-op still works via VPN. [VERIFIED - Steam store + several Steam threads]
- Whether the Steam/Ubisoft Connect build ships pre-patched to 1.05 (and thus includes Nuclear Plant/UN HQ): not confirmed. [GAP]
