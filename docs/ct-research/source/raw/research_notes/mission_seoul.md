Builds covered:
- **Xbox/PC "Seoul 3"** - co-op story mission 3 of 7 (Training Facility > Panama > Seoul 3 > Chemical Bunker > Train Station > Nuclear Plant > UN HQ). PC map file name `02_seoulthree`.
- **PS2/GameCube "Seoul"** - co-op mission 1 after Team Training; split into five sub-levels: Streets, Rooftops, Gardens, Fish Market, Cyber-Cafe.

## Source-counting conventions used in this file
- **Fandom** = English Splinter Cell wiki + Russian Splinter Cell wiki. They are sister Fandom wikis with mirrored page structure (the ru pages carry extra notes and loadout tables), so they count as ONE source family. A claim backed only by en+ru Fandom is tagged [SINGLE SOURCE].

The English original is no longer reachable; I read two machine-translated mirrors on eyewated.com (Cebuano and Lithuanian) and cross-checked numbers/codes between them. Paraphrased below; treat fine wording as approximate because of double translation.

# PART A - Xbox/PC "Seoul 3"

## A1. Setting & premise
- Date in-fiction: 8 July 2007; location Seoul, South Korea, during the North Korean invasion (same day as Sam Fisher's single-player "Seoul" mission). [VERIFIED - Fandom en/ru infobox + Wikipedia/Fandom game page list; the war context also matches Elder-Geek review "parallel missions"]
- Premise: the two trainee Splinter Cells (Agent One / Agent Two) must abduct Jong Pom-chu, a South Korean chemist suspected of working for North Korean intelligence and of manufacturing chem/bio weapons, before NK forces extract him. Lambert's briefing ties this to the Panama mission (Jong allegedly bribed de Medeiros to move illegal cargo through the Canal Zone) and opens by calling the job easy. [VERIFIED - Fandom en/ru briefing + Rybka opening section restates the same goal: take Jong alive from a Seoul hotel before the NK Army secures him]
- Narrative twist: Jong is not in the hotel - the man there is a body double, who reveals NK Special Forces have relocated the real Jong. [VERIFIED - Fandom (Jong Pom-Chu, Agent Two pages; ru notes list "Jong's double" as interrogable) + Rybka step 2 ("fake Jong" in a bathroom) + Prima (man "fitting Jong's description" in the bathroom)]
- Prima's briefing frames this as the agents' "second mission" (Panama first) and stresses Jong knows NK's chem/bio secrets so his death fails the mission. [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom]
- **Sam Fisher crossover (user asked to verify):** In Fisher's single-player Seoul mission, Lambert tells Sam that "Splinter Cells in training" lost their target to NK Special Forces, and Sam must interrogate one of two NK Special Forces soldiers near a truck; over a radio patch a trainee asks where Jong was taken, Sam jokingly threatens the soldier, who says Jong was moved to a cyber-cafe off the fish market by the river; the trainee thanks him. Killing both soldiers first fails Sam's mission. [VERIFIED - Fandom "Seoul/Transcript" + Fandom "Seoul" trivia + GameGrin "Chaos Theory Diaries Part Seven" (SP playthrough describing the same interrogation for the trainees)]

[VERIFIED that the exchange is heard in co-op - Elder-Geek + Rybka; timing after 2nd AA = SINGLE SOURCE (Rybka)]

2. **Find out where Jong is** - added after the hotel reveal (Jong was moved).

5. **Get to extraction with Jong** - carry him to the truck outside the cyber cafe.

Opportunity [VERIFIED - Fandom en/ru + Rybka mention both; NOTE Prima's Xbox/PC objective box lists only the officer interrogation]:
- **Interrogate the North Korean officer** to learn where Jong was taken. The officer stands with two guards in the back alley just outside the hotel's keypad door.
