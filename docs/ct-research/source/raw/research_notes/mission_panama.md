# A. XBOX / PC - "Panama" (Panama Canal Administration building)

## 1. Setting & premise
- Location: the Panama Canal Administration (PCA) building, Panama City; the VP's administration building. [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA]
- Date: 28 June 2007, the same day as Sam Fisher's solo "Bank" mission in Panama. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (mission page, timeline and solo mission list)]
- Briefing (paraphrase): records recovered by Fisher at the MCAS Bank of Panama show that Vice President Segundo Ruiz de Medeiros has been taking bribes to let certain ships skip spot inspections in the Canal Zone. Lambert welcomes the trainees to "the big leagues": they are not finished training, but no other assets are free. Their job is to find whatever records de Medeiros kept. [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA]
- PRIMA adds that they must decide whether de Medeiros acts alone or on someone else's orders. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Story tie-ins to the solo campaign: in the solo Cargo Ship mission Lacerda rants about having to bribe "this Vice President" de Medeiros, and the solo objective there is to find Lacerda's Panamanian contact. Fandom bios say interrogating de Medeiros and the records reveal dealings beyond Lacerda, including with North Korean scientist Jong Pom-chu. That leads into the next co-op mission (Seoul, capture Jong). [VERIFIED - FAN (Cargo Ship transcript) + PRIMA (solo objective list "Find the name of Lacerda's Panamanian contact") for the Lacerda link; the Jong link is SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- EG notes that co-op missions include dialogue with Sam Fisher in parallel missions. Whether that happens in Panama is not confirmed. [UNCERTAIN]

## 2. Objective chain
Primary objectives (all three appear in the briefing list): [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA]
1. Access the archives. Fandom's text places them in the East Wing, top floor. PRIMA's route reaches them on the building's third floor.
2. Access the main server. Revealed after the archive: the archive data has been partially deleted, so the team must get the server backups, which lie past the exposition room on the ground floor. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN for the "partially deleted" update text; PRIMA confirms the server lies past the Exposition Room]
3. Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point: the truck in the exterior courtyard, which is also the insertion point. [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA]
- No secondary or opportunity objectives are listed for the Xbox build, either by FAN or in PRIMA's header. [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA (absence)]
- PRIMA perfect-score rule (applies to all co-op): no kills or injuries (knock-outs only), never identified or alarmed, all objectives done. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Mission notes the game adds as you find intel (FAN "Notes" list): [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
  - Disable the laser using the jammer. MAN-PS2 shows "Jammer" is just the co-op name for the OCP, so this means the pistol OCP. [VERIFIED - MAN-PS2 + PRIMA (uses OCP)]
  - 1523: hall outside the billiards room (from a computer email in which two guards plan to sneak into the lounge's big-screen TV to watch a match).
  - 1534: door from the server room out to the exposition area (computer).
  - 2341: door into the exposition area (computer, a reply in the same email chain).
  - 3245: door between the VP's office and his gallery (interrogation).
  - 3298: the VP's private elevator (interrogation).
  - The RU localisation's interrogation also gives 9728 for "the archive room computer", which is used nowhere. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (RU)]
- Alarm escalation: alarm 2 - guards wear body armour; alarm 3 - helmets; alarm 4 - fortified positions. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Killing the VP fails the mission. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (RU)]

## 4. Spatial flow (Xbox/PC)
- Outdoor courtyard (insertion and extraction at the trucks) → building entered via a 2nd-floor window (vertical entry). [VERIFIED - PRIMA + FAN (truck in exterior courtyard)]
- At least 3 vertical layers inside: 1st/ground floor (Exposition Room, server room, reception hall); 2nd floor (Photocopy Room, Hallway A, Broken Elevator corridor); 3rd/top floor (security office, VP's office, retinal room, Archive Room). An upper crawl/duct level sits above the 3rd-floor rooms (ceiling opening → motion-door halls → air duct over the Exposition Room). The Exposition Room is double-height, with balconies and a ledge you can shimmy along. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Loop structure: outbound route up and in (window → 2F → elevator → 3F archive → over the roof and duct → balcony → elevator down → Exposition → server); return route (Exposition → reception hall stairs up → back to the Photocopy Room → window). So the level is a large loop: the exfil re-uses the entry window but reaches it by a different staircase. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Chokepoints: the Broken Elevator camera corridor; the dual retinal door (hard gate to the archive); the Exposition gates (hard gate, opened only from the Hangover keypad); the Exposition laser grid (crossed twice). [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA; Exposition gate also JV]
- Branches: the billiards/lounge hall (1523), gallery (3245), private elevator (3298), Security rooms, locker room, the extra laser-room guard room. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN] How they connect is not documented. [GAP]

## 5. Security (Xbox/PC)
- Guards: about 22 across the level (FAN RU count). Notable behaviour: the security-office guard comes out to fix a light switched off in the hall; the archive guard tries to turn the lights back on; server-hall guards investigate shot-out lights; reception hall guards watch the stairs; two server-room guards chat (3 random dialogue variants). [SINGLE SOURCE each - PRIMA / FAN]
- Several areas have guard conversations: the alley, billiards/lounge, locker room, VP lounge, server room. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (RU)]
- Cameras: one near the Broken Elevator and one in the server room (beaten by shooting the light near the computer). [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Lasers: the Exposition Room floor grid, whose emergency breaker sits inside the grid itself (in-level email). Beaten with the OCP/"jammer". [VERIFIED - PRIMA + FAN]
- Locks: keypads hacked at Hallway A, the Broken Elevator corridor and the next room; the dual retinal scanner (forced with captives); the Exposition gates (keypad hacked from the Hangover). Codes 1523/1534/2341/3245/3298 as above. Remote hacking (EEV) is disabled in co-op, so all hacking is done in person. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA for the EEV rule]
- A motion-sensor automatic door in the upper halls. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Lighting: switches you can use as lures (hall, archive), lampposts, chandeliers, lights inside the elevator car. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Civilians: Vice President de Medeiros (must not be killed; counted as a civilian knocked out). [VERIFIED - FAN + PRIMA (he is grabbed and choked)]
- Alarm escalation: armour, then helmets, then fortified positions. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Noise: headset voice is audible to guards within about 2 m at the default setting. [VERIFIED - MAN-X + PRIMA]

## 6. Split & regroup points (Xbox/PC)
1. Broken Elevator camera: one covers (OCP on the light), one moves, then swap. Soft split; you regroup at the next keypad. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
2. 3rd floor: the biggest split. Agents go to opposite ends of the hall (one to the security office guard, one to the VP's office) and regroup at the retinal scanner. The two-scanner door forces both a regroup and synchronisation: each agent must bring his own live captive. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
3. Archive: one searches, one guards (knocks out the light-fixing guard). [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
4. Air-duct Hangover: one holds, one hacks while hanging. [VERIFIED - PRIMA + JV]
5. Exposition grid: one suppresses the breaker, one crosses, then swap. This happens twice. The OCP drains while held and recharges, so the coverer is pinned in place. [VERIFIED - PRIMA + MAN-PS2 (jammer drains, stops if user moves)]

## 7. Pacing curve (Xbox/PC)
- Opening: an outdoor stealth approach in the dark plus the first Boost + Human Ladder (a teaching echo of Training). [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Low-tension stretch: keypads and light switches through the 2nd floor.
- First peak: the 3rd-floor double capture. Two agents, two captives, one synchronised door, with the VP as a living key. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Reveal: the archive is partially deleted, so the objective extends down to the server. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Traversal set piece: across the ceiling, through the motion-door halls, the duct Hangover, then the balcony ledge shimmy above the Exposition Room. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Second peak: the Exposition laser grid (OCP relay) and the server room camera. [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]
- Exfil: the grid a second time, then the guarded reception-hall stairs and the courtyard. Tension stays up to the end because there are no checkpoints. [SINGLE SOURCE - EG (no checkpoints; MAN-X mentions only a session Quick Save)]
- Length: 100%-stealth runs are about 16-35 minutes; speedruns about 8 minutes (an Expert/Elite run in 8:05); casual playthroughs 41-52 minutes. Speedrun.com lists a Panama (Co-op) record of about 3:50. [VERIFIED - multiple video lengths; the speedrun.com figure is from a search snippet only]

## 9. Frustrations, exploits, bugs (Xbox/PC)
- Bug: if you are shocked while hacking a console or computer, you become invisible but can no longer open doors yourself. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (EN and RU)]
- Common stuck points (JV): the ground-floor grille (people do not realise the opening keypad is reached by Hangover from the vent above); the balcony after the Hangover (people miss the ledge shimmy and jump to their death); the elevator; finding the server room after the archive. [SINGLE SOURCE - JV (many posters)]
- No checkpoints: a death or failure restarts the whole mission, which frustrates mismatched partners. [SINGLE SOURCE - EG]
- Glitch: guards occasionally "trip" over each other and alert others even when the agents are hidden. [SINGLE SOURCE - EG, general co-op]
- Revive limit: two adrenaline shots, about 30 s to revive or the mission fails. [VERIFIED - MAN-X + PRIMA]
- Online no longer works (Ubisoft servers closed); PC players use LAN/VPN tools. [SINGLE SOURCE - Steam discussions (search snippets)]
