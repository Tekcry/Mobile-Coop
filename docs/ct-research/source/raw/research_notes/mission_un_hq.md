## 1. Setting & premise
- United Nations Headquarters, Turtle Bay, Manhattan; 31 October 2007 (night). Two buildings: a tower (security control room on the 22nd floor) and a second building containing the General Assembly hall. [location VERIFIED - Fandom; hexen2k7 briefing; Ubisoft PR. Date SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom. Building layout SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom mission notes]
- Briefing (paraphrased): minutes ago Third Echelon intercepted an encrypted communication between Jin Soo and the UN Headquarters. Something is wrong; find out what. The building is among the best-protected in the world and is international territory: no lethal force, and Lambert will abort the moment he hears a general alarm. [VERIFIED - Fandom; hexen2k7 description (Feb 2009)]
- The in-game briefing screen also carries a disclaimer that the United Nations has not approved or endorsed the use of its name or logos. [SINGLE SOURCE - hexen2k7 transcription]
- Ubisoft's pitch: infiltrate one of the world's most protected buildings with "no room for kills or alarms"; the only option is to stay invisible. [Ubisoft PR - one origin, reprinted by Hexus, MyGamer, VideoGamer]
- Story function: finale of the co-op arc (Xbox order). Kim uses the remaining detonator (Nuclear Plant data: 4 delivered, 3 used in NY) for a bomb timed to go off during that night's UN peacekeeping assembly, where the Secretary(-General) is to speak on the Korean peninsula's peaceful future. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom data pages; link between missions is my deduction]
- GameSpot (search snippet): once the agents reach the security-control-room floor they find Kim and his men have taken it over and must hurry to defuse a bomb. [SINGLE SOURCE]

## 2. Objective chain
Primary (in order): [VERIFIED - Fandom; hexen2k7 objective list]
1. Infiltrate the building. The service stairs lead to the top floor, but the emergency doors open only from inside - find another way in.
2. Recover the encrypted data sent by Jin Soo.
3. Reach the security control room. Security cameras are off; use the elevator.
4. Stop Colonel Kim. He has taken control of the security control room.
5. Defuse the bomb before it blows. It is in the General Assembly hall's ventilation system; defuse before the countdown ends.
Secondary: none. Opportunity: none. [VERIFIED - Fandom has none; hexen2k7 shows both sections empty]
Mission notes (in-game): no lethal force on UN guards and civilians; general alarm aborts; emergency doors only open from inside; security control room is on the 22nd floor; ventilation system is on top of the General Assembly hall; the hall is in the second building; the ground floor is reachable by a service elevator. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom EN/RU]
How objectives update (inferred from wording): each objective's text reveals the next location - the intercepted data leads to the security room; Kim's takeover is found on arrival (twist); the bomb objective opens only after Kim, and its text names the new destination (GA ventilation, second building, ground floor via service elevator). [UNCERTAIN - inference; GameSpot snippet supports the "discover Kim on arrival" twist]

Data sticks (what the agents can read): [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom "United Nations Headquarters/Data"]
- Computers 1, 2, 4, 5, 6: empty.
- Computer 3: a news-wire item - the UN Secretary speaks tonight at the UN peacekeeping annual assembly about the Korean peninsula's peaceful future (dated 31/10/2005 in the data, inconsistent with the 2007 mission date).
- Computer 7: access - "Open Security Control Room Entrance".
- Computer 8: email from Jin Soo to Kim Pak-Tu - he has sent a precise plan of where to place the bomb; make sure it explodes during tonight's assembly; plus a "Bombing Plan" image.
- Computers 9-10: access - "Disable Metal Detector".

## 3. Ordered progression (assembled; no step-by-step text source)
1. Insertion from outside at height: lock locations include the ROOFTOP (6-pin) and the service stairs go to the top floor, whose emergency doors cannot be opened from outside, so the agents must find another entry. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom notes + lock list]
2. Top floor: lockpick on the stairs near the elevator. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
3. Middle floor: technical room lock; somewhere here the encrypted Jin Soo data (computer 8) and the security-room entrance terminal (computer 7). Security room keypad code 250282 (marked "secret"); the trivia says the code can be learned by luring guards to the keypad and watching them type it via sticky camera + thermal/night vision, or by knocking out a guard and pushing him to another guard (which lowers the stealth rating). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom EN/RU; code also on Fandom keypad-codes page (same source)]
4. Elevator to the 22nd-floor security control room. Kim and his men hold it; "Stop Colonel Kim". [VERIFIED - Fandom + GameSpot snippet]
   - Kim cannot be grabbed from behind or interrogated. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom EN (Kim page) + RU notes, same source]
   - Lethal force IS allowed against the rogue North Koreans (the restriction covers UN guards and civilians). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom trivia + RU note]
   - Kim's fate conflicts: Fandom's Kim page says he is "stunned"; Fandom's New York City page says the agents killed him. hexen2k7's 100% no-kill run lists exactly one knock-out marked "(objective)", consistent with Kim being knocked out non-lethally. [UNCERTAIN - conflicting; non-lethal takedown evidently counts as completing the objective]
5. Down to the second building: lower-floor stairs near the elevator (lock); service elevator to the ground floor; a turret on the lower floors (ammo is "to the left of the elevator near the turret"); a metal detector (a toilet near it has a lock; computers 9-10 disable the detector); in front of the central hall, a left ventilation room and a right technical room (both locked). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom EN/RU]
6. Ventilation above the General Assembly hall: defuse the bomb. The countdown is NOT shown on screen but runs 20 minutes. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU note]. Whether defusal uses the two-man simultaneous-cut mechanic from Train Station is not documented. [Gap]

## 4. Spatial flow
- Vertical, top-down infiltration of a tower: roof -> top floor -> middle floor (technical room) -> 22nd-floor security control room (by elevator) -> lower floors -> service elevator to ground floor -> transfer to the second building -> General Assembly hall and the ventilation space above it. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom notes/locks; order partly inferred - UNCERTAIN]
- Elevators are the spine: the regular elevator to the security room, the service elevator to the ground floor; lockable stairwells sit beside the elevator on top and lower floors (likely alternatives). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Gated checkpoints: emergency doors (inside-only), keypad security room, metal detector, locked side rooms before the central hall. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Exterior exposure: being spotted in lit windows causes mission failure (RU note; whether this means spotted from outside through windows, or in lit window areas, is unclear). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU; meaning UNCERTAIN]

## 5. Security
- UN guards (non-lethal only) and civilians; rogue North Koreans under Kim (lethal allowed). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Fail states: general alarm = abort; metal-detector alarm = failure; detection in lit windows = failure; killing UN guards/civilians forbidden. [abort-on-alarm VERIFIED - briefing in Fandom + hexen2k7 + Ubisoft PR; metal detector and windows SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU]
- Security cameras are "off" per the security-room objective text, i.e. a later state after Kim's takeover or after the agents' hack - unclear. [UNCERTAIN]
- A turret on the lower floors. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU]
- Seven 6-pin locks (rooftop; stairs near elevator top floor; technical room middle floor; stairs near elevator lower floor; toilet near metal detector; left ventilation room and right technical room in front of the central hall) and one keypad (250282). All locks in this mission are 6-pin (harder than Train Station's 5-pin). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]

## 9. Frustrations, exploits, bugs, design flaws
- Instant-fail conditions stack up (general alarm, metal detector, lit windows); one Let's Play episode is subtitled "AKA Suicide Central". [fail rules SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU; LP title weak signal]
- The bomb countdown is invisible (20 minutes) - a hidden timer is a design risk. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom RU]
- Keypad-code exploit: learn 250282 by observing guards; or carry a knocked-out guard to another guard, at a stealth-rating cost. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Kim cannot be grabbed/interrogated, unlike most named targets. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Launch problems on Xbox Live (maps not showing). [VERIFIED]
- Data inconsistency: the news-wire and Jin Soo emails are dated 2005 while the mission is set in 2007. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom data]
- Modern PC co-op (general, not mission-specific): mismatched or edited INI/FOV settings (e.g. widescreen/FOV mods) cause disconnects; players now connect via LAN over a VPN (Radmin) since official servers are gone. [VERIFIED - Steam guide 'Native Xinput Support/DS4 Support & Coop Tips' (id 1762258050); Steam discussion 1651043958651257044 (widescreen mod removal fixed disconnects); co-optimus listing via search snippet notes online servers shut down]
