## 1. Taxonomy (how the game itself classifies co-op actions)

- The manuals split co-op actions into two families [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA]:
  - **"Generic" / free co-op moves** - can be done almost anywhere, triggered by the dedicated Co-op Action button: Short Scale/Boost, Tomoe Nage/Long Jump, Human Ladder.
  - **"Static" (PS2M wording) / "Contextual" (XBM, PRIMA wording) co-op moves** - only at designer-placed spots, triggered from the HUD Interaction menu with the normal Interact button: Dual Rappelling, Hang Over Teammate ("Hangover"), Stand on Teammate's Shoulders.
- Plus non-move team interactions, all via the Interaction menu: Share View (sticky camera feed), Heal/Revive teammate, Share Equipment, and **synchronized actions** (dual retinal scanner, simultaneous bomb wire cut, other "puzzle actions") [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Free moves fail gracefully: if there is not enough room, the agent shakes his head and the controller vibrates [SINGLE SOURCE - XBM p.23].
- Count: a 2005 preview said about 10 new co-op-only moves; the manuals document 6 named moves (3 generic + 3 contextual) plus Share View, Heal, Share Equipment, synchronized actions and the crane [VERIFIED for the 6 - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA; '10' SINGLE SOURCE - GF].
- Universal handshake: one player **initiates** (takes a pose and waits); the second player walks up and **completes** with the same button/menu entry; only then does the animation play. When one agent initiates, an automatic voice line tells the partner he is waiting, and another plays on completion [VERIFIED - XBM pp.24-26, PS2M, PRIMA]. A HUD "Co-op Action" icon appears for the partner when the other is ready [VERIFIED - XBM p.23 item 10, PS2M item 10].

## 2. Generic (anywhere) moves

### 2.1 Short Scale / Boost
- Names: manuals title it "Short Scale/Boost" (XBM, PS2M); PRIMA calls it "Boost"; Training Facility objective calls it "Short Scale" (FW). Russian localisation: "подброс" (toss/boost) [VERIFIED for English names - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA, FW; Russian name SINGLE SOURCE - RUW].
- Inputs: initiator crouches then presses Co-op Action (Xbox Black; PS2 L2; GC Z + D-pad Left; PC default F or Page Down). Completer approaches and presses Co-op Action [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA, FW controls page].
- Outcome is situational: either boosts the partner up to grab a ledge or pipe, or vaults him over an obstacle (e.g. a wall) [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M]. PRIMA: boosted agent automatically grabs the ledge/pipe above [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA].
- Animation (from XBM icon): crouching agent cups hands / offers knee, partner steps in and is lifted [UNCERTAIN - inferred from manual pictogram only].
- Typical chain: Boost -> Human Ladder (see 2.3) so both agents reach the ledge [VERIFIED - PRIMA basics + PRIMA walkthroughs].
- Training: "Jump over the wall using the Short Scale" - one agent goes over, opens a door from the other side for the second [VERIFIED - FW Training Facility, RUW].

### 2.2 Tomoe Nage / Long Jump
- Names: "Tomoe Nage/Long Jump" (XBM, PS2M), "Tomoe Nage" (PRIMA, FW); Russian: "длинный прыжок" (long jump) [VERIFIED].
- Inputs: initiator stands (not crouched - PRIMA), faces AWAY from the intended landing direction, presses Co-op Action; a target reticule appears that he steers; red reticule = throw impossible. Completer approaches and presses Co-op Action and is thrown [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Purpose: cross wide gaps and low obstacles such as laser grids [VERIFIED - PRIMA, FW Training Facility objective "throw your teammate over the laser"].
- Offensive use: an NPC near the landing point is hit and knocked out [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA, FW Tomoe nage page]. PRIMA's Train Station route deliberately throws an agent onto a guard lured by a shot-out light; IGNW suggests throwing agents at Panama courtyard guards and setting a Tomoe Nage behind a door a guard will open [VERIFIED - PRIMA + IGNW].
- Animation: judo sacrifice throw - thrower falls backwards and launches partner with his feet (FW describes the real judo move; XBM pictogram shows a flying body) [SINGLE SOURCE - FW for animation detail].

### 2.3 Human Ladder
- Inputs: the agent already hanging from a ledge (or pipe - PS2M) presses Co-op Action; the other stands beneath him and presses Co-op Action, then climbs up his body to the ledge [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Usually follows a Boost so that the second agent also gets up [VERIFIED - PRIMA].
- Mentioned in lore text as part of the agents' training ("human ladder, back to back climbing") [SINGLE SOURCE - FW Agent Two page].

## 3. Contextual / static moves (only at marked spots, via Interaction menu)

### 3.1 Dual Rappelling ("Co-op Dual Rappelling")
- Initiator selects "Initiate Rappelling" in HUD interact menu at a rope anchor; completer selects "Complete Rappelling" and goes down on the partner's rope [SINGLE SOURCE for exact menu labels - PRIMA].
- Rope-holder shifts the rappeller side to side with the triggers (Xbox L/R triggers; PS2 L1/R1; PRIMA "Fire and Secondary Fire") ; rappeller moves up/down with left stick and can use weapons [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Can also be used to climb back UP a partner's extended rope [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]. Training objective wording: "help your teammate to reach the upper part" [SINGLE SOURCE - FW Training Facility; RUW calls it paired descent/ascent].

### 3.2 Hang Over Teammate ("Hangover")
- Names: "Hang Over Teammate" (XBM, PS2M), "Hangover" (PRIMA, Training Facility objective in FW). Russian: roughly "hang on rope with partner's help" [VERIFIED].
- One agent lowers his partner, suspended upside down by a rope at the ankles, usually through a ceiling trapdoor/hole [SINGLE SOURCE for "ankles/trapdoor" - PRIMA].
- Rope controller raises/lowers with triggers [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Suspended agent can: hack nearby computers/keypads; use secondary weapon (pistol, incl. OCP/jammer); hide with an "inverse crouch" (Xbox left trigger / PS2 L1 / PRIMA secondary-fire trigger hold); perform an inverted neck snap on an unaware guard below via "Grab Character" [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Training: "Hack the computer to deactivate the laser using the Hangover move" [VERIFIED - FW, RUW].

### 3.3 Stand on Teammate's Shoulders
- Initiator selects "Initiate Climb Shoulder"; completer selects "Complete Climb On" and climbs up; top agent can interact with elevated objects and use weapons [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M; menu labels SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA].

## 4. Team interactions (not "moves")

### 4.1 Share View
- When one agent uses a camera gadget (sticky camera), partner can pick "Accept View Sharing" in Interaction menu to watch the feed [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Not available in split-screen, because you can just look at the other half of the screen [SINGLE SOURCE - XBM p.25].

### 4.2 Heal / Revive teammate
- See coop_gadgets_controls_comms.md Part D for full details.

### 4.3 Share Equipment
- Equip the gadget, approach partner, choose "Share Equipment"; only items marked with a dot in the inventory can be given [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA]. PS2M adds the share request can be cancelled before the partner answers [SINGLE SOURCE - PS2M].
- Note: the Fandom Training Facility page labels a "Share Equipment" text as cut content (deleted data). The manuals of BOTH Xbox and PS2 and the Prima guide describe it as a shipped feature, so the "cut" label probably refers only to a deleted training-panel text, not to the feature [UNCERTAIN - needs in-game confirmation].

### 4.4 Synchronized actions
- Both agents must act at the same moment: defusing bombs (simultaneous wire cut), dual retinal scanners, opening doors that need two guards forced to scanners [VERIFIED - PRIMA, XBM; bomb wire cut also FW Train Station].
- Without headset (online), synchronisation is automatic: first player selects the action, second selects it, and the game syncs them; PRIMA says puzzles become easier because timing is not required [VERIFIED - XBM p.27 "Puzzle Action", PRIMA].
- Training: dual retinal scan - each agent hacks one scanner; failure is "dangerous" (RUW: failing costs health; can be hacked solo but failure hurts) [SINGLE SOURCE - FW/RUW].

### 4.5 Crane gameplay (newly found)
- XBM describes "Crane Gameplay": one agent operates a crane while the other gives directions via a "Crane Communication" interaction (stick directions send "Back/Forward/Left/Right/Lower/Raise/Stop" voice messages) [SINGLE SOURCE - XBM p.27]. Used in the Xbox/PC Chemical Bunker Blue Room to set a missile on a holder, followed by a simultaneous wire cut [VERIFIED - XBM + PRIMA].

### 5.3 Frequency summary (PRIMA routes, both builds)
- Boost (+/- Human Ladder) is by far the most common, used in every non-training mission (roughly 3-5 per mission) [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA].
- OCP/jammer alternation ("one jams, the other moves, then swap") appears in every mission [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA; concept VERIFIED by XBM, PS2M, FW OCP page].
- Tomoe Nage: 0-2 per mission; Hangover: about 1 per mission (Panama, Chem Bunker, Train Station on Xbox; Chem Bunker, Panama on PS2/GC); Dual Rappelling: Chem Bunker (both builds), Train Station (PS2/GC, 3x); Stand on Shoulders: Seoul AA vehicles (both builds) and PS2/GC Panama elevator.
- Synchronized actions in every main mission: dual retinal scanners (Panama, Chem Bunker), simultaneous wire cuts (Chem Bunker missile, Train Station bombs), simultaneous switches (PS2/GC only), simultaneous code lock (PS2/GC Chem Bunker).

## 7. Versus (Spies vs Mercs) overlap
- Xbox/PC Versus spies have "Team Moves": Boost (only where a Boost icon shows, lower-left of screen) and Human Ladder after it; plus "team abilities": nursing (heal each other), sharing gadgets, and removing a merc's Spy Finder tag from a teammate [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA versus chapter]. WP also says versus added "cooperative moves for the spy team" [VERIFIED - PRIMA + WP].
- Versus spy controls on Xbox: White button = start/finish a co-op move; A = heal partner, give/accept gadget (contextual) [SINGLE SOURCE - XBM p.31].
- Prima says these Team Moves are only in the Xbox/PC versions [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA]; consistent with FW Multiplayer page saying PS2 versus is basically a Pandora Tomorrow clone, and the PS2 manual's spy-abilities list contains no co-op moves [VERIFIED - PRIMA + FW + PS2M].
- No evidence that Tomoe Nage, Hangover, Dual Rappelling or Shoulders exist in Versus [not found].

## 8. Signposting / teaching (how the game tells players what to do)
- Training mission is a move-by-move tutorial with objectives named after each move and wall panels that explain moves/equipment (reading them is itself a primary objective; skipping lowers the rating per RUW) [VERIFIED - FW, RUW, IGNW].
- Striped squares on the floor mark where training moves are performed [SINGLE SOURCE - IGNW].
- Generic moves: initiator's pose + automatic voice line + partner's HUD "Co-op Action" icon tell the partner to come and complete [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA]. In Versus a Boost icon appears lower-left only where a Boost is possible [SINGLE SOURCE - PRIMA].
- Contextual moves appear as named entries in the HUD Interaction menu only at valid spots [VERIFIED - XBM, PS2M, PRIMA].
- Mission notes in the OPSAT spell out co-op requirements, e.g. Seoul: climb on your partner's shoulders to disable the tanks; a turret can be disabled with the jammer; Panama: disable the laser using the jammer; Train Station: both agents must cut the active wire at the same time [VERIFIED - FW + RUW mission pages (same wiki family, but RUW Seoul shoulder note is RUW-only: SINGLE SOURCE)].
- Failed free moves give feedback: agent shakes head + controller rumble [SINGLE SOURCE - XBM].
