## A. XBOX / PC - "Training Facility"

### 1. Setting & premise
- Location: Third Echelon Training Facility, Fort Meade, Maryland, USA. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN, which cites the in-game location caption]
- Date: 23 June 2007. Fandom says this comes from the PS3 version's game files, not from on-screen text. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN] [UNCERTAIN as to whether it is ever shown in-game]
- Story position: first co-op mission; next is Panama. Wikipedia lists the co-op as a 7-mission story mode with two "Splinter Cells in training", Agent One and Agent Two. [VERIFIED - FAN + Wikipedia]
- Briefing (paraphrase): as trainees the two agents must learn to rely on each other; the only things between them and failure are their own wits and their partner; the course teaches them to pull off tasks a lone agent could not. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN, EN and RU texts match]
- Facility look (Fandom description): mostly above-ground; an outdoor course plus a central training structure built from cheap cinderblock, plywood and plasterboard with barbed wire on wall tops, like a military/police field-skills course. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN] Fandom also uses a screenshot captioned as the facility interior.
- Agents are told apart by goggle-light colour: Agent One blue, Agent Two red. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]

### 2. Objective chain
All objectives are primary and are revealed in sequence as the course progresses (each station has its own objective line). Listed in Fandom's order: [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN for wording and order]
1. Read the wall-mounted panels. Panels explain each move and gadget; you need them to understand how to clear each area.
2. Get over a wall with the Short Scale (boost). One agent goes over; the door is then opened from the far side for the partner.
3. Use the jammer so a camera does not detect you (synchronise: one jams while the other moves).
4. Do the Tomoe Nage: throw your partner over a laser; the thrown agent then turns the laser off with a switch.
5. Use the "Hangover" (hang the partner upside down) to hack a computer and turn off a laser before entering a small room.
6. Hack the dual retinal scanner: each agent hacks one scanner to open the door. The objective text warns that failing is dangerous.
7. Dual Rappelling: help your partner reach an upper area. (Note: EN wording says "reach the upper part"; the RU text uses both "descent" and "ascent" - direction is unclear.)
8. Reach the end of the course.

How objectives update: one per station, as each zone is reached. [UNCERTAIN - inferred from the per-station wording; not described explicitly by any source]

Scoring quirk: reading the panels is tracked as an objective. V339 says 100% needs you to re-read at least one panel. RU Fandom says skipping the read-the-panels objective lowers your rating. [VERIFIED - V339 + FAN]
- Killing does not fail the mission but lowers the rating. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN (RU)]
- No secondary or opportunity objectives are recorded for this build. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN] (V339 calls the panel re-read an "optional objective", which suggests the panel line counts as optional for scoring. [UNCERTAIN])

Corroboration of individual stations:
- Short Scale wall at the very start: several JV posters were stuck at the first training wall. One answer says boosting someone vertically means the booster must stand, not crouch. [SINGLE SOURCE - JV] PRIMA and both manuals agree: the Boost initiator crouches, and the Tomoe Nage initiator must stand (not crouch). The JV answer is best read as 'standing gives a Tomoe Nage, crouching gives a Boost'. [VERIFIED - PRIMA + MAN-X + MAN-PS2]
- Hangover before the computer: a JV poster, giving a hint for another level, points to the training move done just before hacking the computer, where one spy ends up upside down. [VERIFIED - FAN + JV]
- Dual retinal scanner: a JV poster asked whether the training scanner needs hacking because two faces must be scanned at once. [VERIFIED - FAN + JV]
- Jammer vs camera: the 'jammer' is the co-op name for the pistol's OCP. MAN-PS2 says 'OCP, or Jammer'; MAN-X lists the Jammer as the co-op-only gadget; PRIMA explains that the co-op OCP must be held down, drains while held and refills afterwards (tapping saves charge). [VERIFIED - MAN-PS2 + MAN-X + PRIMA for the gadget; the specific training camera is FAN only]

### 4. Spatial flow
- Mostly above-ground; outdoor course plus a central cinderblock/plywood structure. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- The flow is linear, one lesson per station. The Short Scale wall is a split point: one agent goes over and the door re-merges them. The Tomoe Nage laser is a split: the thrown agent's switch re-merges them. The Hangover hack works on two levels (holder above, hacker hanging below). The retinal door is a forced synchronised regroup. The Dual Rappel works on two levels. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN objective text; layout inferred from the mechanics, UNCERTAIN]
- No loops, shortcuts or branches are documented. [GAP]

### 5. Security
- 1 camera, handled with the jammer. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Lasers: at least 2 (one switched off by the thrown agent, one by the Hangover hack), plus a "laser room". [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- 1 dual retinal scanner door. [VERIFIED - FAN + JV]
- Guards: none documented. Stats imply possibly 1 knock-out-able person. [UNCERTAIN]
- Civilians, lighting, noise: not documented. [GAP]

### 6. Split & regroup points
- Short Scale wall: the boosted agent is alone until he opens the door. The door is locked from the far side, which forces the boost. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Camera: an asymmetric role, one jams and one moves. Moving breaks the jam, so the jammer has to stay put. [VERIFIED - FAN + MAN]
- Tomoe Nage laser: the thrown agent must hit the switch for the partner. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
- Hangover: one holder, one hacker. [VERIFIED - FAN + JV + MAN (move mechanics)]
- Retinal door: both must be at their scanners. [VERIFIED - FAN + JV + MAN]
- Rappel: rope holder plus rope user. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN; mechanics MAN]

### 7. Pacing curve
- Tutorial cadence: read a panel, perform a move, move on. The only hint of danger is the retinal-scan warning that failure hurts (lost health). [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN] No set pieces or reveals are documented.
- Short level: speedrun-style videos run about 3-4 minutes (V339 4:03 at Expert/Elite/ghost; hexen2k7 3:14). Casual playthroughs run 9-16 minutes. [VERIFIED - multiple video lengths]

### 8. Wow moments
- No player commentary specific to Training was found. General co-op praise in reviews focuses on acrobatic moves "like Chinese acrobats" (NWR, GameCube), throwing your partner, and the camera-jam run, all of which Training introduces. [SINGLE SOURCE per claim - NWR]

- The 100% rating needs a pointless panel re-read. [VERIFIED - V339 + FAN]
- One JV poster says that after finishing a later Xbox co-op mission the game reloaded the Training mission from the start instead of continuing. This is a possible progression bug and is not Training-specific. [SINGLE SOURCE - JV] [UNCERTAIN]
- Cut content: Fandom lists a deleted data item (an email dated 06/23/07 from "Dan" to "Seb") describing a "Share Equipment" move. Yet the Xbox manual documents Sharing Equipment as a live feature. So the move may exist in the game while its training email was cut. [UNCERTAIN]

## C. Cross-build co-op mechanics relevant to Training (from the Xbox manual)
[SINGLE SOURCE - MAN unless noted; these are official rules]
- Two classes of move. Co-op moves can be done anywhere with room (if there is not enough space the agent shakes his head and the pad vibrates). Contextual co-op moves happen only at marked spots, via the interaction menu (one agent starts, the other completes).
- Short Scale/Boost: the initiator crouches and presses Co-op Action; the partner approaches and presses Co-op Action. Depending on the spot it boosts the partner to a ledge or pipe, or vaults an obstacle.
- Tomoe Nage/Long Jump: the initiator faces away from the throw direction and picks a target (a red target means impossible); the partner completes it. An NPC at the landing spot gets knocked out. [VERIFIED for the knock-out effect - MAN + NWR "tossing your buddy ... into enemies"]
- Human Ladder: an agent hanging on a ledge initiates; the partner stands below and completes it, climbing his body. [VERIFIED - MAN + NWR + JV (Seoul sewer description)]
- Dual Rappelling (contextual): the rope controller moves with the triggers; the agent on the rope can use weapons.
- Hang Over Teammate (contextual): the holder moves the partner up and down with the triggers; the hanging agent can use a secondary weapon, do an inverted neck snap, or take an inverted crouch for cover.
- Stand on Teammate's Shoulders: the rider can use weapons.
- Share View: view the partner's camera gadget feed (not available in split-screen).
- Heal Teammate: revive a downed partner. Usable only twice per game, and you have about 30 seconds to reach him or the mission fails.
- Sharing Equipment: hand over gadgets marked with a dot in the inventory.
- Jammer: co-op-only gadget; partially disables electronics while held on target; a red/green indicator shows valid targets; it stops if the user moves.
- Voice: the Xbox headset voice can attract or distract guards and can give you away if loud (default radius about 2 m). Without a headset, automatic voice prompts replace it ("Up/Down/Stop" for the Hangover, "Left/Right/Stop" for rappel).
- Synchronised actions (dual retinal scanner, puzzle actions) sync automatically once both press Action.
- HUD shows an alarm counter; each alarm has global repercussions.
- Quick Save exists during a co-op session (wiped at session end). Story-mode saves must be identical on both consoles. EG says co-op has no checkpoints, which fits with only a manual session quick save. [VERIFIED for "no checkpoints" - EG + MAN]
- Elite mode: no firearm ammo, knife usable; unlocked by finishing Story mode.
