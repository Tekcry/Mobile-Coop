# PART A - Xbox/PC "Chemical Bunker"

## A1. Setting & premise
- Location: Nampo (Namp'o-si) missile bunker complex, North Korea (Fandom infobox: "Nampo Missile Bunker, South Pyongyan"). Date 12 October 2007. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom en/ru; ru gives "chemical warfare bunker, outskirts of Namp'o-si"]
- Briefing (paraphrase): Jong Pom-chu, interrogated by special agent Jack Baxter, admitted passing classified bio-weapon research to North Korean intelligence (CGIB), who then blackmailed him into buying controlled viral-engineering equipment and smuggling it through Panama. His communications point to a bio-weapons programme in old bunkers near Namp'o. Mission: get into the complex, identify chemical/biological weapons, and bring back samples of any agent under development. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom en/ru briefing; Baxter's off-screen role also on Fandom John Baxter page]
- Prima's briefing (paraphrase): Jong's interrogation raised suspicions about the NK chemical-weapons bunker outside Namp'o-si; the trainees must infiltrate the secret underground bunker to learn the North's plans and chem/bio capabilities. Prima calls it the "third COOP mission" on Xbox/PC and the "second" on PS2/GCN. [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom briefing]
- Story links: in-game emails are addressed to "Kim" (Colonel Kim Park-chu), described elsewhere as the former commanding officer of this bunker who later steals bio-warheads and seizes Grand Central Station (next mission). [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom (Data page emails + Kim Park-Chu + Train Station briefing)]
- Tone/character: a lab chimpanzee named **Java** - a technician's email claims she can count (screeches once per banana held) and that her blood is in the fridge (code 1423). [VERIFIED for Java's existence/name - Fandom Data email + YouTube descriptions by MintyJonesGaming ("fall in love with Java the monkey") and Magic Man ("java the monkey")]

## A2. Objective chain (Xbox/PC)
Primary, in game order [VERIFIED - Fandom en/ru + Prima objective box (identical six primaries, same order)]:
1. **Infiltrate the bunker down one of the silos** - the only way in is down a launch tube.
2. **Recover the viral protocol from the main laboratory** - needed to manufacture an anti-virus.
3. **Acquire the appropriate chimpanzee blood serum** - from a test chimp.
4. **Retrieve a sample of the anti-virus from the cryogenic chamber** - manufactured from the protocol.
5. **Retrieve a viral sample from one of the missiles** - from a warhead in the storage facility.
6. **Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point** - the Zodiac at the end of the missile-boat dock.

Opportunity [VERIFIED - Fandom + Prima (both list exactly this one)]:
- **Recover the videotape of the monkey autopsy** - the object on the table in the video surveillance room. ru Fandom adds a soldier at the computer in the camera-control room can be interrogated, and the tape is in the same room.

Keys, codes and how they are found:
- Mini-lab door code **5791** - from interrogation. [VERIFIED - Fandom + Steam "SCCT Co-op door codes" thread lists 5791, 1423, 8416, 1961 for Chemical Bunker]
- Refrigerated locker in the mini lab **1423** - from a computer email (the "Talking Monkey?" email). [VERIFIED - Fandom + Steam code list]
- Main-lab security port **8416** - from interrogation. [VERIFIED - Fandom + Steam code list]
- Missile warhead casing **1961** - from a computer email ("Warhead Access", Bae to Kim). [VERIFIED - Fandom + Steam code list]
- Computer 3 on Xbox/PC offers a security-system option to **disable the camera system**. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom Data]
- Computer 5: email "Anti-viral protocol" (Pok to Kim) - the protocol needs monkey blood serum plus two chemical compounds, ingredients attached as NOTES; follow-up email "Mixing the protocol" - ingredients must be mixed **simultaneously in protocol order, and two people are required**, and it is dangerous. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom Data; danger corroborated by Neoseeker gas/death posts -> VERIFIED that mixing errors hurt]
- Computer 8: "Disarming the warheads" - after opening the casing, the safety must be disabled by **cutting two hot wires simultaneously**, strongly recommended with two people; plus "Crane is back!" email saying a repaired crane will be used for final loading of armed missiles once its operator returns. [emails SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom Data; the crane is player-operated per Prima (see A3 step 21)]
- Blood type and mixing sequence are **randomised**: protocol component one is serum type A, B or O (chosen at mission start); each agent's colour sequence (user A: green/red/violet; user B: blue/yellow/aqua) has 3 inputs on Normal, 4 on Hard, 5 on Expert. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom en/ru notes; the randomness is echoed by a Neoseeker poster who thought the colours "may change every game" -> VERIFIED that the sequence is not fixed]
- Alarm escalation: alarm stage 2 enemies wear body armour, stage 3 helmets, stage 4 fortified positions. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Opportunity for "confirm the intention to launch a chemical weapon" exists as an objective only on PS2/GC; on Xbox/PC the technicians' conversation can still be overheard. Scientist in the monkey lab can be interrogated and is heard talking to the monkey. [SINGLE SOURCE - ru Fandom]

## A3. Ordered progression (Xbox/PC)
Co-op moves marked REQUIRED / OPTIONAL. Order follows Prima's numbered steps.

**Silo (surface -> bunker)**
1. Scale the fence ahead of insertion, climb down a ladder to the two missile silos, knock out the two patrolling guards. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
2. At the far silo, **Dual Rappelling**: one agent lowers the other down the shaft; the rappeller uses a control panel at the bottom, then climbs back up his partner's rope. CO-OP REQUIRED. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker poster ("lower your friend... press the switch... pull him back up")]
3. At the other silo, the edge control panel now raises a platform; one agent jumps on, the other operates the panel to lower it and jumps on quickly; ride down to the doorway = "Infiltrate the bunker down one of the silos". [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker]
   - Shortcut/exploit claims: ru Fandom (drop onto partner, then revive/heal, skipping the elevator); NickyTheGamer mentions "a BIG shortcut" early. [UNCERTAIN]

**Laboratories (lower level)**
4. Shoot hallway lights, knock out the hallway guard, take a crawlspace; from its end **overhear two technicians** in the lower **Blue Room**, then shoot out all the Blue Room's lights now (you return there later). [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; ru Fandom also notes the technicians' talk can be overheard]
5. Second crawlspace -> tall ladder -> ventilation duct; one agent drops silently through a hole into the **Monkey Room** and knocks out the technician from behind. [Monkey Room VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom (scientist in the monkey lab) + Steam "monkey behind a window" memory]
6. Hallway outside: shoot the light, knock out the guard, shoot the light above him to lure a second guard from beyond the **laser beams**, knock him out. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
7. **Laser corridor:** hand-over-hand along an overhead pipe past the first laser set; at the pipe's end, **Tomoe Nage** throws one agent past the second laser set; he deactivates the lasers at a computer around the corner. CO-OP REQUIRED (throw + switch-off for partner). [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; the brief's "Tomoe Nage over laser" move matches]
8. **Operation Room** (upper ledge): one agent shoots the x-ray light board to black out the room while the other drops to knock out a technician and a guard; the ledge agent drops through a trapdoor into the small **Camera Room** and takes the **videotape** from the table = opportunity "Recover the videotape of the monkey autopsy". [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom (object on the table in the video surveillance room)]
9. North door toward the **Main Lab**: knock out the hallway guard; one agent hacks the keypad of the **Main Lab Control Room** while the other waits at the Main Lab door; inside, knock out a guard sleeping in a chair and press a red wall switch to open the Main Lab for the partner. CO-OP: split, one opens the way for the other. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; Fandom gives the main-lab "security port" code 8416 from interrogation as an alternative to hacking]
10. In the Main Lab, sneak up and knock out both technicians; use the computer in the small office = "Recover the viral protocol". [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom objective; Steam/Fandom code 8416]
11. Stairs up to the bunker's first level; navigate to the **Mini Lab** (adjacent to the Monkey Room); hack its keypad (or 5791), then the refrigerator keypad (or 1423); check Notes for which serum is needed and take it = "Acquire the appropriate chimpanzee blood serum". [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom (random A/B/O type, codes) + Steam code list]
   - Vial count conflict: Neoseeker says take all three; Steam 2014 says one per player; ru Fandom says one type per agent and wrong picks block the chamber. Prima says only "consult notes and collect the needed sample". [UNCERTAIN]

**Cryogenic Chamber**
12. Back through the Operation Room, west door; knock out the hallway guard; use OCPs to pass a **surveillance camera near an elevator**; enter the **Cryotank**, one agent at **Station A**, the other at **Station B**. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker (chambers labelled A/B above the doors)]
13. Review the protocol in Notes and mix: Prima says it must be done **quickly** and the agents **take turns** adding each ingredient. Wrong inputs release gas from the ceiling that badly hurts/kills (Neoseeker, several threads); Fandom's email warns two people are needed and it is dangerous; sequence length scales with difficulty (Fandom). CO-OP REQUIRED by design. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker + Fandom + Steam + jeuxvideo.com]
   - ru Fandom exploit: one agent can run between both stations and do the whole sequence. [SINGLE SOURCE]
14. A guard arrives by the nearby elevator - knock him out (mind the camera); take the elevator up to the first level. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker ("a guard will exit from the lift next to the camera... take the lift up")]
15. Hide behind crates left of the elevator; fire a **Sticky Camera** near the next guard and gas him. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
16. **Storage room** (crates): one agent climbs the crates and shoots out the far-wall light; both sneak up and grab the two guards by the far door, then **simultaneously force them to use twin retinal scanners**. CO-OP REQUIRED (synchronised action). [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; Prima's general co-op chapter cites twin retinal scanners as a standard synchronised puzzle]
17. Knock out the hallway guard; door at the end leads to the **upper level of the Cryogenic Chamber**; shoot the corner light so the guard across the room cannot see; **Hangover** at the top of the Cryotank lowers one agent to collect the anti-virus = "Retrieve a sample of the anti-virus". CO-OP REQUIRED. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker ("bring your partner back up") + PS2 Fandom wording ("access the chamber from above")]

**Missile storage (Blue Room)**
18. **Tomoe Nage** one agent over the Cryotank; he knocks out a guard and opens a **one-way door** for his partner; take the elevator down toward the Blue Room. CO-OP REQUIRED. [VERIFIED - Prima + Neoseeker (two threads: throw partner over the tank with the aiming reticle, partner opens a door, small lift to the storage bay)]
19. Shoot the hall light when the doors open; enter the **Blue Room (missile storage area)**, which should still be dark from step 4; knock out all guards and technicians. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
20. East door -> **Mechanic Room** computer: emails explain how to operate the **crane**, give warhead code **1961**, and (third email) how to disable the safety. [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom Data (Computer 8: warhead access 1961, disarming via two hot wires, "Crane is back!")]
21. **Crane:** one agent operates it from the north **Control Room** while the other stands by the missile holder across the room and talks him into placing a missile on the holder. CO-OP: spotter/operator - communication-driven. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; the crane email on Fandom corroborates that a crane exists]
22. **Warhead:** open with 1961; both agents use **Thermal Vision** to find their active wire and **cut simultaneously** = "Retrieve a viral sample from one of the missiles". CO-OP REQUIRED. [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom email (cut the two hot wires simultaneously)]

**Exfiltration**
23. Trapdoor in the Blue Room floor -> ladder -> sewer tunnels -> ladder up into the **Missile Boat Room**; shoot its lights, sneak past (or knock out) guards to the extraction point, a small motor boat (the Zodiac) at the far side. [VERIFIED - Prima + Fandom objective (Zodiac at the end of the missile-boat dock)]
