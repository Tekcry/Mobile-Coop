# PART A - Xbox / PC build ("Train Station")

## A1. Setting & premise
- Location: Grand Central Station, Manhattan, reached from below through sewers, a train repair yard, a hydro tunnel, a machine room and a metro station. [VERIFIED - Prima walkthrough; Fandom; hexen2k7's description reproduces the briefing naming Grand Central]
- In-game date 18 October 2007. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom]
- Campaign position: 5th co-op level counting Training (Training, Panama, Seoul 3, Chemical Bunker, Train Station). Prima, written before the downloadable missions, calls it the agents' "fourth and final mission". So it was the ORIGINAL retail finale; Nuclear Plant and UN HQ were added later. [VERIFIED - Prima; Fandom; speedrun.com level list; DeepVelocityOps chapters; PleasePlayMore and BoSstonPL call it the fourth mission]
- Premise (paraphrased from the in-game briefing): Colonel Kim, former commander of the missile bunker, broke with the North Korean army over ending the war and leaving Seoul. He took bio-weapon warheads, disappeared, and six hours earlier resurfaced in New York with NKA defectors who seized Grand Central. They have made no demands. Stop the bio-weapon release and find out what Kim is really after. [VERIFIED - Fandom briefing; same text in hexen2k7 description]. Prima's summary frames it as bombs planted by "Kim and Chu" under the station. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
- Kim is never met here; he is heard only on the phone with Chu. [VERIFIED - Prima (the bugged call is between Chu and Kim); Fandom "Kim Park-Chu" page]
- Story hook forward: the next briefing says forensic work on the bombs found "3 double-laser detonators" traced to Yongbyon. [VERIFIED - Fandom Nuclear Plant briefing; hexen2k7 Nuclear Plant description]

## A2. Objective chain
Primary (game order): [VERIFIED - Prima; Fandom; hexen2k7 description - all three list the same six]
1. Figure out what Colonel Kim's real agenda is.
2. Deactivate the bombs under the train station (three bombs in the subway tunnels, strong enough to collapse several city blocks).
3. Bug the telephone in the train station hall (Fandom/hexen wording adds "before Chu reaches it").
4. Get the antidote from the locker.
5. Eliminate the contaminated soldier in the bathroom.
6. Stop Kim's men from leaving with the rest of the toxin (Prima's wording: "the rest of the virus").
Secondary: none. [VERIFIED - Prima, Fandom, hexen2k7]
Opportunity:
- Follow Chu to the locker (room). [VERIFIED - Prima, Fandom, hexen2k7]
- Secure the chemical toxin held by the infected soldier (pick up the vial he drops after he is killed). [VERIFIED objective - three sources; "vial on the ground" SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; Fandom instead says wait for the body to cool down]
- Recover schematics for the bombs (first computer). [Listed by Fandom and hexen2k7 (2 sources) but NOT in Prima's Xbox/PC objective box, which shows only two opportunity objectives. Possibly an OCR loss or a later build change. -> UNCERTAIN whether it existed at the guide's build]

How objectives are revealed:
- The phone call is the reveal engine: after the bug is planted, the agents must wait and listen as Chu talks to Kim. They learn one of Kim's men accidentally infected himself and is hiding in a bathroom, and that Kim is sending Chu to a locker room to get the anti-virus (Prima names locker 9201). This sets up objectives 4-5 and the "follow Chu" opportunity. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; the "long phone conversation" is corroborated by a 2018 speedrun note -> wait is VERIFIED]
- Whether objectives 4-6 are hidden until the call ends, or listed from the start, is not stated. Prima's objective box shows all six (but a guide box would). [UNCERTAIN]
- Locker code 0315 comes from interrogating Chu. [VERIFIED - Prima; Fandom (EN page + keypad codes page)]

## A3. Ordered progression (Prima perfect-score route; step order reconstructed from garbled OCR)
1. Insertion in a sewer tunnel. Both agents shimmy along an overhead pipe to cross a gap. Knock out the first guard when he walks into shadow. One agent fires a sticky camera at the far wall around the next corner and gasses the second guard. Another overhead pipe gets them past crates. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
2. Climb shelves at the tunnel's end and drop through a hole in the wall into the "Train Reparation" (repair) area. Shoot out or throw objects at all lights, then knock out the guards in the dark. An automatic machine-gun turret sits on the tracks: one agent holds it off with the pistol's OCP while the other runs behind it and switches it off. The door by the turret leads to the Hydro Tunnel. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Co-op move: OCP cover + disarm = REQUIRED-ish two-person action (a solo player cannot both jam and walk around it). [UNCERTAIN - inferred]
3. Hydro Tunnel - bomb set 1. Two bombs sit on facing walls with a laser beam between them; touching the beam detonates them. One agent shoots a light on the far wall to pull a guard over; the other sets up a Tomoe Nage by the laser and throws his partner over the beam at the moment the guard is near, so the thrown agent lands on the guard and knocks him out. The second guard who comes to check is knocked out too. Then the thrower walks to the wall bomb on his side without breaking the beam; the thrown agent goes through a nearby door to the matching bomb. Both cut the live wires at the same moment. Thermal vision shows which wire is live; they must talk to sync. [SINGLE SOURCE for the throw-into-guard tactic - Prima; the simultaneous-cut mechanic VERIFIED - Prima, Fandom, AnandTech thread, Elder-Geek review]
   - Tomoe Nage over the laser: REQUIRED in Prima's route (there may be other ways round; not documented). [SINGLE SOURCE]
   - Simultaneous wire cut: REQUIRED. [VERIFIED]
   - The in-game intel describes two stages: the first simultaneous cut breaks the "laser link" and wakes a second security system needing a second simultaneous cut; the live wire changes over time ("pulses"). [Fandom transcription of the notebook; Prima says "cutting the two live wires"; a Neoseeker forum answer (search snippet) says thermal vision shows the wire that lights on each pulse -> VERIFIED in substance]
4. Go to the far end of the train tunnel. Boost + Human Ladder up to a ledge, then down a ladder into a small room. One agent uses OCP on that room's light while the other opens the door into the large Machine Room and immediately shoots its overhead light, darkening half the room. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Boost + Human Ladder: REQUIRED (height). [SINGLE SOURCE]
5. Machine Room - bomb set 2. While guards go to look at the broken light, both agents hug the right wall to the bombs at the far end. The lead agent creeps along the laser (without touching it) to the matching bomb. Simultaneous cut again. A hallway beyond leads to the Metro Station. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
6. Metro Station - bomb set 3. The agents split: one creeps through shadows on one side (OCR garbled, probably the right side of a parked train), the other through the narrow gap on the train's left side. Disarm the third set -> primary "deactivate the bombs" complete. The right-side agent cuts through the dark train car to rejoin. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Forced SPLIT by the train; REGROUP through the car.
7. Both creep along the train's left side to the last car, cross the tracks behind a stationary guard, and take the stairs. Knock out the guard in the next hallway; one agent uses a sticky shocker on the guard by the phone booths. Ride up the escalators, shooting the overhead light and a lit billboard to darken the top. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
8. Ticket booth: Boost one agent onto its roof; he drops through a roof trapdoor onto the guard inside, knocking him out, then opens the door for his partner. Out the far door and down a hallway into Grand Central's main hall. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Boost: REQUIRED in this route.
9. Main hall - the payphone. Go behind the circular phone booth in the middle of the hall, Boost + Human Ladder onto its roof, move to the front edge, then do a Hangover (one agent lowers the other head-down on a rope) so the hanging agent plants the bug on the phone. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Hangover: REQUIRED in Prima's route. Then wait and listen to the Chu-Kim call (see A2). [phone objective VERIFIED; method SINGLE SOURCE]
10. Tail Chu to the locker room without being seen, quietly knocking out guards on the way (opportunity "follow Chu"). In the locker room one agent knocks out Chu's bodyguard while the other grabs Chu from behind and interrogates him for the code 0315. One agent opens the locker and injects himself with the anti-virus. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; code VERIFIED]
   - CONFLICT: Fandom RU says Chu can be interrogated but cannot be knocked out and always dies; Suru's timestamp list calls this beat "Killing Chu"; Fandom's opportunity note says "kill Chu near the lockers". Prima only says grab and interrogate. [UNCERTAIN what happens to Chu after the interrogation; 2 non-Prima sources say he dies]
11. Leave by the double doors, knock out the hallway guard, go through the door marked "NY Transit Police" and knock out the guard there (Fandom's data table lists a disabled "Police Station" computer - consistent). Only the injected agent goes on to the bathroom. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; police station VERIFIED via Fandom data table]
12. Bathroom: the injected agent sneaks behind the vomiting infected soldier and stabs him (primary - a REQUIRED kill), then picks up the vial (opportunity). Once the soldier is dead the partner may enter safely. Only one agent can use the antidote; entering without it kills you. [VERIFIED - Prima ("safe for the other agent once the infected guard is dead"); Fandom RU (only one antidote; infected room is lethal; soldier cannot be taken non-lethally)]
13. Boost + Human Ladder into an opening in the bathroom ceiling, crawl the duct, drop out into the Final Metro Station. [VERIFIED - Prima; Neoseeker forum reply (search snippet) says the bathroom vent co-op move leads to the lower platform]. REQUIRED.
14. Finale: one agent goes down an escalator to knock out the guard at the bottom while the other follows with pistol ready to shoot lights; strike when the far guard turns away. Shoot the remaining lights and knock out the last two guards. To end the mission, jump onto a glass dome, smash through it and land on the roof of the subway train Kim's men are escaping on. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]. Suru's run reaches "Extraction" at 13:30. [SINGLE SOURCE]

## A4. Spatial flow
- Vertical structure: starts at the lowest layer (sewer), rises through service spaces (repair yard -> Hydro Tunnel -> Machine Room -> Metro Station), climbs stairs/escalators to the ticket hall and the Grand Central main concourse, then drops back DOWN through a bathroom ceiling duct to the Final Metro Station, ending with a drop through a glass dome onto a train roof. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima; overall down-up-down shape consistent with Suru's beat order]
- All interior/underground; the main hall is the one large open public space. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
- Chokepoints: each laser-linked bomb pair; the turret on the repair-yard tracks; the ticket booth; the phone booth; the single-agent infected bathroom; the ceiling duct. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
- Branches: the Metro Station split along both sides of a parked train, re-merging through the train car. [SINGLE SOURCE - Prima]
- Other rooms named: Warehouse (5-pin lock), and locks on Bomb 1 and Bomb 3 per Fandom; Prima's route mentions no lockpicking, so these locks are probably on alternative routes. [SINGLE SOURCE - Fandom; inference UNCERTAIN]

## A7. Pacing curve
- Act 1 (underground infiltration, ~45% of a fast run): sewer stealth -> light-killing in the repair yard -> three bomb pairs in quick succession (Suru: 3:06, 4:43, 5:28). Peak tension at each wire cut. [VERIFIED order]
- Act 2 (public spaces): escalator climb, ticket-booth ambush, then the phone booth set piece in the grand hall. The forced wait while the Chu-Kim call plays is the main rest beat and the story reveal. [Prima; speedrun comment]
- Act 3 (tail and contamination): moving-target stealth (follow Chu), interrogation, antidote, a lone agent in the infected bathroom. [Prima]
- Finale: duct drop, last guards, leap through the glass dome onto the escaping train. [Prima]
- Timers: no on-screen mission timer is documented; the Hall/Chu beats are event-driven. [UNCERTAIN]
- Run lengths: hexen2k7 Elite 100% ghost 11:41; Suru 13:38; PC IL records 4:52 (2026) and 6:48 (2018); casual playthroughs 30-70 min. [multiple uploads]

# PART B - PS2 / GameCube build ("Train Station")

## B1. Setting, premise, structure
- Same setting; THIRD mission of the PS2/GC co-op campaign (Team Training, Seoul, Chemical Bunker, Train Station, Panama). [VERIFIED - Prima ("third mission"); Fandom; NickyTheGamer ("third chapter")]
