## 1. Setting and premise
- **Place and time.** The United Nations headquarters in Manhattan, at night: a tower plus a second building containing the General Assembly hall. [VERIFIED - FAN + PR + hexen2k7; building layout SINGLE SOURCE - FAN notes]
- **Briefing (paraphrase).**

  - The building is among the best-protected in the world, and it is international territory.
  - **No lethal force** is allowed, and the controller **aborts on a general alarm**.
  - [VERIFIED - FAN + hexen2k7 + PR "no room for kills or alarms"]
- **Story function.** The colonel's last bomb is timed for a peacekeeping assembly that night. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN data; the link between missions is our deduction]

## 2. Objective chain [VERIFIED - FAN + hexen2k7]
1. **Infiltrate the building.** The service stairs reach the top floor, but the emergency doors there open only from inside, so the agents must find another way.
2. **Recover the intercepted data.**
3. **Reach the security control room** (22nd floor) by elevator. The cameras are off.
4. **Stop the colonel.** He has seized the security control room. This twist is discovered on arrival. [SINGLE SOURCE - GameSpot excerpt]
5. **Defuse the bomb** in the ventilation above the General Assembly hall before the countdown ends.

There are no secondary or opportunity objectives.

**Mission notes** spell out the route [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]:
- the security room is on the 22nd floor;
- the hall is in the second building;
- the ground floor is reached by service elevator;
- emergency doors open only from inside.

## 3. Assembled progression [UNCERTAIN order; elements SINGLE SOURCE - FAN]
1. **Entry from height.** The controller's opening line orders the pair to **rappel down to the window-washing platform** and get in from there. The emergency doors open only from inside. [VERIFIED - game text, Xbox + PC; roof-door start also confirmed by a speedrunner]
2. **Top floor.** A stairwell lock beside the elevator.
3. **Middle floor.** A technical-room lock, the intercepted data, and the security-room entrance terminal.
   - The security-room keypad code can be learned by luring guards to the keypad and watching them type it with a sticky camera plus thermal or night vision. That is a two-player-friendly observation trick.
   - Alternatively, push a knocked-out guard into another guard, which costs stealth rating.
4. **Elevator to the 22nd floor.** The colonel and his men hold the room.
   - The colonel cannot be grabbed or interrogated.
   - Lethal force *is* allowed against his men (the restriction covers UN staff and civilians).
   - His fate conflicts between wiki pages (stunned vs killed). A no-kill run counts one "objective" knockout. [UNCERTAIN]
5. **Down through the lower floors.** A turret, a **metal detector** (disabled at terminals) with a locked toilet next to it, then the service elevator to the ground floor and across to the second building.
6. **Locked side rooms** in front of the central hall: a ventilation room and a technical room.
7. **Ventilation above the General Assembly.** Defuse the bomb. The game text has controller warnings at 20, 10 and 5 minutes and at 60 seconds, so the countdown is announced by voice-text even though no number is shown. [VERIFIED - game text + FAN RU] When it starts is undocumented. The defusal mechanic is not in the text. One player comment mentions wires, which suggests the series' two-person wire cut is reused. ## 13. Design reading (analysis)
1. **Rules from the fiction.** International territory means no lethal force. A world-class guarded building means one alarm ends it. The constraints feel justified rather than arbitrary.
2. **Real security logic as obstacles.** Emergency exits that only open from inside (true of real fire exits), a metal detector at a public entrance, and a security control room as the hub. These are things a real building would have.
3. **Top-down infiltration.** Entering from the roof and working down is a classic counter to ground-floor security, and it gives a vertical spine.
4. **Risks to avoid.** Several instant-fail rules stacked together, plus an *invisible* timer, risk unfairness. If a finale uses a countdown, show it.
5. **Twist via objective text.** Arriving to find the room already taken re-scopes the mission at the two-thirds mark.
