## 1. Setting and premise
- **Location.** A nuclear research complex in North Korea: a power plant and a separate radiochemistry laboratory joined by an outdoor footbridge. [VERIFIED - FAN + PR + hexen2k7 briefing]
- **Briefing (Xbox order, paraphrase).**
  - The station attack was stopped, but the colonel escaped.
  - Forensics on the defused bombs traced their twin-laser detonators to this plant, long suspected of being a weapons-research cover.
  - The colonel could not have acted alone. The pair must find out who helped him and how many detonators he received.
  - [VERIFIED - FAN + hexen2k7]
- **Payoff in the data.** Emails show the lab director shipped **four** detonators on orders arranged by an intelligence officer. Three were used in New York, so **one bomb remains**. This sets up the UN finale. [SINGLE SOURCE - FAN data; deduction is ours]
- **One uploader** describes a PC briefing that traces the plant via the UN bomb instead, which implies the PC text was rewritten for the swapped order. [UNCERTAIN]

## 13. Design reading (analysis)
1. **Infrastructure as a puzzle box.** Fans that must be stopped before their duct becomes a route is a strong real-world-logic obstacle: machinery has states the players can change.
2. **Two buildings and a bridge.** A crossing gives the mission a clear midpoint and an exposed outdoor beat.
3. **Investigation objective.** "Who helped him?" is answered by data spread over two computer clusters. That requires visiting both buildings, and the payoff ("one bomb left") propels the story.
4. **Lesson from the frustrations.** A machine-state puzzle needs a clear readout of state (are the fans running?) and an obvious control location, or players stall.

## 14. Gap-closing update (primary game files + community)

- **Route sequence from the controller's guidance lines** [VERIFIED - identical Xbox and PC game text]:
  1. Start with no blueprints: get inside and find the main control room.
  2. With the blueprints taken, head to the turbine zone.
  3. The security doors are blocked; a nearby computer controls them.
  4. The turbine is overhead: reach the stairs on the far side of the room, then cross the turbine room toward the control room.
  5. The lift cannot be called from there, so find another way up.
  6. The controller scolds players who linger near radioactive material.
  7. The lab server is reached through a **ceiling trap-door**.
  8. After learning who helped the colonel, leave by the lab's side door.
- **Designer triggers.** Messages fire at the security doors, on the stairs to the turbine room, on the footbridge outside the lab, and at the archives. [SINGLE SOURCE - level package]
- **Payoff email.** Four detonators were delivered, so one bomb remains. [VERIFIED]
- **Text-only controller.** He has no voice in this mission. [VERIFIED - player comments + Steam]
- **Hardest mission.** Players call it the hardest and longest co-op mission, with a confusing layout and many sub-objectives. Speedruns use an invisibility glitch, so they are not evidence of the intended route. [SINGLE SOURCE each]
