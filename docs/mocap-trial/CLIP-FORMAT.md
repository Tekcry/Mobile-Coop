# mocap-trial - the clip format the retarget writes

The mocap clips use the hand-keyed clips' format (`src/anim/keyed.ts` `KeyedClipDef` / `keyedClip`, sampled by the same
`sampleKeyed`), so the switch only picks which set the graph reads (`src/anim/mocap.ts`). Read from
`scripts/anim-keys/build.mjs` (`clipSource`) and `scripts/anim-keys/py/key_clip.py` (the bake), and from the runtime
(`keyed.ts`, `animGraph.ts`, `footPlanner.ts`). Wren: height H = 1.68 m, K = H / 1.75 = 0.96.

(Integration) The files hold the CMU clips (SOURCES.md "Used"); `build/mocap/clips/{alt,extra}` hold the rest. The retarget overwrites
each file whole: a header comment (generated by, from which CMU take(s) and frames, Blender version, Wren 1.68 m, "do not
edit"), `import { keyedClip } from '../../keyed';` and `export const MOCAP_<NAME> = keyedClip({ ... });` with `name`
`mocap_<name>`. Every array is flat, one row per frame, values rounded to 5 decimals (as `clipSource`).

## Frames, units, phase
- Body frame at 1.75 m: x right, y up, z forward. Every length is Wren's metres / K (the runtime multiplies by the rig's
  height / 1.75). Angles in radians, quaternions (x, y, z, w), Babylon's left-handed axes (yaw + turns toward +x, right).
- `frames` N: the samples. A cycle is sampled at phases i / N, i = 0 .. N - 1 (no repeated end frame: the runtime wraps
  frame N - 1 to frame 0, so the loop must close). The hand-keyed clips use 32; any N >= 8 works; 32-48 is plenty.
- Phase 0 = LEFT heel strike, 0.5 = RIGHT heel strike. Time-warp each half cycle of the take (left strike to right strike,
  right strike to the next left strike) onto 0.5 each, so the contacts sit on the game's gait clock windows.
- Root and root motion: NONE in the clip. Per frame the root is the ground point under the take's straight-line root
  trend over the cycle: origin = the pelvis's ground projection at phase 0, moving at the take's mean velocity along the
  cycle's mean heading (circular mean of the pelvis yaw); its heading is fixed. Everything below is relative to it: the
  pelvis's forward surge, sway and bob stay in `pel`, the stride-locked pelvis yaw in `q`. The game moves the root.
- Cadence and stride on our speeds: the cadence is always the game's gait clock (`stepLength(speed)` in `motion.ts`, two
  steps a cycle). Game stride per cycle (m) and cycle time: 1.4 m/s 1.16 m / 0.83 s; 2.1 m/s 1.44 m / 0.69 s; 3.0 m/s
  2.04 m / 0.68 s; 3.8 m/s 2.42 m / 0.64 s; 4.4 m/s 2.60 m / 0.59 s. Upper-body channels play on that clock (their
  timing follows the game's cadence); the feet's fore-aft positions are scaled by game stride / `stride` (below). Pick
  takes near those speeds so the scale stays near 1 (0.8-1.25 looks right; report the scale per clip).

| `pel` | 3 | Pelvis joint position minus its rest (Wren's bind pelvis joint) in the root frame, / K. |

## Fields (optional, the same as the hand-keyed clips)
`wrist` (8: wrist L, R local to the forearm), `fore` (8: forearm twist L, R, local to the elbow node about the bone),
`head` (4: (integration) the head's turn from upright-forward in the root frame, the clip's mean pitch taken out: the rig holds the head level along the look first and turns it by this channel on the look, so a turn on the neck tipped the gaze 17-21 deg down), `sh` (8: shoulder L, R in the chest frame about the shoulder joint),
`clav` (8: clavicle L, R local in the chest frame), `fing` (20: per hand L then R, thumb .. little, [curl, spread] added
to the grip shape; curl 1 = closed, spread 1 = 30 deg). Leave out a channel the take has nothing for (identity).
Do NOT write `leg` (FK legs over the planner) or `legd` (deltas on the IK legs): the clip's legs come through `feet`.

## Clip feet (`feet`, `stride`; A3: the clip places the steps)
With `feet` the mocap clip drives Wren's steps: the foot planner (`FootPlanner.clipFoot`) does not choose steps; it lifts
each foot when the gait clock's stance window ends, moves it onto the clip's ankle position (relative to the moving root,
the gap from where it lifted fading over the swing), lands it where it is, puts it on the ground (height and slope probe)
and pins it there with no slide until the clip lifts it. The leg IK then reaches the planned foot (knee pole from
`kneeOut`, the pelvis dropping if a leg cannot reach).

| Field | Per frame | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `feet` | 10 | Per side, L then R (`keyed.ts` `KF`): ankle x (lateral), y (lift), z (fore-aft) in the ROOT frame / K; foot pitch (rad); contact (0 / 1). |
| `stride` | | The root's travel over one cycle, / K (take speed x cycle duration / K). Required with `feet`. |
| `travel` | | (mocap-trial-2, strafe) The direction the root travels over a cycle: `'z'` forward (absent: every forward cycle), `'+x'` right, `'-x'` left (a sideways cycle); (mixamo-retarget I7) `'-z'` backward. |

- x, z: the ankle joint's ground projection relative to the root (above). While a foot is planted in the take its z falls
  back at the take's speed; the runtime scales z by game stride / `stride`, so a planted foot moves back under the body at
  exactly the game's root speed and the pin holds. x scales with the body only (the track width stays the take's).
- y: the ankle joint's height minus its flat-foot rest height (Wren's ankle above the sole), / K, swing frames only;
  write 0 on contact frames (a planted foot sits on the ground). Clamped at 0 by the runtime. Do not add the heel / toe
  rise: in a clip swing the rig adds nothing (`heelLift` returns 0), the clip's y is the whole lift.
- pitch: the foot's pitch about its lateral axis relative to level ground, + heel up / toes down (the planner's sense:
  toe-off positive, a heel-first strike negative). On contact frames it raises the heel through the rig's foot model
  (toe on the floor).
- contact: 1 while the take's foot is planted (heel or toe on the floor, little velocity), else 0. The runtime times the
  contacts from `duty` and the phase (left [0, duty), right [0.5, 0.5 + duty)); write the flags so they agree after the warp.
- When it holds (`mocap.ts` MOCAP_FEET): the mocap share of the keyed pose at least 0.5 (holstered, standing, on foot,
  not at a wall), turning slower than 1 rad/s, faster than 0.3 m/s. Otherwise the planner places the steps as today (its
  turn rules: the turn-step clock, the twist cap, the stance band; anim-natural N1's rules once that merges); a clip swing
  already in the air finishes under the clip. So in this pass fast turns, starts and stops keep planned feet; the turn /
  start / stop slots pose the body only (their `feet` are ignored).
- Blending: walk / jog / run blend their feet linearly by the speed blend; the strides blend the same way.
- Sideways cycles (`travel` `'+x'` / `'-x'`, mocap-trial-2 strafe, BRIEF A10: CMU 69_56 right, 69_59 left): the root is turned
  to the body facing (faceRoot), then the take's travel onto the x axis (emit.mjs `travel`); `stride` is the sideways travel
  over a cycle. The game plays them as the aimed glide's sideways pose (animGraph `keyedDrawnSet`) over the planner's
  side-steps, not as clip feet (their `feet` are written but not read): the side-step keeps the captures' rhythm
  (motion.ts `GLIDE_SIDE`, mocap.ts `MOCAP_STRAFE`). MT2-STRAFE.md.

## One-shot slots (turns, start, stop)
The same fields, not a cycle: frame 0 is the first frame of the move, frame N - 1 its last; sampled by progress 0..1
over the frames (the last frame held, never wrapping to frame 0). No phase warp; the root is the take's own root path
with its heading taken out progressively (below). Played over the standing gait pose by their weight (in over the first
10 %, out over the last 25 % of the progress, `MOCAP_SLOT`), with the procedural feet.
- Turns: progress = the body yaw the game has turned since the turn began / 90 deg (CT turns at up to 450 deg/s, so the
  clip plays at the game's turn speed, not the take's). The root's heading must turn 0 -> 90 deg over the clip with the
  body: write the trunk relative to that heading (the head and chest leading, the pelvis trailing show as yaw in `q`). The
  in-place turn and a missing L / R side play the other mirrored (`mirrorKeyed`: x negated, quaternion y and z negated,
  sides swapped). A spin longer than 90 deg on the spot replays it. Triggers: turning faster than 1 rad/s; on the spot
  under 0.5 m/s.
- Start: progress = seconds since the pace left rest / `MOCAP_START_SECONDS` (write the take's length there).
- Stop: progress = seconds since hard braking (over 1.5 m/s^2 from over 0.5 m/s) / `MOCAP_STOP_SECONDS`.

## Layer scales (`MOCAP_LAYERS`, src/anim/mocap.ts)
All 1 (every procedural layer as today); the job lead tunes them once the clips land: `armSwing` (the tuner's arm swing
gain on the clip's arms), `trunkTwist` (the tuner's trunk counter-rotation), `keyLean` (the tuner's forward lean),
`pelvisFollow` (the hips trailing the chest toward the planted feet), `weightLoad` (the Weight section's plant / landing
drop), `stopSettle` (the procedural stop settle), `bodyMotion` / `turnStep` (reserved for anim-natural N2 / N1, not on
master yet).

## Check before handing a clip over
`keyedClip` throws on a wrong array length or `feet` without `stride`. Then: `npx vitest run tests/mocapSwitch.test.ts`
(its placeholder proofs swap the hand-keyed clips back in with the layers neutral, so they keep passing; its
integration cases measure the real clips on a headless rig), then the GPU contact sheets (current vs mocap) on the laptop.
