# Gate dead-line-v2-G1: Dead Line v2, Area 1 greybox geometry

Stage G1 only, Area 1 only. Map `dead-line-v2` (`?autostart=dead-line-v2&mode=sandbox`, or Play, "Dead Line v2", Deploy). Generated from `docs/design/map-dead-line-v2.json` (unedited) by `scripts/gen-dead-line-v2.mjs` into `src/world/maps/deadLineV2.geo.json`, replayed by `src/world/maps/deadLineV2.ts`. No guards, lamps, AI, art or audio; flat debug light (theme light level 0.95). The old Dead Line (`deadLine.ts`, `gen-dead-line.mjs`, its JSON, its menu card, its suites) is untouched and still runs. Areas 2 to 4 are not built.

The task said to point the existing generator at the v2 JSON. It was written for the indoor v1 schema (it walls every space, builds stairs, and its `yard` line is v1's); v2 is outdoor, with blocks as the walls. So v2 has its own generator, and `gen-dead-line.mjs` stays as it was for the old map.

## Where the JSON could not be built exactly, and what was done
1. **DP3 cannot be climbed.** Its foot (33.3, 20.5) is inside the skip block SKIP (33 to 37, 19.4 to 21, 1.4 m). Built as written; no route uses DP3. Needs a JSON fix (move the pipe or the skip).
2. **Roof lights are not enforced as unwalkable.** The block is a solid 0.3 m glazed upstand; the controller steps up to 0.65 m, so a player can stand on it. The engine has no fall-through glass. The routes keep to the gutter strip. Decision for Michael: breakable glass over a closed void, or a taller frame.
3. **Anti-climb spikes** are 288 thin pins on a 0.3 m grid (a solid 0.4 m slab would be a step). The capsule cannot pass between pins; whether the controller can balance on a pin's top is untested.
4. **Palisades.** RLY is a 0.05 m "block" and the substation fence has no JSON element; both are built from the label and the gate: upright 0.04 m bars every 0.2 m with two rails, on the open edges of the compound and of the forecourt. They carry `noLedge`.
5. **Manhole covers** are closed plates flush with the street (walkable, as in reality), so the UP and FP1 routes can cross M1. Lifting them is the hold interaction (mission logic, later). The BELOW test lifts them by removing the cover colliders (`removeChild` on the level's shape container); that is test-only.
6. **Slab `y` is read as the underside** (ceiling plane, eave, bridge soffit), 0.3 m thick; the bridge is 1.0 m thick so its top meets the viaduct top (7.5). The eaves therefore sit flush with the roof walk. The downpipes hang off the eave front 0.08 m out: the JSON foot at z 20.5 is 0.18 m from the pipe.
7. **Arches.** The JSON has the viaduct as one solid block with the A17 bricked infill; there are no arch shapes to build. The arches' shutters are the solid wall.
8. **Open edges the JSON does not close.** The ground slab covers the whole footprint, so a player can walk off Viaduct Road to the south (x 0 to 12, z below 10), east into the field behind the viaduct (z 38 to 60), and through the vehicle gate gap (x 191 to 193, z 11 to 17) onto the empty ground of Area 2. The JSON lists no barrier for any of them. Not fixed (JSON untouched).
9. All doors and the gate are unlocked; locks, holds, lamps and the light circuits are not built (no objective logic in G1).
10. The tunnel is below the nav sampler's floor of -1.5: the guards' grid does not cover it (no Area 1 guard goes below ground).
