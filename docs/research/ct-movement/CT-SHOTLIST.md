# Chaos Theory capture list (M20: pure Chaos Theory)

What Michael records from Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory so the locomotion update prompt can copy CT exactly.
Written sources gave no CT timings, distances or poses (docs/research/ct-movement/). Footage is the authority (M20).

## Before you start (once)
1. Use the PC version if you have it (best for 60 fps and for item Z). Any version works otherwise.
2. Record VIDEO at 60 fps (OBS, Xbox Game Bar Win+Alt+R, or console capture). Screenshots only where it says STILL.
3. Game settings: default camera, default field of view, HUD on, subtitles off. Do not change them between clips.
4. Use a plain, well-lit spot with a normal door nearby (a door is about 2 m tall: I use it as a ruler).
5. Use a controller if you have one (analog speeds), keyboard otherwise. Say which in the file name (`pad` / `kb`).
6. Save each file as `NN-name.mp4` (or `.png`) using the numbers below, into
   `C:\NightShift\Mobile-Coop-locomotion\pc\ct-ref\` on the desktop (that folder stays local, it is never committed).
7. In each clip, do the action once, then repeat it once more. Hold still for 2 s before and after.

"Side view" = turn the camera until the hero is seen exactly side-on, then do not touch the camera.
"Front view" = camera in front of the hero, facing him. "Behind" = the normal play camera.

## Z. Game files (PC only, 2 minutes, saves hours)
Z1. Copy the CT install's `System` folder `.ini` files (e.g. `SplinterCell3.ini`, `SplinterCell3User.ini`, any
    `*.ini` with "Camera" or "Echelon" in it) into `pc\ct-ref\ini\`. They may hold the exact camera distances.

## A. Standing still and posture (STILLs unless noted)
A1. Standing, weapon away: side STILL, front STILL.
A2. Standing, rifle out (not aiming): side STILL, front STILL.
A3. Standing, pistol out: side STILL.
A4. Crouched, weapon away: side STILL, front STILL.
A5. Crouched, rifle out: side STILL, front STILL.
A6. Stand still for 10 s, side view, video: breathing and idle movement.

## B. Speeds (side view, walk past the camera left to right, about 4 s each)
B1. Standing, weapon away: every speed step from slowest to fastest, one after another, without stopping. One video.
B2. Same, crouched. One video.
B3. Standing, rifle out (not aiming), slowest to fastest. One video.
B4. Aiming the rifle and walking forward, then sideways, then backwards. One video.

## C. Starting and stopping (side view)
C1. From standing still, push to full speed. Repeat at slow speed. Video.
C2. Walking at a slow speed, let go of the stick. Wait 3 s. Push again. Video.
C3. Same at a middle speed. Video.
C4. Same at full speed. Video.
C5. Same three, crouched. One video.
C6. Same, rifle out. One video.

## D. Turning
D1. Standing still, push the stick slowly round a full circle (camera behind, then side). Video.
D2. Standing still, flick the stick to directly behind the hero. Video.
D3. Standing still, spin the stick fast in circles for 5 s; watch the feet. Video, side view and from above if the camera allows.
D4. Walking slowly: curve left, then right. Video from behind.
D5. Full speed: turn 90 deg, then reverse direction completely. Video from behind and side.
D6. Crouched: turn on the spot, slow and fast. Video.
D7. Aiming the rifle while standing still: turn round with the camera. Video.

## E. Crouch, roll, jump, fall
E1. Stand to crouch and back, standing still and while walking. Side video.
E2. Roll from full speed. Side video.
E3. Jump standing still; jump while walking; jump at full speed. Side video.
E4. Drop off something about waist high, chest high, and from one storey; with crouch held and without. Side video.

## F. Camera
F1. Walk forward 5 s, stop, wait 3 s, without touching the camera. Video from behind.
F2. Walk while looking left with the camera, then right: does the camera swing sides by itself? Video.
F3. Raise the rifle to aim and lower it, standing 3 m from a door facing it. Video. Then an aim STILL.
F4. Same with the pistol. Video + STILL.
F5. While aiming the rifle, switch shoulder, then switch back. Slow if you can. Video. Same with the pistol.
F6. Sniper / zoom view in and out, and hold breath. Video.
F7. Crouched aim (rifle): aim STILL from 3 m to the door.

## G. Back to wall
G1. Put the hero's back to a wall, standing: side STILL, front STILL, and from above if possible.
G2. Same crouched: side STILL, front STILL.
G3. Slide along the wall left and right, standing then crouched, slow then full speed. Video from behind.
G4. Slide to the end of the wall at an outside corner and keep pushing the stick. Video.
G5. At that corner, peek round it (however CT does it), standing then crouched. Video from behind; then a STILL of the camera view while peeking.
G6. While on the wall, draw the rifle and try to aim. Video (what happens?).
G7. Leave the wall: by pushing away, and by pressing the button. Video.
G8. Crouch next to a waist-high crate or low wall, back to it, then try to aim over it. Video + STILL of the highest point.
G9. Same, at the end of the low wall: try to aim round the side. Video.

## H. Doors
H1. Open a door very slowly with the stick, stop half way, then close it slowly. Video from behind.
H2. Open a door at normal speed and walk through. Video.
H3. Leave a door open and let a guard find it (if possible). Video.
H4. Bash a door, ideally with a guard behind it. Video.
H5. Optic cable under a door. Video.
H6. Pick a lock. Video.

## I. Close combat
I1. Walk up behind an unaware guard and strike (non-lethal). Video, side view.
I2. Run up to a guard from the front and strike. Video.
I3. Strike a guard who has seen you and is facing you. Video.
I4. Press the strike button with no guard near. Video (does anything happen?).
I5. Lethal knife attack from behind and from the front. Video.
I6. Grab a guard from behind, walk with him, then knock him out. Video.
I7. Pick up a body, carry it, drop it; throw a guard or body over a railing if you can. Video.
I8. Whistle to draw a guard. Video.

## J. Traversal (one short side-view video each)
J1. Climb onto a low crate; climb a chest-high wall; climb onto a high ledge.
J2. Hang from a ledge, shimmy left and right, pull up, drop.
J3. Ladder up and down. Drainpipe up and down.
J4. Horizontal pipe: hands, legs up, upside down, aim upside down, drop.
J5. Split jump: get in, aim, get out.
J6. Wall jump (jump, then jump off the wall).
J7. Zipline, with feet tucked up.
J8. Rappel down, swing out, aim.
J9. Into and out of a vent or crawl space.
J10. Fence climb (if any level has one). Window entry.

## K. Vision modes
K1. Thermal vision in a dark room with a guard close (3 m) and far (15 m+): STILL each, then a 5 s pan video.
K2. Thermal looking at a lamp, a window, and a wall someone is standing behind: STILLs.
K3. Night vision, same room: STILL + short pan.
K4. EMF (electromagnetic) vision, same room: STILL + short pan.

## L. Co-op (only with a second player)
L1. Boost, human ladder, hang over teammate, partner throw, dual rappel, back-to-back climb. One video each.

## Priority
If time is short, in this order: Z, A, B, C, D, F, G, then I1-I4, H1, K1-K2, E, J, the rest.
A-D and F-G decide stops, posture, turns, camera and the wall. Everything else refines later steps.
