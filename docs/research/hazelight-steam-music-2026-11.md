## A. It Takes Two (2021)

### Process
- About 60 developers, UE4, about 2.5-3 years. https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/it-takes-two-lovingly-marries-story-and-gameplay-together
- Pods of two designers, two programmers, two artists, two animators; each pod owns levels; a designer acts as mini
  producer. https://80.lv/articles/hazelight-studios-working-culture-and-approach-to-creating-collaborative-gameplay
- A good prototype mechanic takes "a week or two"; making it feel good takes much longer.
  https://80.lv/articles/it-takes-two-creator-shared-how-filmmaking-helped-the-game-mechanics
- Mechanics cut when they "would take too much time to polish". https://www.avclub.com/josef-fares-it-takes-two-interview
- A 2-minute section took about 6 months. https://80.lv/articles/it-takes-two-creator-shared-how-filmmaking-helped-the-game-mechanics
- "For each level we set up our own rule set... teaching a player a new game in an hour"; the Tree level was planned at
  1 hour and ran to nearly 2; "design by subtraction". https://amara.org/subtitles/tUIr67mipkE6/en/1/download/It%20Takes%20Two.en.vtt
- Played through the whole game after each big milestone; "we make something, play test it, talk about it, and then we
  change it". Same source.
- Pair tests with staff watching; finishing a section "without communicating with each other once" is a design
  failure. https://80.lv/articles/hazelight-studios-working-culture-and-approach-to-creating-collaborative-gameplay
- Testers do not steer the vision. https://www.gameinformer.com/feature/2021/02/12/it-takes-twos-creator-on-love-endings-and-a-1000-pledge

### Design
- Asymmetric abilities per chapter, one sets up and the other cashes in (nails and hammer, sap and match, time rewind and
  clone). Hard part: making both "feel equally fun"; fix example: enemies more aggressive toward the less busy player.
  https://80.lv/articles/hazelight-studios-working-culture-and-approach-to-creating-collaborative-gameplay ; https://hone.gg/blog/it-takes-two-chapters-list/
- Abilities come from the story (the time / clone pair from an argument). https://screenrant.com/it-takes-two-co-op-gameplay-changes-levels/
- Systemic abilities broke puzzles and needed rework. https://amara.org/subtitles/tUIr67mipkE6/en/1/download/It%20Takes%20Two.en.vtt
- No role swap mid-session; listed as a drawback. https://www.well-played.com.au/it-takes-two-review/
- Three co-op types: parallel, step-by-step, simultaneous. https://amara.org/subtitles/tUIr67mipkE6/en/1/download/It%20Takes%20Two.en.vtt
- A strong scene is shown once. https://80.lv/articles/it-takes-two-creator-shared-how-filmmaking-helped-the-game-mechanics
- 25 optional competitive minigames off the main path. https://www.powerpyx.com/it-takes-two-all-minigame-locations/
- Split screen even online. https://primagames.com/gaming/it-takes-two-quick-tips ; narrow FOV and motion sickness
  complaints: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1426210/discussions/0/3077621289882944908
- Fares: events cannot be triggered by location because you do not know where the other player is; two renders limit
  art. https://www.theringer.com/2021/4/22/22396763/josef-fares-it-takes-two-interview-co-op-gaming
- Failure: instant respawn, mash to revive faster, checkpoint only when both die, no lives.
  https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2021/03/24/it-takes-two-review/ ; shared health rejected; "It's fun to die... but it's
  not fun to redo it." https://amara.org/subtitles/tUIr67mipkE6/en/1/download/It%20Takes%20Two.en.vtt
- People communicate badly; puzzles that need both and no backtracking keep them together.
  https://80.lv/articles/hazelight-studios-working-culture-and-approach-to-creating-collaborative-gameplay
- "Whatever happens in the story is something you should be playing." https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/it-takes-two-lovingly-marries-story-and-gameplay-together

### Friend's Pass and sales
- Owner hosts, one friend joins free, EA account required, launch price US$39.99.
  https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201210006222/en/EA-and-Hazelight-Announce-It-Takes-Two-Coming-to-Consoles-and-PC-on-March-26-2021
- 1M (Apr 2021), 5M (Feb 2022), 10M (Feb 2023), 20M+ (Oct 2024), 30M+ (Apr 2026).
  https://opencritic.com/news/29011/it-takes-two-crosses-30m-sales-split-fiction-now-over-7m-techraptor
- Growth through word of mouth, TGA win, streamers, little marketing. https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/43403-it-takes-two-devs-share-insight-into-how-the-game-reached-20-million-sold

### Reception
- Metacritic 88-89 (Switch 83); OpenCritic 88, 93% recommend; TGA and DICE Game of the Year.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_Takes_Two_(video_game) ; https://opencritic.com/game/10974/it-takes-two
- Praise: "some of the best co-op gameplay in years"; controls stay consistent across genre changes.
  https://opencritic.com/game/10974/it-takes-two ; https://gamerant.com/takes-two-review-roundup/
- Complaints: writing weaker than gameplay; unlikeable leads too long; the comic helper character irritating; padded second half; tonal

  https://gamerant.com/takes-two-review-roundup/ ; https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2021/03/24/it-takes-two-review/ ;
  https://www.tomsguide.com/reviews/it-takes-two ; https://www.gamegrin.com/articles/why-i-think-it-takes-two-is-overrated
- **Music:** Kristofer Eng and Gustaf Grefberg; each level scored like a new game with its own instrument rules; boss
  music left open for dialogue. https://asoundeffect.com/it-takes-two-game-audio-score/ ; reviews called it "a bouncy
  score that gets the job done"; most reviews do not mention it; no music or audio nominations at BAFTA (8 noms) or TGA.
  https://www.tomsguide.com/reviews/it-takes-two ; https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/returnal-and-it-takes-two-lead-2022-bafta-games-nominations
  [Inference] no recurring theme across levels, so little sticks.

## B. A Way Out (2018) and Split Fiction (2025)

### A Way Out
- Co-op only and split screen only "from day one". https://gamingbolt.com/an-interview-with-josef-fares-the-creator-of-a-way-out-i-always-knew-we-had-something-special
- Split resizes; a partner's story scene can take about 80% of the screen while the other keeps control.
  https://whatculture.com/gaming/a-way-out-could-be-the-split-screen-revolution-we-39-ve-been-waiting-for
- One plays while the other is in a cutscene. https://www.pcgamer.com/a-way-outs-co-op-only-split-screen-only-concept-is-risky-yet-very-cool
- One character's way (overt) vs the other's (strategic); both must agree; no middle way.
  https://epiloguegaming.com/collaboration-cooperation-and-contention-in-a-way-out/ ; choices change the gameplay
  scenario, not the story. https://gamingbolt.com/an-interview-with-josef-fares-the-creator-of-a-way-out-i-always-knew-we-had-something-special
- Distract the guards while the partner digs, then swap. https://epiloguegaming.com/collaboration-cooperation-and-contention-in-a-way-out/
- Lookout while the other uses the stolen tool; sneaking is light. https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/a-way-out-review
- Synced climb: fall out of step and both fall. https://epiloguegaming.com/collaboration-cooperation-and-contention-in-a-way-out/
- Gas station: one disables the phone, the other subdues customers; poor coordination lets a customer call police.
  https://gameinformer.com/games/a_way_out/b/playstation4/archive/2017/06/13/the-uncompromising-vision-of-co-op-title-a-way-out
- 37 chapters in 5 acts, 6-8 hours. https://www.gamerevolution.com/guides/378215-a-way-out-how-many-chapters-how-long
- Ending turns the players against each other; it lands because of the bond formed.
  https://epiloguegaming.com/collaboration-cooperation-and-contention-in-a-way-out/
- Sales: 1M in two weeks, 13M by April 2026; US$29.99; Friend's Pass started here. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Way_Out ;
  https://gematsu.com/2017/12/way-launches-march-23-friends-pass-free-trial-announced
- Reception: Metacritic 78-79; BAFTA Multiplayer. Complaints: QTE-heavy, simple button presses, weak late shooting,
  archetype characters, choices without consequence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Way_Out ;
  https://gameinformer.com/games/a_way_out/b/playstation4/archive/2018/03/22/game-informer-a-way-out-review ;
  https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/a-way-out-review
- Music: Sam Hulick and Gustaf Grefberg; little critical coverage. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Way_Out

### Split Fiction
- 8 chapters plus 12 side stories; main game about 11-14 hours. https://www.nme.com/guides/gaming-guides/split-fiction-length-chapters-3844126
- "Introducing new mechanics every 15 minutes." https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/03/05/split-fiction-josef-fares-interview/
- Metacritic 90-93; 7M sold by April 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_Fiction
- Complaints matching "tedious, one-hit ideas":
  - "never gave its constantly shifting mechanics room to breathe", "relentless barrage of stuff", repeated boss fights,
    villain "a cartoon". https://www.avclub.com/split-fiction-review
  - "two dull, broadly-written characters", "always fairly obvious what you need to do". https://www.pcgamer.com/games/adventure/split-fiction-review
  - Bosses "repeat X three times", juvenile writing. https://mp1st.com/reviews/split-fiction-review-split-decision
  - "quantity over quality with the mechanics", "We abandoned the game around 4 hours into it" (Reddit).
    https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1swxvhl/snapshots/2026-04-27T15%3A47%3A26.535906Z
  - Steam negatives: cliché story, unclear objectives, poor pacing, weak soundtrack, repetitive bosses, no difficulty
    toggle. https://vaporlens.app/app/2001120/split_fiction.md
- Story came after the mechanics: https://www.gamesradar.com/games/adventure/split-fiction-ups-the-ante-on-it-takes-twos-wacky-co-op-action-while-showing-why-human-creativity-still-matters
- "just because something was very expensive, it needs to be reused. But why?" https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/03/05/split-fiction-josef-fares-interview/
- Hazelight cuts roughly 20-30% of ideas; tutorials shortened to seconds. https://videogameschronicle.com/features/interviews/split-fiction-game-interview
- Music: 45 tracks, a sound per chapter; one shared music state for both players; stingers matched to the current chord.
  https://www.asoundeffect.com/splitfictiongameaudio/

### Why A Way Out worked where Split Fiction tired players [Inference, supported above]
- Grounded story with stakes and two contrasting characters; variety built on human actions (sneak, distract, climb,
  drive); short (6-8 h). Split Fiction swaps the core action every few minutes at twice the length with weaker writing,
  so there is no mastery and no drama to carry it.

## D. Engine, Steam and Friend's Pass

### Wrapper
- Tauri uses WebView2 on Windows and WebKitGTK on Linux; documented Linux WebGL problems.
  https://v2.tauri.app/develop/debug/linux-graphics/ ; Steam Overlay does not work over WebView2 without workarounds.
  https://www.construct.net/en/blogs/ashleys-blog-2/trying-show-steam-overlay-1861
- Electron and NW.js bundle Chromium; overlay works with `--in-process-gpu`. Same source.
- Overlay flags `--in-process-gpu --disable-direct-composition`; overlay can freeze if the page stops redrawing.
  https://github.com/JamesMoulang/electron-steam-notes
- Windows 11 Alt+Tab ghost windows unresolved as of Oct 2026. https://github.com/ceifa/steamworks.js/issues/95
- Init Steam before `app.whenReady()`; hardware acceleration must stay on. https://github.com/ceifa/steamworks.js/issues/102
- Babylon.js games on Steam via Electron exist but are small (Tuggowar, Crystalline Bliss).
  https://forum.babylonjs.com/t/tuggowar-now-on-steam-with-new-look/48990 ;
  https://dev.to/lucasdamianjohnson/i-finally-published-my-first-game-to-steam-using-electron-my-own-voxel-engine-2ikc
- Web stack rules out consoles in practice (CrossCode ports needed an interpreter).
  https://www.rpgsite.net/news/9249-deck13-and-radical-fish-games-provide-an-update-on-the-console-versions-of-crosscode
- Babylon in Chrome on Steam Deck reported much slower (2024). https://forum.babylonjs.com/t/steam-deck-linux-and-chrome-performance/47555

### Steamworks bindings
- steamworks.js: achievements, cloud, ownership, auth, Steam Input, rich presence, lobbies, invite dialog, overlay; but
  networking only via the old ISteamNetworking P2P calls. https://github.com/ceifa/steamworks.js ; Valve marks
  ISteamNetworking deprecated. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/multiplayer/networking
- Maintenance reportedly stalled [verify]. https://dev.to/arty_prof/steamworks-ffi-node-a-steamworks-sdk-library-for-javascript-game-frameworks-15h1

### Steam networking
- Networking Messages / Sockets relay over Valve's backbone and hide IPs; free since 2019; NAT traversal handled.
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/multiplayer/steamdatagramrelay

  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/multiplayer/matchmaking

## E. Music and sound

### Chaos Theory and the series
- Amon Tobin built the score from field recordings and sound design. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amon_Tobin
- 6-8 minute tracks split into four independent stress layers triggered by what the character does.
  https://gamespot.com/articles/qanda-chaos-theory-composer-amon-tobin/1100-6117182/
- In game: ambience or silence exploring, bass on suspicion, full beats on alert, settling after evasion.
  https://patrick-kulikowski.squarespace.com/liner-notes/2015/5/30/liner-notes-xix-the-paranoiac-beats-of-splinter-cell-chaos-theory
- Earlier: Plowman's state-named tracks (Splinter Cell); Lalo Schifrin theme (Pandora Tomorrow). Conviction used three
  stemmed layers. https://splintercell.fandom.com/wiki/Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_(OST) ; https://www.mixonline.com/?p=76712

### Other models
- Mark of the Ninja: ambient base; high energy when spotted; "drops a notch" while searching; back up if found; fade
  when the search ends; avoid flip-flopping. https://designingsound.org/2013/06/the-dynamics-of-mark-of-the-ninja
- Thief: sparse mix so you can hear what is going on. https://pcgamer.com/stealth-is-everywhere-in-games-but-the-innovations-of-thief-have-been-forgotten
- Deus Ex HR: ambient / tension / combat cues per situation, minimal melody to avoid repetition.
  https://www.gamespot.com/articles/sound-byte-meet-the-composer-of-deus-ex-human-revolution/1100-6330367/
- Hitman (Jesper Kyd): modular tracks; against short repeating loops. https://www.alexanderpublishing.com/samples/soniccontrol/Jesper-Kyd-Talks-About-Hitman_06-15-2004.pdf
- Techniques: vertical layering and horizontal re-sequencing (Winifred Phillips, GDC 2021).
  https://www.gamedeveloper.com/blogs/pure-vertical-layering-for-game-music-composers-from-spyder-to-sackboy-gdc-2021-

### Implementation
- Raw Web Audio: sample-accurate loops, stems started at the same time into gain nodes, bar-synced switches.
  Howler.js for sound effects. Tone.js if a musical clock is wanted. https://github.com/goldfire/howler.js ;
  https://tonejs.github.io/docs/14.7.77/Transport
- FMOD has an HTML5 build; Wwise free under US$250k budget; check current terms [verify].
  https://fmod.com/docs/2.03/api/platforms-html5.html ; https://www.audiokinetic.com/en/blog/free-wwise-indie-license
- Ogg in Electron; add AAC for Safari in the browser build; avoid MP3 for loops. https://github.com/Fyrd/caniuse/issues/6805
- Memory: decoded 48 kHz stereo is about 23 MB per minute per stem; keep loops 1-2 min, load per level [Inference].

### Sound design for co-op stealth
- Players map patrols from guard sounds; hard floors cause a "Pavlovian cringe". https://pcgamer.com/stealth-is-everywhere-in-games-but-the-innovations-of-thief-have-been-forgotten
- Symmetrical propagation (if you hear them through a wall, they hear you); distinct foley per playable character.
  https://www.asoundeffect.com/dishonored-2-sound/
- Guards louder than the player; a voice performance per alert state. https://designingsound.org/2013/06/the-dynamics-of-mark-of-the-ninja

### Music brief template (the spec for the generator)
- Project: 2-player co-op third-person stealth, PC / Steam, night-city techno-thriller.
- Identity: one sentence (for example "night-city breakbeat noir: found-sound percussion, bass, sparse electronics"),
  3 reference tracks and what to take from each, one recurring main theme.
- Do not: generic orchestral tension, constant music, melodies that tire on loop.
- States: Menu (through-composed), Infiltration (sparse, near-silent option), Caution (bass and pulse), Alert (full
  percussion); switches on the next bar; Alert-to-Caution tail of 1-2 bars or under 10 s.
- Delivery: 48 kHz WAV masters plus Ogg; 4 stems per state; sample-accurate loop points; tempo and key sheet; stingers
  in key; room left for radio and banter.
