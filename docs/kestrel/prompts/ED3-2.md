### Decisions
| # | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Hub** (Menu on the pad; H on the keyboard opens it on Help): a full-screen pause-style overlay (`#hub`) with a tab row Play, File, Checks, Levels, View, Settings, Help. LB / RB change tabs (wrapping), D-pad up / down picks a row, left / right changes a value, A does it, B or Menu closes. Mouse: click tabs and rows; Esc closes. Text at least 20 px at 1080p. While open, nothing else reads the pad; any drag is cancelled on open. Hub rows and their words come from one pure table (`src/editor/hub.ts`, Babylon-free) so the e2e and Vitest can read them. |
| 2 | **Play tab**: Quick walk at the dot (closes the hub, same as L3); Walk test as the player, shown greyed with "Comes in the next part" (ED2 Part B). |
| 3 | **File tab**: Save (with the unsaved count on the row), Revert to last save (asks first, existing), and the change list newest first (the undo history, `model.past`). A on a line asks "Undo back to here?" with the in-editor yes / no dialog, then undoes every edit down to and including that line. |
| 4 | **Checks tab**: the problem lines (existing checks text), new ones marked; A on a line closes the hub and goes there (`goToProblem`); a "Next problem" row goes to the next line after the last one visited. |
| 5 | **Levels tab**: the editing level list (replaces the wheel's Level ring; A edits that level), then everything the Levels panel has today (presets, picked levels, ghost, exploded and gap, cut height, building filter). Reuse `renderPanel`'s builders rather than copy them. |
| 6 | **View tab**: layout 3D / Both / Plan, plan labels on / off, small plan on / off (3D only), level below on plan on / off (review 4.5; it leaves the Levels tab). Only rows that already exist as actions; floating names come in D rest. |
| 7 | **Settings tab**: rumble on / off, stick speed (0.5 to 2.0, step 0.25, default 1.0; scales pan and orbit), invert tilt, text size (Normal / Large, Large = 1.25x on the pad screen, hub and wheels). Saved with the existing pad preferences (`PREFS` key; view preferences only). |
| 8 | **Help tab**: a picture of the controller (inline SVG outline, Xbox or PlayStation names by `style`) with every button labelled with its building-state action, generated from `actions.ts`; under it the full table by group (existing `helpRows`). The old help overlay's pad row (Save, Revert, Walk, rumble) and `padHelp` go; the help overlay for keyboard and mouse is the hub's Help tab. |
| 9 | **Y wheel becomes the Build wheel**: Select, Room, Wall, Door > (the door kinds), Window, Measure, Paste and Paste to level above (only with something copied); Stair, Ladder, Floor hole and Object stay hidden until those tools exist. Edit, Undo, Walk, View, Level and File leave the wheel (Undo is X; Walk, View, Level and File are in the hub; Edit is the Selection wheel). Hold Space stays the mouse Build wheel. |
| 10 | **Selection wheel**: RB held (`rbTap === 'hold'`) with something selected opens it; with nothing selected RB held shows "Select something first" and nothing opens. It shows only what fits the selection: Move (grab it, as A in the middle), Properties (the side panel, as today), Duplicate, Rotate 90 (rooms, walls, stairs, ladders), Copy, Copy to level above, Change type >, Delete. Split joins in ED3-3 (hidden entry now). Same `Radial` rules as the Build wheel (point with either stick, rest opens a submenu, release picks); RB released closes it. Mouse: hold R. |
| 11 | **Change type >**: door kinds for doors and windows (the opening's `type`), room kinds for rooms, wall kinds for walls; one undoable edit. `ROOM_KINDS` in `ops.ts` follows schema.md line 35 (adds `room`, `stair`, `plant` to the six it has); the Properties list uses the same constant. |
| 12 | **Copy to level above**: copy plus paste to the level above in one step and one undoable edit, selecting the copy and moving the editing level up with it. At the top level it says "No level above" and does nothing. |
| 13 | **Edge-aware grab** (pure, `src/editor/grab.ts`): given the selected element and the dot, return the part A would take and its words. Rooms, stairs and floor holes: within the edge band of an edge (inside or just outside the rectangle) stretch that edge (handles W, E, S, N, words "Stretch east edge" etc.; the nearer edge wins at a corner), else "Move". Walls: within the band of an end, "Stretch this end" (handle a or b), else "Move". Doors and windows: within the band of a side, "Widen" (handle l or r), else "Slide along the wall". Outside the element plus band, null (A selects what the dot is on, as today). With the pad, `primary()` uses this instead of picking orange handles; the mouse keeps the handles. |
| 14 | **Grab preview**: before A, the part A would take lights up in 3D and on the plan (the edge, the wall end, the opening side, or the whole outline for Move), and the dot's text and the hint bar say the words from decision 13. Meshes are made once and moved; nothing is allocated per frame when the part has not changed. |
| 15 | **Magnetic dot** (pure snap search in `grab.ts` or a new `magnet.ts`): on the editing level, the dot pulls onto wall ends, room corners and opening centres (points), then wall centre lines (the nearest point on the line, grid-snapped along it); points beat lines. Inside the pull radius the orbit target eases onto the snap point while the left stick is under half deflection, and pan speed drops inside the radius; leaving needs the release radius. `point()` returns the snap point exactly while snapped. While dragging, the dragged element's own points are skipped. LB held turns it off (grid only). |
| 16 | **Snap feedback**: the dot pulses once and the pad rumbles once (`rumble('snap')`) when the dot snaps to a new point or line; nothing repeats while it stays snapped. The dot text names what it snapped to ("Wall end", "Room corner", "Door centre", "On wall"). |
| 17 | **Ghost previews in 3D** for every tool before A: Room and Wall show a start marker at the dot with the words "A: start a room here" / "A: start a wall here" (after the first A the existing drawn preview carries on, with its live size by the dot); Door and Window show a see-through ghost of the opening, at its default size, on the nearest wall within 1.2 m (the same search and snapping as `toolDown`), with its width and "A: place <kind> on <wall name>"; with no wall in reach the dot turns red and says "Move onto a wall". Measure shows its line and length from the first point. Ghost meshes are made once and reused. |
| 18 | **Ghosts on the plan**: when the plan pane is shown, the same ghost is drawn there as a dashed outline (opening on its wall, start marker). Mouse use of the plan is unchanged. |
| 19 | **RB tap while grabbing** rotates the grabbed room, stair, ladder or wall 90 degrees about the dot (preview only; the drop commits move and turn as one edit; B puts it back as it was). Openings and floor holes ignore it. |
| 20 | **Action map, hint bar, editor.md, tests**: every binding above goes into `actions.ts` (`menu` gains the hub context or the hub has its own hint row; `RB hold` in `selected`; `RB` in `drag` is rotate; `KeyR` hold for the mouse Selection wheel); remove the wheel-only actions that left; `conflicts()` stays empty. editor.md: rewrite the Controller section (buttons, both wheels, hub tabs, grab words, magnet) and remove help-as-a-menu. Vitest for `hub.ts`, `grab.ts` (every part and word, band at small sizes, null outside) and the magnet search (points beat lines, release radius, own points skipped, LB off), and the action map; e2e in section 4. |

### Constants (one table at the top of `grab.ts` / `magnet.ts`; Michael tunes after his pad test)
| Constant | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edge band, rooms and walls | min(0.5 m, a quarter of the shorter side or of the wall length) | small rooms keep a middle to move by |
| Edge band, openings | min(0.25 m, a quarter of the opening width) | a 0.9 m door keeps a middle |
| Pull radius / release radius | 0.5 m / 0.7 m | review 4.4 "within about half a metre"; the gap stops flicker |
| Pull ease | 12 per second (exponential) while the stick is under 0.5 | settles in about 0.2 s |
| Pan inside the radius | 0.6 x normal | review 4.4 "slows a little near them" |
| Opening ghost reach | 1.2 m | `toolDown`'s `nearestWall` reach (main.ts, the door branch) |
| Ghost look | 35 % opaque; red when it cannot be placed | readable over walls |
