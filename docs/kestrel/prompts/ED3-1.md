## 2. What to build
The scheme is section 4.1 of the review. ED3-1 builds the camera, the button map, the tool bar and the card. The
hub, the Selection wheel, edge-aware grab, the magnetic dot and ghost previews are ED3-2: leave the Y wheel's View,

### Decisions
| # | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Builder (orbit) camera** for the pad. A new Babylon-free module `src/editor/orbit.ts`: state `{ tx, tz, floorY, dist, yaw, pitch }`; pure functions for the camera position from that state, pan by stick (camera-relative), orbit, tilt, zoom, and clamps. `CameraControls` gains a mode `fly` / `walk` / `orbit`; orbit sets the camera from `orbit.ts` each frame. |
| 2 | **Entering orbit** (first pad input, or leaving a quick walk): target = the point under the screen centre on the editing floor (`view.planePoint`); if the ray misses or is over 140 m away, the point 25 m ahead along the camera's yaw. `dist` = the camera's distance to that point, clamped. Yaw and pitch keep the current view so nothing jumps. |
| 3 | **Leaving orbit** (mouse or key input): the camera stays exactly where it is and the fly camera takes over from that pose. No jump either way. |
| 4 | **Pad mapping in orbit** (constants table below): left stick pans the target across the editing floor, relative to camera yaw, speed scaled by `dist`; right stick x orbits, y tilts; LT / RT zoom out / in (squared, as the current triggers). L3 no longer toggles fast. |
| 5 | **The dot is the target.** In orbit, `PadControl.point()` returns the snapped orbit target (no screen ray), so the dot is always on the editing floor in every layout, including Plan only. Grabs and drawing follow it as now; D-pad nudges while grabbing stay. |
| 6 | **Level change follows**: `setLevel` moves the orbit target's `floorY` to the new level's view floor, eased over 0.25 s (exploded view offsets included, via `viewFloor`). |
| 7 | **View tap: top-down swap.** Eases pitch to the top-down limit and yaw to north-up over 0.3 s; tapping again restores the previous yaw and pitch. Moving the right stick while top-down just orbits / tilts from there (the swap forgets the saved view). The status strip shows "Top-down" while in it. |
| 8 | **View hold: layout** cycles 3D, Both, Plan (`setLayout`). Remove `cycleFocus` and View-hold pane focus. Panels opened from the wheel (Levels panel, Properties, Checks, Changes) still take pad focus and B still leaves them; only the cycling goes. |
| 9 | **Plan follows the dot.** Remove the pad plan crosshair mode (`planFrame`, `planPoint`, the `#cross` element's pad use). With the pad, the plan pane draws a marker at the orbit target and pans to keep it in view (the pane, not the data, moves); in Plan only, LT / RT zoom the plan instead of the 3D camera. Mouse use of the plan is unchanged. |
| 10 | **D-pad left / right: tool bar** (wraps at the ends). Remove the D-pad "previous / next near the dot" (`near`). D-pad up / down stays level up / down; while grabbing or drawing the D-pad still nudges. |
| 11 | **Tool bar** (pad screen only, bottom centre, above the button prompts): Select, Room, Wall, Door, Window, Measure, as large icons or short words with the current one lit; the Door slot shows the current door type under it. Stair, Ladder, Floor hole and Object slots appear only when those tools exist (Parts E, F). Picking a tool from the Y wheel lights it on the bar; Y tap (last tool) is unchanged. Measure on the bar starts the existing measure (A, A). |
| 12 | **RB tap: go to the selection**: moves the orbit target to the selection's centre on its level (eased 0.3 s), keeping yaw, pitch and zoom. R3 no longer looks at the selection. |
| 13 | **R3: level display** cycles Up to this level, Single level, Whole building (the existing presets; ghost and exploded stay as set). The status strip shows the result. |
| 14 | **L3: quick walk** at the dot: `walkAt` on the editing level at the orbit target, facing the camera's yaw; left stick walks, right stick looks (existing walk code). L3 again (or B) returns to orbit exactly as it was before. Tab stays the keyboard's walk. |
| 15 | **LB held: precision**: 0.1 m grid (as now) and pan, orbit and zoom at 30 % speed. |
| 16 | **Selection card** (pad screen only, bottom right, read-only, at least 20 px text at 1080p): the selected element's name and kind, its size (room width x depth, wall length and thickness, opening type and width), and its level name. Uses the existing `label()` and fields; hidden with no selection. |
| 17 | **Action map, hint bar, help, editor.md**: every pad binding above goes into `src/editor/actions.ts` (`pad` and `ctx`); remove `near`, `paneFocus`, `fly` (pad part), `fast` (pad part), `lookSel`; add `zoom`, `orbit`, `topDown`, `toolPrev/Next`, `display`, `quickWalk`, `precision`. `conflicts()` stays empty. Keyboard and mouse bindings do not change. editor.md: rewrite the Controller table and the plan-with-pad and side-panel lines to match. |
| 18 | **Tests**: Vitest for `orbit.ts` (position from state, pan relative to yaw, clamps, entering from a fly pose keeps the view, top-down swap and restore) and the action map; `scripts/e2e-editor.mjs` updated and extended (section 4). |

### Constants (editor defaults; one table at the top of `orbit.ts`; Michael tunes after his pad test)
| Constant | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zoom distance | 4 to 140 m, start 25 m | 25 m is the current `overlook` default; 140 m sees the 110 x 80 m site whole (RULES 2) |
| Tilt (pitch) | 0.35 to 1.55 rad (about 20 to 89 degrees) | 1.55 is the current pitch clamp in `controls.ts` |
| Pan speed | 0.9 x `dist` m/s at full stick | crosses the view in about a second at any zoom |
| Orbit rate | 2.4 rad/s at full stick, squared | `LOOK_RATE` in `controls.ts` |
| Zoom rate | dist x 1.5 per second at full trigger, squared | even feel near and far |
| Eases | level 0.25 s, top-down 0.3 s, go to selection 0.3 s | short enough not to wait on |
