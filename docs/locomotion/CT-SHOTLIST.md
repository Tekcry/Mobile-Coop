# Chaos Theory reference shot list (M19)

Michael captures these from Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory so the movement can be matched from footage instead of guesses.
Research could not find CT timings, camera distances or Sam's wall pose in any source (docs/locomotion/research/).

## How to capture
- Video beats screenshots: most items are motion. Record short clips (3-10 s each) at 60 fps if possible (PC capture
  with OBS / Xbox Game Bar, or the console's capture). A still is enough only where marked STILL.
- Keep the default camera and FOV; do not move the camera during a clip unless the item says so.
- Stand next to something of known size where possible (a standard door, about 2.0 m high, 0.9 m wide) so distances
  can be measured.
- For body poses, swing the camera to the side view (90 deg to Sam) before starting; do one front view where marked.
- Name files `NN-short-name.mp4` / `.png` with the numbers below. Put them in a folder on the desktop:
  `C:\NightShift\Mobile-Coop-locomotion\pc\ct-ref\` (pc/ is gitignored, the files stay local).
- Training level or the first mission (Lighthouse) is fine for most; a lit corridor helps the pose items.

## A. Speed and walking (pad, side view unless noted)
1. Stand still 5 s, weapon holstered: idle posture (side, then front). STILL x2 + 5 s clip.
2. Same with the weapon drawn (SC-20K): idle posture (side, front). STILL x2.
3. Walk past the camera at each speed step from slowest to fastest, 3 s each, holstered (side view). One clip.
4. Same at each speed, crouched. One clip.
5. Same, weapon drawn, standing and crouched. Two clips.
6. Full stick from standing still to top speed, 3 s (side). Clip.
7. Release the stick at a slow walk; then at a crouch walk; then at full run (side). Three clips. (Stop behaviour.)
8. Start again immediately after each stop (does he resume the frozen step?). Clip.

## B. Turning
9. Standing still, turn the stick slowly round 360 deg (top-down if the camera allows, else behind). Clip.
10. Standing still, flick the stick 180 deg behind. Clip.
11. Standing still, spin the stick in circles fast for 5 s (watch the legs). Clip.
12. Walking: gentle curve left then right. Clip.
13. Running: 90 deg turn, then a full reversal. Clip (side or behind).
14. Crouched: turn in place and a 180. Clip.

## C. Posture details (STILL, side and front)
15. Standing holstered, standing drawn, crouched holstered, crouched drawn: four side STILLs + four front STILLs.
16. Mid-stride at walk, at run, at crouch walk: pause the game mid-step if possible (side). STILL x3.

## D. Crouch, roll, jump, land
17. Stand to crouch and back, standing still and while walking. Clip.
18. Roll from a run (side). Clip.
19. Standing jump; running jump (side). Clip.
20. Drop from about 2 m and about 4 m; with crouch held and without. Clips.

## E. Camera
21. Walk forward 5 s then stop, without touching the camera: how the camera follows and settles. Clip.
22. Raise the weapon to aim (SC-20K and pistol): the transition into the aim view and back. Clip each.
23. Aim view STILL (rifle, pistol), with Sam standing 3 m from a door so distance can be measured.
24. Switch shoulder while aiming (rifle, then pistol): watch the gun and hands. Clip each, slow if possible.
25. Sniper / scope view and hold breath. Clip.

## F. Back to wall and corners
26. Put Sam's back to a wall, standing: side STILL, front STILL, from above if possible.
27. Same crouched: side and front STILLs.
28. Slide along the wall both ways, standing and crouched. Clip.
29. Slide to the end of the wall at an outside corner and keep pushing. Clip.
30. Peek round the corner from the wall (standing, crouched): body, head and camera. Clip.
31. Draw and aim while at the wall edge: what happens (lean out? shoulder switch?). Clip.
32. Crouch behind a waist-high object (crate) and aim over it: how high he rises. Clip + STILL.
33. Aim round the side of the waist-high object at its end. Clip.

## G. Doors
34. Open a door slowly with the stick, then close it slowly. Clip.
35. Open a door at normal speed and walk through. Clip.
36. Optic cable under a door. Clip.
37. Bash a door (with a guard behind if possible). Clip.
38. Lockpick a door. Clip.

## H. Close combat
39. Grab a guard from behind, walk with him, knock out. Clip.
40. Run up and strike a guard from the front (palm / melee). Clip; also press attack with no guard near.
41. Knife attack (lethal) from behind and from the front. Clips.
42. Pick up and carry a body; drop it; throw it over a railing if possible. Clip.
43. Whistle to lure a guard. Clip.

45. Ledge hang, shimmy, pull up, drop.
46. Ladder up and down; drainpipe up and down.
47. Horizontal pipe: hands, legs up, inverted, aim inverted.
48. Split jump: enter, aim, drop.
49. Wall jump / double jump.
50. Zipline (and tuck feet).
51. Rappel down, swing, aim.
52. Vent / crawl space enter and exit.

## J. Vision
54. Thermal vision in a dark room with a guard near and far: STILL near, STILL far, short clip panning.
55. Night vision and electromagnetic (EMF) vision, same: STILLs.

## K. Co-op (only if a second player is available)
