### Conforms
- G44 base rule with lookouts on top: pitch is measured from the guard's own eye (`src/ai/enemy.ts:2428-2429`; calm guards only, `:2458`). A raised lookout therefore sees his own level fully, while players above a ground guard keep the G44 penalty. No special case is needed - `src/ai/perception.ts:56-64,159-163`.
- Missing partner triggers an investigation: a `missing` incident at the partner's last route point plus a radio call - `src/ai/enemyManager.ts:2207-2222`.
- Paired searches kept (G180 over G11): lead plus a cover held `pairBack` 5 m short - `src/ai/incidents.ts:20,82,450`.
- Fixed patrols, no randomness (VISION rule 8, G143, bible P5 / 5.7): the patrol glance seed is deterministic (`src/ai/patrol.ts:72`); search points use a golden-angle bearing (`patrol.ts:213-219`); pairs use no RNG (`src/ai/pairs.ts:15`); combat uses a seeded mulberry32 (`src/ai/squadRoles.ts:649-689`, `src/ai/enemy.ts:346`); `searchArea`, `sweepTeam`, `response`, `incidents`, `alarm`, `hearing` and `radioNet` have no `Math.random`. The only live-mode `Math.random` is the think cadence: a 0.22-0.30 s sample jitter (`enemy.ts:389,2336`), while the meter integrates every frame (`enemy.ts:2568`). Detection stays explainable - `src/ai/enemy.ts:342-346`.
- Sector Ops cap 32 alive (G332, G187) - `src/game/sectors.ts:508`, applied at `src/game/modes/sectorMode.ts:317-318`.

- Marlow Yard cap (G187): `MAX_ALIVE` 12 and `SET_GUARDS_MAX` 3 - `src/ai/enemyManager.ts:83`, `src/world/maps/ctcourseSets/types.ts:191`.
