# R3 - Sector Ops section 3: co-op-only routes and the team door

Built in `src/world/maps/warehouseS3.ts` (`buildAssemblyS3`, block "(sector-fixes R3) co-op-only routes" after the services,
and the plant room's south wall), data in `S3_COOP` and `S3_BOLTS`. No lamps, lights, cameras or lasers touched. The lip band
is read from the constants: `WALL_JUMP.maxUp` 3.8 + 0.5 = 4.3 to `TEAM.boostMax` 4.5. Both lips are at 4.4.

The hall roof is at 6.03. It is drawn as visual panels plus collision-only skylight sheets (`warehouse.ts`). Any 4.3-4.5 top
under it therefore has 1.63 m of room. (e2e fix) A climb-up off a lip needs a standing body's room over the top
(`AttachController.climbRoom`, 1.75 m), so the first build (a crouch gallery under a collision copy of the roof) could not be
climbed onto: the roof over each platform is now cut open (`S3_ROOF_HOODS`, the panels in `warehouse.ts` and the sheets in
`warehouseDress.ts`) and closed by a raised hood (a roof-ventilator upstand, underside `S3_COOP.hoodUnder` 6.3: 1.9 m of
room). The climb-up still ends crouched (G4).

## Team door

The plant room's service door into the fire-exit lobby:

- A 1.2 m gap in the plant room's south wall (z -5.6, x 22.6-23.8) with a lintel. Hinge (22.65, -5.6), width 1.1, yaw PI/2,
  `swing: -1` (into the plant room's empty south-east corner), `locked: true, breachable: false`.
- `S3_BOLTS = [{ at: [23.2, -5.6], side: [0, -1] }]`: the lobby side unbolts it. RT already reads it in
  `warehouseSectors.ts` (`bolts: S3_BOLTS`).
- Reason: the exit lobby must never be a way into the plant, so the bolt is on the lobby side. The engineer uses the hall
  doors.
- Use: one player gets into the lobby and lets the partner through from the plant room. The way in is the fire door, or the
  plant room's own ladder (21.0, -2.0) to the plant roof, the lobby roof and the drop hatch (22.5, -7.2). The partner then
  goes straight into the extraction. This skips route 3's way out past the tour guard's lane (x 15) and the lobby fire door.
- Not on any guard route: at least 1.5 m from every post and 1.0 m from every route leg (the tour guard's lobby leg is about
  2.2 m south). None of the three routes passes through it. The fire door (unlocked) and the roof hatch stay the other ways
  in.
- Sight: the gap is east of every line between the engineer's and tour guard's spots. The M8 checks in `sectors.test.ts`
  pass unchanged.
