# Warehouse redesign - section 1 walk guide

Greybox prototype (no art), for Michael to judge the direction (M1, M5). Branch `feature/warehouse-redesign`; the
version label shows the build's commit (2dba40b or later). Sections 2 and 3 are still the old build; their redesign is
in `SPEC.md` 2.2 and 2.3.

**Menu:** Sector Ops > Warehouse (any difficulty). Solo or co-op (lobby: Sector Ops). Start: the yard's south-west
corner. Objective: download the camera recorder in the security office (hold A about 10 s). Exit: the fire lobby (C2)
to section 2.

**Three routes to try**
1. **Ground:** crouch under the part-raised west shutter, cross the goods-in bay, reception, the corridor, the security
   office; out by the corridor's west door to the lobby.
2. **High:** the drainpipe beside the skip (x -22.4) to the roof, the roof hatch (x 5) down to the plant deck, drop
   through the security office's ceiling hatch. Back up from the cabinet under the hatch (unverified: if the climb
   fails, the hatch is drop-only), or out on foot; west over the roof and down the smoke vent onto the lobby roof.
3. **Service:** the lane behind the trailer, the workshop door, the east ladder to the crane walkway, west into the
   office ceiling void (crawl), drop through the mess hatch onto the camera operator.

**Judge:** whether the building reads as a real warehouse (roofs on rooms, door and window sizes, walls with a reason);
whether the routes feel free-flowing; whether the 8 guards' jobs and timed gaps give clean, isolated takedowns; the
crawl under the shutter and into the void; light and shadow in the yard and bay (not checked on a GPU yet).

**Known open:** the cabinet climb back up into the deck hatch, the camera in the 1.3 m void openings (L7), and the lights
need a playtest. Loosenings L1-L15 and decisions A2-A7 are in `BRIEF.md` for review.
