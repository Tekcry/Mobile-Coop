## Where the JSON could not be built exactly, and what was done (for the D1 owner)
1. **Stair B (SB)**: the 20-riser stair with a 2.0 m landing needs 7.7 m of run, but the well block HWELL is 5.0 m. The stair top is set at the link end b (x 39.5), the foot at x 31.8, and the G slab hole is the well widened 0.45 m west and 0.50 m east so a standing player has 2.0 m under the slab. The rails stand on the widened hole.
2. **Trench TRN and culvert CUL (level T, y -1.4)**: the engine's duct verb enters at a wall vent, never a floor hatch. Built below ground as listed (floor, walls, 0.1 m grille plate), with a 1.4 x 1.0 m pit at each end of the link path (open to the yard or room, a 1.4 m drop), a wall vent at each pit's inner end and a ladder up each pit. The CUL path runs 1 m and 2 m past its T rect: the tunnel is the rect united with the path. The part of TRN's rect beyond its path is a dead-end stub.
3. **Hatch ladder H and roof ladder RLd** have link ends 2.3 - 2.5 m from the wall they climb: each ladder stands on the first wall along the line from its lower end (H: the tunnel's south wall, 1.3 m from the JSON point; RLd: the south facade).
4. **VDUCT**: the housing does not collide (it stands in the walkable Control room and corridor, where the design has free floor); it crosses the wall through a 1.2 m gap with a lintel at 1.3 m.
5. **Breaker box VBK1** (block x 29 .. 31, z 3 .. 4) stands inside the doorway AV1 (27.5 .. 30.5): the design's own overlap, built as designed.
6. **Window GDW overlaps the door GD** by 0.4 m (6.5 .. 8.5 against 8.1 .. 9.9): the door wins, as in the design's move and sight model.
7. **The lane** south of the yard has no space in the JSON, but the gates Gp and Gv open onto it: a floor strip from the footprint edge to the yard wall. Perimeter walls follow the footprint.
8. **The skylight slot** is a hole between the two roof decks; the parapets on both edges stand (a gap only for the beam). A fall lands on the Test room or Control room floor 3.3 m below.
9. Wall thickness is 0.1 m between two spaces (centred) and 0.3 m on the outside. At 0.2 m centred, 8 camera samples hit a wall (rule 27), because the design's walls have no thickness.
10. All doors are unlocked; locks, holds, circuits and alarms are not built (no objective logic in G1).
