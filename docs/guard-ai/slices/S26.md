# S26 Guards hear you in a fight and a search (CT rule 8) - brief (planner, 2026-10-23 ~22:15)

Michael (M1): "if they are within range they should hear it, depending on whether they are near something noisey";
"making them smarter, work together ... they should be smart and tactical". CT (research `ct-behaviour.md` section 0
rule 8, [C / 1], MM:477-482, R10:24-37): "Guards walk straight to a sound source; in a fight they aim at the last spot
the noise rose above ambient." Rule 9: searching guards move slowly and carefully. Light and shadow is the core, but
after contact is broken the player's noise is the other half of the stealth game.

## The gap (origin/feature/guard-ai-s24 96d565e5 = S24 src ef44eea5; re-grep every anchor on the S25 tip)
S1-S25 made calm guards hear by range, walls and masking. Once a guard is in combat or searching, he is deaf:
- `EnemyManager.hear` (`src/ai/enemyManager.ts:2520-2546`) skips every alerted guard (`e.alerted ... continue`). A
  player who broke line of sight can run, land, roll or fire a suppressed shot 3 m from a guard in combat: nothing.
- A searching guard gets `Enemy.hearTurn` (`src/ai/enemy.ts:937-951`): "Already investigating or searching: only the
  meter" (capped `HEARING.turnMax` 0.45, never enough to see), and he keeps walking his ring / plan point. Running
  behind a searcher in the dark is free.
- The intruder board (S24, `src/ai/intruders.ts`) is written only by sightings (`reportSighting`,
  `enemyManager.ts:548-552`) and located gunfire (`noise`, `:2456-2509`). No noise ever moves a player's last known
  position, and S25's probable area keeps growing from the last sighting (`areaRadius(seenT)`), so a footstep heard 1 s
  ago does not narrow the search.
- `hear(pos, reach, kind, skip)` does not say who made the noise (S24 added `by` to `noise` only), so a body noise
  cannot be tied to an intruder slot (G139).
Co-op: the client's footsteps and quiet shots reach `hear` (`src/net/coopHost.ts:1299`, `:1307`) like the host's, so
the gap is the same for both players.

## Goal / done looks like
- A searcher (after combat, or a calm body / device / pair search) who hears a footstep, landing, roll, fence rattle,
  noisy hold, takedown or suppressed shot inside his effective reach (walls and masking as S1) turns and walks to that
  spot next at his search pace, looks there, then goes on with his search. A crouch-walk (silent band, G40) is never
  heard; a sprint 5 m behind him in the open is (step reach ~7 m on concrete: `noiseRadius(5.0)` 6.96 x
  `SURFACE_NOISE`; a jog ~3.4 m).
- A guard in combat who cannot see his target and hears a player's body noise relocates that player's last known
  position to the noise (heard, not seen): his group's flow field, roles and the S25 plan follow the new spot.
- G139: the noise locates its maker only. A guard hunting A who hears B goes to look (searcher) or turns (combat), and
  takes B as his intruder only when what he knows of A is stale (8 s).
- Lures (bottle, noise maker, sticky-cam ping) still pull searchers (a CT distraction), never a guard in combat.
- Calm guards: unchanged (S1 tiers and the board). Parked modes (`coordinated` false): unchanged. Solo: the same
  rules on slot 0.

## Data shapes
```ts
// src/ai/hearing.ts (addition, pure)
/** (guard-ai S26) Hearing in a fight and a search. `tier`: the lowest hearing tier that counts (1 = anywhere inside the
 *  effective reach). `gap`: s between two re-aims of one guard. `reportGap` / `reportMove`: a heard report of one
 *  intruder at most this often (s) unless it moved this far (m). `switchAfter`: s since his own intruder was last
 *  seen or located before a heard other one takes over (G139). `searchLures`: lures pull searchers. `bark`: s between
 *  two "I heard something." of one guard. `enabled` false: today's deaf rule. */
export const COMBAT_HEAR = { enabled: true, tier: 1, gap: 1.5, reportGap: 1, reportMove: 1.5, switchAfter: 8,
  searchLures: true, bark: 6 } as const;
/** What a guard does with a noise: 0 nothing, 1 look (a searcher walks to it), 2 locate (report its maker's position
 *  as heard; a searcher also walks to it, a guard in combat turns his hunt to it). Pure, no allocation.
 *  state: 0 calm (not handled here), 1 searching, 2 combat. body: a player's own noise (`by` set). */
export function combatHearAct(state: 0 | 1 | 2, sees: boolean, tier: HearTier, body: boolean, lure: boolean): 0 | 1 | 2;
```
```ts
// src/ai/intruders.ts (addition)
/** Seconds since slot i was last seen OR located (a heard footstep, a located shot); 99 never. `report` resets it
 *  always (`seenT` only when `seen`). S25's probable area grows from it (A157). */
readonly locT = new Float32Array(MAX_INTRUDERS).fill(NEVER);
```
`Enemy` (new public reads): `searchSpot(out: P2): P2` (his current spot, tests / e2e), `inCombatSearch` (the private
`combatSearch`), `heardAt` (s since his last re-aim, 99 none). `EnemyManager.hear(pos, reach, kind = 'noise',
skip = null, by: string | null = null)`.

## Allocation and cost
`hear` runs per noise event (footsteps 4 Hz per moving player). New per event: the searching / alerted guards in reach
go through `tierOf` (a wall ray only where it decides the tier, as S1), skipped first when they see their target or
re-aimed within 1.5 s. No closures, no arrays, `hyp3`; per-slot timers are typed arrays made once. A heard report
moves a slot at most once per second, so the S24 flow rebuild (one per update at most, on a move) stays at today's
rate or below.

## Assumed decisions (proposed; the lead moves accepted ones into BRIEF.md)
- **A155** Guards in a search hear like anyone (S1 reach, walls, masking) and walk to what they hear (their next search
  point); guards in combat who cannot see their target relocate a player by his body noises (footsteps, landing, roll,
  rattle, holds, takedowns, suppressed shots). Undo: `COMBAT_HEAR.enabled = false` (`src/ai/hearing.ts`).
- **A156** In a fight or a search any audible noise counts (tier 1, the whole effective reach), not only the inner half
  calm guards are sent at: they are listening. Undo: `COMBAT_HEAR.tier = 2`.
- **A157** A heard position is "located", not "seen": it moves the intruder's last known position and restarts the
  probable-area clock (`locT`), but not `seenT` (the ghost's age, `forget`, `sightT`). Undo: `buildPlan` reads `seenT`.
- **A158** (G139) A noise locates only the player who made it; a guard switches his hunt to that player only when his own
  intruder was last seen or located 8 s ago or more. Undo: `COMBAT_HEAR.switchAfter = Infinity` (never switch).
- **A159** Lures and other non-body noises pull searchers (CT distraction), never a guard in combat, and never move an
  intruder's position. A sweeping response team (S13) reports what it hears but keeps its stack. Undo:
  `COMBAT_HEAR.searchLures = false`; the crew rule in `hear`.
- **A160** Throttles: one re-aim per guard per 1.5 s; one heard report per intruder per 1 s unless it moved 1.5 m; a heard
  centre more than 1 m from the plan's re-centres it at once (claims dropped). Undo: the `COMBAT_HEAR` numbers.
