# S7 - Cameras see bodies (integration, 2026-10-23)

Job guard-ai, slice S7 (design 9, goal 9, M6, G240, A5). The security-side body pass was already built and tested
(`S7-module.md`); this step wires it to the incident board and the guards. Branch `feature/guard-ai-s7`, not committed
(the job lead commits).

## Built
- **Wiring (GameState, host only).** Beside `new SecuritySystem(...)` (the `!this.puppet` block): `setBodySource(() =>
  bodies)` on the manager's kept `bodies` array (closure made once), `onBody` -> `EnemyManager.cameraReport(ev,
  "Camera N")` (N = the camera's place in the map's security list, 1-based), `onCameraLine` -> a radio bark
  (`hud.barks.say('cam:<id>', line, true)` + the `bark` event for the chirp). `placeBark` puts a `cam:` line over that
  camera's lens. `CtDevices` and `onEvent` untouched.
- **`EnemyManager.cameraReport`** (coordinated modes only; parked modes return -1): finds the body by id; found,
  carried, hidden, not present or gone -> ignored (`camIgnored`). Else `IncidentBoard.reportBody` on the floor under
  it, keyed by the body's number; a NEW incident gets the desk's line (`camBodyLine`, room = `RoomRect.name` under the
  body), then an immediate board pass. `camReports` / `camIgnored` counters for tests.
- **Board (`incidents.ts`).** `reportBody(x, z, y, key, lethal)`, `want[]` (1 KO, 2 dead), `MODE_BODY`. Merge: a
  `camBody` merges only with the open `camBody` of the same body (cross-camera dedupe; two bodies side by side are two
  incidents). Assign: calm free guards within `callR` 25 m (never dogs / melee, held, busy, tasked, investigating,
  searching, alerted), best by path (the S2 `best`: 3 nearest get a path query, officer 1.6, cooldown 0.8); a dead body
  sends the next best too. Nobody in range: retried every 5 Hz tick for `camWait` 30 s (a guard walking into range is
  sent), then closed. Follow / silent escalation (`missing` after 25 s) / close are the S2 ones.
- **Guard (`Enemy.checkBody`).** Straight to investigating (no 2.4 s look first), bark `checking`, walks at the
  investigate pace with the body as the spot, held by the task (`AlertInput.hold`) for up to `bodyWalk` 45 s. Within
  `BODY.close` 2.5 m and a clear line to the hips (0.35 slack, the guards' own body ray), `ctx.bodyFound(this, b)`:
  the existing body-found path (bark "Man down!", KO -> he wakes him, search; the squad within 22 m searches until S4).
  Ends early: body carried / hidden / gone / found by someone else (normal end of an investigation where he is), a
  glimpse (meter >= investigate: the task drops, he investigates the glimpse), or any level change.
- **Sector Ops.** `SectorMode.unloadSection` calls `g.security?.clearBodyReports()` (covers section loads and restarts).
- No wire change (host-only; clients see the guards through snapshots). Protocol untouched.

## Numbers

## Polish
1. A body carried off before the guard arrives and dropped again in the same camera's view is not reported again by
   that camera (the module reports once per camera per body).
2. The dead body's second guard walks to the same point as the first (no stand-off or cover position).
3. "Camera N" numbers count every camera of the map (Sector Ops: across sections), not per section.
4. No "Copy" from the sent guard to the desk beyond his "Checking it out."; the desk line has no `{room}` when the body
   lies outside every room rect.

## Chain fix (bodies), 2026-10-23
e2e-stealth-ai 'bodies' failed on the chain tip ae1f1895 ("the finder searches (unaware)") but passed on S4 alone, S7
alone and the S8 tip. Cause (test only): `EnemyManager.bodiesFound` is not reset between scenarios, and the loop stopped
on `bodiesFound > 0`; when 'radio window' left a find behind (guard b noticing one of its KO'd guards, random perceive
timing), the loop exited after 0.1 s, before w had looked. Fix: count from a baseline taken at the scenario start and
assert the finder is w (`onBodyFound` hook). No game change. Verified by the laptop run on the fixed commit.
