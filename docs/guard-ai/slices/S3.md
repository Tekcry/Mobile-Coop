## Built

  ticked at 10 Hz with the board's `GuardView` and a reaction lane (difficulty x officer factor, no random term).
- Voice: `AiContext.shout` in coordinated modes uses `hearsVoice` (12 m reach at the investigate tier: ~6 m open,
  ~2.7 m through one wall, less by noisy machines), one wall ray only where it decides; joins after `RADIO.voiceDelay`.
- Contact call: a guard who goes to combat on his own shouts, then `radioCall(-1, 'contact')`; on completion the
  section hears it nearest first (`staggerDelay`, cap 4.5 s x reaction), bark `joining`. No relay.
- Cuts: `Enemy.die` (kill, KO), `beginTakedown`, `blind`, `EnemyManager.despawn`; `clear` on `despawnAll` /
  `resetMemory`. A blinded or released guard still in combat calls again.
- Reports as calls: light check (`light`), pair lead (`missing`), a board task ending calm (`standDown`): one "Copy."
  from the first receiver.
- S2 polish 1 (full pool evicting the incident being escalated) and 2 (empty noise checks end calm, `calmEnd`; gunfire
  keeps the 45 s cooldown) fixed.
- `RADIO_ALARM` false: `command` calls (S6) do nothing yet. Parked modes (`coordinated = false`) keep the old window.
