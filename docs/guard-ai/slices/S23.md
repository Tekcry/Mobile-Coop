## The gap (from code, origin/train/31 e383ba16 = 4.80.0)
`Enemy.perceive` (`src/ai/enemy.ts:1864`) looks at **one** player: the nearest living one (`:1874-1885`), and casts its
chest / head / hips rays (`:1892-1921`), sight rate (`:1926-1936`), smell (`:1938-1946`), instant detection (`:1948`) and
combat sighting (`:1955`) only for him. A guard never sees the farther player:
- Partner A crouched behind a wall 4 m from a guard, partner B lit in the open 9 m in front of him: B is invisible.
- In combat, a guard shooting at B stops (`los` false) the moment A comes nearer behind cover.
- `EnemyManager.assignFlanker` (`src/ai/enemyManager.ts:2667`) measures every guard against `players()[0]` (the host).
- The host's awareness arcs (`src/game/gameState.ts:2584`) read `e.inSight`, which is "sees his target", so the host
  gets an arc for a guard who is filling up on the partner.
Cameras already do it right: one meter per player (`src/security/securitySystem.ts:228-238`). In solo nothing changes
(one player), so this is invisible to every solo test today; e2e-coop has no guard-perception block.

## Goal / done looks like
- A calm guard notices whichever player he can see best, not the nearest: with A hidden behind a wall nearer and B lit
  in front of him, his meter fills on B and he goes to combat on B (`target` = B).
- In combat he keeps shooting at the player he sees; a nearer hidden player does not blind him.
- Solo: identical behaviour and numbers (one player, the same rays, the same rates).
- The host's arcs show only guards that see the host's own player (or are within `ARC_NEAR`).

## Build
1. `src/ai/targetPick.ts` (new, pure, no Babylon), with:
```ts
/** Up to this many players are perceived (= MAX_PLAYERS in src/net/protocol.ts; src/ai must not import src/net). */
export const MAX_INTRUDERS = 4;
/** `all` false: the nearest player only (today). `sticky` false: in combat the best-seen player each think. */
export const TARGETS = { all: true, sticky: true } as const;
/** Could `sightRate` be above 0 for this player (no rays needed to say no)? Mirrors sightRate's two terms exactly:
 *  inside the field (fieldFactor(angle) > 0 and dist < its range) or felt close by (dist < closeRange, speed > 0.15).
 *  Alerted guards and dogs always pass (combat sight is the rays alone; dogs smell). */
export function mayBeSeen(dist: number, angle: number, speed: number, alerted: boolean, dog: boolean): boolean;
/** Index of the player to perceive as the target, -1 none. `n` players; `dist` Infinity = dead / skipped.
 *  Calm (not alerted): the highest `rate` (> 0); none: the nearest. Alerted: `cur` while `los[cur]`; else the nearest
 *  with `los`; else `hunt` (S24 passes his intruder's index; S23 passes -1) when alive; else `cur` when alive; else the
 *  nearest. Ties: the nearer, then the lower index. No allocation. */
export function pickTarget(n: number, alerted: boolean, cur: number, hunt: number, los: Uint8Array, rate: Float32Array,
  dist: Float32Array): number;
```

## Allocation and cost

`tmp`, `tmpHead`, `tmpHips`, `tmpEye`), `hyp2` / `hyp3` only, no `Math.hypot`. Rays: today 2-3 per think for one
player; worst case now 3 per live player, and `mayBeSeen` skips players a calm guard cannot see (behind him beyond 1.8
m, outside 25 m in focus / 12 m peripheral). Two players, 24 guards: at most ~2x today's perception rays, usually far
less. Perf is not measured (no rendering change).
