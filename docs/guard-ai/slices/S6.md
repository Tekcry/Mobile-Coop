# S6 - Alarm and reinforcements (implementer report, 2026-10-23)

Spec: BRIEF M4 ("After a raised alarm": a panel hit or a radio report that gets through; killing the caller first stops
it), M1 ("any reinforcements must be aggressive"), goal 6; PLAN row S6; design section 7; S3-CHECK item 8 (net
section). Branch `feature/guard-ai-s6` (chain tip S3+S4+S5+S7), nothing committed (the job lead commits).

## Built
- **Command call** (`CommandCall` in `src/ai/alarm.ts`, pure; `EnemyManager.command`). A `contact` call that gets
  through (`sink.completed`) owes the alarm (`contactThrough`): the officer in the caller's voice range (alive, free),
  else the caller, else the next guard in the fight starts a `command` call (`RADIO.report` 4 s x reaction; bark
  `command` "Command, contact. Send backup." at its start). On completion `radioAlarm` raises the alarm with no panel
  (`raiseAlarm(e, null)`, `alarmBy = 'radio'`). One at a time (the net refuses a second `command`).
- **Cut and hand-over.** The call is cut by the S3 paths (down, grab, blind, despawn). `CommandCall.tick` (after the
  net's tick, 10 Hz) sees the call gone without the alarm and waits `ALARM_CALL.retry` 3 s, then `nextCaller` picks a
  guard still in the fight (in combat, or the search after it; not blind, held, passive, melee or a dog): out of the
  target's sight first (`Enemy.targetInView`), then an officer, then the lower key (no randomness). Nobody left: the
  debt lapses, no alarm. Contact call cut first: nothing is owed.
- **Gates.** `RADIO_ALARM` true; command calls only with `stealth && coordinated` and no alarm raised (panels' rule).
  A panel alarm settles the debt; `resetAlarm` (Sector load / restart) and `clear` drop it.
- **Net section.** `EnemyManager.setSection(i)`; `SectorMode.loadSection` sets it to the live `SectorDef` index
  before the alarm can go (S3-CHECK item 8). The lane stays null (one live section).
- **Profile.** Kept S5's string `Enemy.profile` ('standard' / 'aggressive', lead note); no second field. New
  `Enemy.behaviour` getter = `BEHAVIOUR[profile]` (`squadRoles.ts`). Reinforcements are marked `profile = 'aggressive'`.
  High-security posts keep 'standard' (S8 maps posts to `P_POST` at merge).
- **Reinforcements.** `EnemyManager.spawnReinforcement` / `joinReinforcement` -> `Enemy.joinAsReinforcement(route)`:
  aggressive, told by command (`radio(0)`: in combat on the shared LKP the next step, bark `joining`, no contact call
  of their own), entry route run first at run speed (dropped on sight of the target or after `ENTRY_MAX` 30 s), never
  calm (`AlertInput.hunting` = fought && `neverCautious`: the search after combat never ends).
  - Sector Ops (`sectorMode.raised`): `SectorDef.entries` in turn (route, yaw, y), else `reinforce`; pending entries
    carry `reinforce` / `route`; `fill` spawns them unalerted, `placeSlot`, then `joinReinforcement`.
  - Other live maps (`StealthSystems.onAlarm`): `MapLayout.guardAi.entries` of the alarm caller's section
    (`entriesFor`), the one furthest from the player, else the furthest `MapLayout.reinforce` point; grunt, grunt, heavy
    as before. A live mode's `onAlarm` is reached even with no reinforce points (it returned early before).
  - Parked (Hunter, Mission; `coordinated` false): unchanged (Hunter's doubling via `mode.onAlarm`, `em.reinforce`).
- **Combat reads (3 lines in S8's branch, values unchanged for 'standard').** `Enemy.decide`: cover on contact
  `Math.random() < behaviour.coverFirst` (was 0.85), the hold between bounds `behaviour.advanceHold` (was
  `ADVANCE_HOLD` 2.5, constant removed), the out-of-sight last stretch `runToContact ? runSpeed x 0.75 : walk x 1.15`.
  The entry-route run is a new block before the combat switch (after the alarm-panel run). S8: keep these reads (or
  swap them for your numeric profile) when merging.

## Numbers
`RADIO.report` 4 s (unchanged), `ALARM_CALL.retry` 3 s (new), `RADIO.callIn` 2.5 s: alarm about 6.5 s x reaction after
the sighting. `BEHAVIOUR.aggressive` (unchanged, S8 module): coverFirst 0.3, advanceHold 0.8, runToContact,
neverCautious. `AGGRESSIVE_JOG` 0.75 x run speed, `ENTRY_MAX` 30 s (new, enemy.ts). `ALARM.squad` 3 and Sector
`perStage` unchanged.
