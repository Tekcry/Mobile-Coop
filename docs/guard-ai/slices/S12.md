## Choices
- The heavy keeps his plates and face-plate rule: the gear branch skips `kind === 'heavy'` (the spec's "the heavy
  keeps its own rules"). The enforcer's shield still stops frontal rounds first; a round past the shield meets gear.
- The deflect path reuses `strikeStagger` (no fire: `decide` returns early while `stagger > 0`; burst and wind-up
  dropped). `strikeStaggerT` is set too; nothing reads it today.
- Bark order: `deflect` first, then `alert()`'s `contact` (urgent, so it cuts in); an already-alerted guard just says
  the deflect line (cooldown permitting).
- Parts are built only when a guard first gets gear (Marlow Yard builds none), then toggled. Colours: the faction's
  helmet and accent colours (`factionLook`, at build time). A dog never shows gear.
- Section default gear reaches every guard `fill` places, reinforcements included (S13 adds stage gear on top via
  `setGear`).
- `placeSlot` always calls `setGear` (no tag, no default = no gear), so a re-placed guard loses gear the new slot does
  not give.
- The dropped helmet starts at head height (1.65 m x his scale) and lands 0.7 m along the shot, `0.45 x` its height
  above his feet (fixed arc, no physics body).

## What S9 must carry (protocol 32)
- `EnemyState.al` bits `AL_HELMET` 32 and `AL_ARMOUR` 64 from `Enemy.helmet` / `armour`; the client's `EnemyPuppet`
  builds the same placeholder parts and toggles them, and on a helmet bit falling 1 -> 0 drops a helmet through its
  own `HelmetDrops` (the arc is deterministic, `helmetArc`).
- `hitConfirm` kind `'deflect'` (`protocol.ts:129` union). Today `coopHost.ts:1018` sends a confirm only when
  `res.dealt > 0`, so a client's deflected round shows no marker: S9 sends `res.deflect ? 'deflect' : ...` when
  `res.dealt > 0 || res.deflect`.
- Optionally the `deflect` bark through S9's `bark` event.
