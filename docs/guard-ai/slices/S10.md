## Built
- `EnemyManager.buildPair` (called from `placeSlot` when the slot list is known): when the second of two guards tagged as
  a pair is placed (his tag names the partner, or a placed guard's tag names him; the mirror entry is skipped), one
  `PairMeet` (A = the lower slot, id = section x 100 + it). Meet stops: new pure `pickMeet` (pairs.ts): the nearest
  pair of stops within `PAIRS.meetMax` 6 m whose stays overlap (`pairWindow`), a tie to the longer stay; speed = the
  slower guard's `walkSpeed` (a timed loop walks at `walkSpeed`). The two walkers' current loop times line the clocks
  up (new `PatrolWalker.loopTime()`), so guards placed at different moments still agree. No window (no timed loop,
  periods differ, no overlap, a dog): `pairSkips` + a console warning, no pair.
- `EnemyManager.pairStep` at the board rate (`PAIRS.tick` 0.2 s), after the guards move, no allocation: fills `pm.a` /
  `pm.b` from `guardAtSlot` (present = alive, not held, still in his slot; calm = `Enemy.pairCalm`: unaware, caution 0,
  no incident, no camera body), `interrupt()` when either is `Enemy.disturbed` (a `hearTurn` look, a post lure, sight
  evidence above `PERCEPTION.leak`), `step`, then acts:
  - meeting: `Enemy.pairFace` / `pairPt` set (NaN otherwise). `Enemy.calmDecide`'s patrol branch still steps the walker
    (clock and schedule run on), walks him onto his meet point and stands him there facing his partner (a reused goal
    object). After the talk he walks on late; the next timed stop absorbs it.
  - `sayLine` -> new `Enemy.sayLine(text)`: the bark callback `onBark(e, line, false)` (spoken, over the speaker on the
    HUD and as a subtitle), skipping the voice table. Lines are the module's `PAIR_LINES` (<= 90 chars, calm, G82).
  - `requested` -> `pairMissing`: coordinated, a `missing` incident at the partner's last route point (`lookX/Z`) with
    the one left as its only perceiver, keyed by the missing guard's number: the board sends him as the lead and the
    nearest free guard as cover (S5b's path; his `pairSearch` barks "missing"). The missing guard joins `silent`, so the
    squad radio check does not miss him a second time. Parked modes: `searchAt` there.
  - A `missing` pair whose two guards are present again (a woken partner takes his slot via `swapSlot`) is `reset()`.
- Cleared with the section (`clear`, a new slot list in `placeSlot`).
- `Enemy`: `pairFace`, `pairPt`, `patrolRoute`, `loopTime()`, `routePoint(out)`, `pairCalm`, `disturbed`, `sayLine`.
