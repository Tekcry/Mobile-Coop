## Choices
- Cache by time and cell pair together (both asked for in the brief): the verdict is fresh within 0.25 s and recast at
  once when either end changes cell, so a burst cannot run on across a line that just changed.
- Reposition reuses the existing cover pick (its relocation range, scores and reservations) with one extra filter,
  rather than a new search; the cast budget keeps the pick bounded. The fallback is the existing chase (flow field round
  the pane: E2 glass is a nav fence).
- The S8 `squadRoles` / `coverGrid` modules were not wired on the build branch. After the merge of master 4.75.0 (S1-S12):
  `decide` runs S8's `roleStep` first, then the armour check (for a role holder too; every role fire path goes through
  `tryFire`, so the gate covers it); a reposition calls `ctx.blockCover` on the cover it leaves and drops `roleCover`
  until the next roles tick (A82); the gate field is `fireGate` (S12 brought a gear flag `armour`); `pauseT` uses the
  seeded `rng`.
- `heldTicks` / `casts` on the gate and `roundsFired` on the guard are debug counters the e2e reads.

## e2e (written, not run)
`scripts/e2e-sectorops.mjs` `--part=6`, block "guards hold fire at armour (guard-ai S16)" (:2674-2756): seeded
`Math.random` (mulberry32), sim-time steps (0.1 s). Walks to S4 as `campusS4Part` does, finds the NOC pair (on the
mezzanine, x -4..8, z 42..48, y > 3), alerts them, puts the player on the hall floor at the first of five spots in front
of the window one of them sees him from, then 10 s: asserts a guard saw him (sight through clear armour), the gate cast
and held, 0 rounds fired from the NOC, both still alerted; logs per guard whether he left the NOC, moved or held.

Risks:
- The hall spots are picked from the window geometry (eye 5.15, sill 4.6), not walked: if a pod, basket or rail on the
  hall floor blocks all five, the "sees him" assert fails (setup, not the gate). Widen the spot list then.
- Other S4 guards wake on the player in the hall (the AI is on for the whole section); they may engage him (no damage,
  `damageMul` 0) and noise or a radio call can change the pair's state; only rounds fired from inside the NOC count.
- The pair may be high-security posts (leashed): then they hold (A84), which the block accepts.
- A NOC guard leaving by the stairs and firing from the hall within the same 0.1 s step as he crosses z 41.8 would count;
  unlikely at walk / run pace.
- The muzzle sits 0.45 m ahead of the eye: a guard pressed against the pane could put his muzzle past it (then the round
  is not blocked and he fires). The NOC pair stand ~1 m back (z 43).
