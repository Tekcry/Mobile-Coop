## Numbers

turnMax 0.45 (below `PERCEPTION.investigate` 0.6, above `suspicious` 0.3). Loud shot 28 m: combat inside ~7.0 m in the

open, 12.7 m behind a wall.

## Behaviour changes
- A noise sends ONE guard (nearest inside half its effective reach); every other guard in reach turns to look and
  stays at his post (a close one may show suspicious for ~3.5 s, then calm). A guard beyond half the reach (e.g. 6 m of
  a 9 m noise) now only turns; before, he walked over.
- Loud gunfire: combat only inside a quarter of the effective reach; between that and half, one guard is sent to look
  (and the LKP is set for him); beyond, heads turn. Walls now count for gunfire (before: none). A shot behind a wall
  20 m off reaches nobody.
- Walls for ordinary noises: the ray is cast wherever one wall changes the tier (before only beyond 45 % of the reach),
  so a wall can now also drop a close hearer from "sent" to "turn".
- Footsteps (4 Hz): only the nearest close guard is sent; others stop and face the sound while it continues. As the
  player moves, the nearest can change between steps, so two guards can end up investigating a walking player (no
  dedupe until S2).
- Searching guards: a tier-2 shot no longer alerts them (it did within 28 m); they keep searching.
