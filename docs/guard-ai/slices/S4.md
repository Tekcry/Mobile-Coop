## Built
- **Stable guard keys.** `Enemy.key` (0-31) stamped in `EnemyManager.spawn`: the lowest key not held by a guard on the
  list or a knocked-out body (`freeKey`), `awareness.resetKey` on it (a dead guard's key is reused clean). `Body.guardKey`
  and `Body.guard` (the downed `Enemy`) set in `addBody`.
- **Revive keeps the guard.** `revive(b, by)` builds the new `Enemy` facing as he fell (old yaw, no `Math.random`, A7)
  and `Enemy.inherit(old, b.age)`: key, squad (`swapSquad`: his place on the roster, off the silent set), held room,
  passive, `info`, sniper post and the patrol walker itself (route, post, facing; its clock moved on by the time down, so
  a timed loop rejoins on schedule; back on the route at the nearest point).
- **Down hook.** `ctx.onKilled` -> `downed(e)` (coordinated only): `awareness.onDown(key, !e.ko)`, then the witness pass.
  `despawn` counts as dead; `clear` (Sector unload / restart via `despawnAll` / `resetMemory`) clears the table.
- **Witnessed kill** (`witnessKill`): every calm guard (not alerted, held, passive, melee or dog) who would notice the
  body there (`bodyNoticed`: field, the light on it, 20 m) plus one LOS ray is a witness. Dead: the nearest finds the
  body at once (`bodyFound`, every witness held). Knocked out: he goes to wake him (A14). A dog (no body): witnesses
  held, the nearest searches and radios.
- **Body found** (`bodyFound`, coordinated): dead -> bark `body`, search; the guards who hear his shout (S3 voice model,
  `hearsShout` / `voiceMask`) are aware, the nearest calm one searches with him; `openHold('dead', finder | voice |
  witnesses)`; a `body` radio call (inc = the hold id); on delivery each receiver `addAware` -> held; caller barks
  `deadReport` on completion. No alarm (A2). Knocked out -> bark `koFound`, walk over, wake (existing reviving flow).
  Parked modes: the old code path (22 m squad search), untouched.
- **KO caution** (`woke(by, e)`): `startKo(by.key, e.key)`, the waker's search restarts (`Enemy.koSearch`: AlertMachine
  search clock to 0, re-centred), a `ko` radio call (bark `koReport`, one "Copy."; nobody else cautious).
- **Tick.** `awareness.tick(0.2)` + `syncCaution()` in the 5 Hz board block; `syncCaution` also after every change.
- **Cautious behaviour** (`Enemy.caution`, `setCaution`): `AlertInput.cautious` -> an unaware guard goes to `cooldown`
  and the cooldown never ends while cautious (sight x1.3 = `SENSITIVITY.cooldown`; the board's cooldown weight picks
  him first); patrol `PACE.cautious` 0.95 (not on timed loops); `PatrolWalker.waitMul` = `CAUTION.waitMul` 0.6 on route
  waits and post glances; torch rule of a searching guard. No bark on unaware -> cooldown.
- **Barks**: `koFound`, `koReport` (radio), `deadReport` (radio); the three urgent (they follow the `body` line within
  the bark cooldown).
- **Readouts**: `EnemyManager.awareness`, `cautionTable`, `cautionOf(e)`, `killsWitnessed`, `lastWitnesses`,
  `lastHold`.
