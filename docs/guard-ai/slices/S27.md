## Goal / done looks like
- No guard with a role stands upright and still in the open out of sight of his target for more than 3 s: he crouches
  while he waits for the group, then takes the nearest protecting cover (today's `pickCover`); with no cover anywhere
  he stays crouched (the 12 s rule still ends it).
- A suppressing overwatch fires only along a clear line from his muzzle to the spot; he never changes his own
  `lastKnown` by suppressing.
- The reporter throws nothing and flanks nowhere while his contact call is open.
- After any alarm stage (Sector Ops: panel, camera, laser) no guard of the live section is holstered.
- R_NONE guards, the parked modes and a lone guard: unchanged.

## Build
1. Open wait (`roleStep`, the tail `:2522-2531`): while out of sight in the open (`!los`, state `chase` / `idle`):
   `openT += dt` and `wantCrouch = true` (the reporter as today); `openT >= SQUAD.openWait`, or no role cover at all
   (`c < 0`, any role but a point, which keeps `closing` as today): `setState('seekCover')`, `coverPicked = false`,
   `armourPick = false`, return null, so today's `seekCover` branch runs `pickCover` (the nearest protecting cover,
   reserved through `reserveCover`, so roles and today's pick share `coverSet.owner`); `pickCover` false: today's
   fallback (`attack`, then `chase` after 2.6 s out of sight: the cover-to-cover advance at `ADVANCE_PACE`, walked).
   `openT` = 0 whenever he sees his target, is in or on his way to cover, or his role changes. His role stays: the
   next roles tick may hand him a cover again (a held cover gets `stayBonus`, so no churn).
2. Suppress (`suppressLkp`, `:2557-2562`): aim at `suppressAim` (the hunt's spot + 1.1 m), not `lastKnown`;
   `aimAt` (`:3055`) returns `suppressAim` for a `suppress` round while `suppressing` is set (cleared after the
   `tryFire` call), else `lastKnown` as today (the attack / enforcer suppress after a fresh sight keeps today's aim).
   With `SQUAD.suppressView`: every 0.25 s while suppressing, one ray muzzle -> `suppressAim` against static geometry
   (`G.STATIC`, as `coverSees`) caches `suppressOk`; not ok: no round, `suppressHeld++`. Both callers (`:2529`, `:2815`)
   go through it. `rolesFor`'s cover choice (`:2749-2761`) is unchanged.
3. Reporter (`inCover`, `:2807-2814`): the `onRadio` block moves above `if (this.tactics(t))`; in `attack` (`:2716`)
   the call becomes `!this.onRadio && this.tactics(t)` (a reporter caught in the open with his call still open fights
   back with his rifle, as S8 allows, but throws and flanks only after the call).
4. Alarm stage: the manager's ctx gets `alarmStage: () => this.alarmStage`; `carryCalm` passes `this.ctx.alarmRaised()
