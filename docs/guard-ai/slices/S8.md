# S8 Tactics - integration (guard-ai, 2026-10-23)

Wires the pure modules `src/ai/squadRoles.ts` + `src/ai/coverGrid.ts` (slices/S8-module.md) into `Enemy` /
`EnemyManager`. A first implementer was cut off mid-work (WIP commit 2d3dfc51 + uncommitted tests); this pass reviewed
that WIP, kept it (it was sound and green) and finished the rest.

## Built
Kept from the WIP (2d3dfc51), reviewed:
- `EnemyManager`: `coverSet` / `coverGrid` built at load; `reserveCover` / `releaseCover` mirrored into
  `coverSet.owner` (owner = guard `num`); `rolesStep` at 2 Hz in coordinated modes (one reused `RoleInput` /
  `RoleOutput`, member arrays reused): the engaged group = alerted, free, cover-using guards (`inGroup`), >= 2; LKP,
  player cover time, caller; overwatch low-cover view ray to the LKP (max 4 re-picks, a blind cover blocked 5 s);
  one of point / flank moving at a time (both standard); `setRole` on every guard (`R_NONE` outside the group).
  `contactCaller` = the engagement's first contact caller (reporter); `combatSeed` / `blockCover` on `AiContext`;
  `reset` clears reservations, blocks, caller.
- `Enemy`: `role`, `roleCover`, `roleMoving`, `roleRun`, `mateMoving`, `onRadio`, `closing`; `setRole` (bark
  "Covering!" when a mate starts moving), `roleStep` before today's state machine (bound to the role's cover when the
  group allows: standard point only out of sight and after `advanceHold`; out of sight in the open the role guard waits
  for the group; a point with no cover walks the last stretch, `closing`), `boundTo` (A* + reserve + `seekCover`; no
  path -> `blockCover`), `suppressLkp` (overwatch fires at the shared LKP while a mate moves). Reporter: holds fire in
  `seekCover`, stays crouched in cover while his call is open. A role guard does not step out of cover after 5 peeks.
  Combat `Math.random` -> `this.rng` (hit -> cover 50%, cover-first `< profile.coverFirst`, attack -> cover 2.5-4.5 s,
  strafe time / side, blind-fire 30%, grenade cooldown, burst length / pause, spread 2 draws); reseeded on going to
  combat. Aggressive profile: jog on the last stretch, presses in while strafing.
- `barks.ts`: `moving`, `covering`, `flanking`.

Finished in this pass:
- `Enemy.rng` moved up beside `num` (field initialisers draw from it): the initial `pauseT`, `strafe`, `grenadeCd` now
  come from the seeded dice; the `rand()` helper (Math.random) is gone. `thinkT` (perception cadence) stays on
  `Math.random` on purpose: e2e-stealth-ai 'vision-detect' seeds `Math.random` so three runs with different guards
  (different `num`) detect on the same frame; a `num`-based stagger would break that.
- `Enemy.profile` renamed `tacticsProfile` (numeric code): feature/guard-ai-s5 adds `profile: BehaviourProfile` (string)
  on `Enemy`, so the two would have collided as a duplicate member at the merge.
- `roleStep`: a reused goal object (decide runs every frame: no allocation on the role path); a failed bound clears
  `roleCover` so a blocked cover is not re-pathed (A*) every frame until the next roles tick.
- Point: after 5 peeks in its cover with no sight it goes `closing` (walks the last stretch, out of the group; the next
  tick picks a new point), so a group holding in band still closes in. `peekCycles` reset on each bound.
- Single flanker: cleared only while a group has roles (`rolesActive`); a lone guard in a coordinated mode keeps
  today's `assignFlanker` (the WIP cleared it for every coordinated guard).
- `EnemyManager` non-combat `Math.random` (module note): reinforcements face the LKP (else yaw 0), a revived guard
  yaw 0, radio-check spread a golden-ratio sequence (`radioSeq`, reset with the manager). Left on purpose: the parked
  modes' `callAlert` / shout jitter (parked code; the radio net replaces them in the live modes).

drops back into cover and stays down while he radios; one moves up cover to cover ("Moving up!"), one covers from
cover and fires at where you were ("Covering!"), and one works round the side ("Flanking!"). Only one moves up at a
time and nobody runs at you across the open. Stay hidden: they still close in (the point walks the last stretch).
