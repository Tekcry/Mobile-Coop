## Built
- `src/ai/incidents.ts` (new, pure, no Babylon, fixed pools):
  - `INCIDENT` table (numbers below), `IncidentKind` (all design kinds; S2 uses noise, shots, distraction, light,
    missing), `LEVEL` codes, `GV_*` flags.
  - `GuardView` (struct of typed arrays: id = `Enemy.num`, x / y / z, level, flags, task), grows by doubling only.
  - `PathLen` interface (the manager's: straight when the nav line is clear, else A* length; tests: a fake).
  - `IncidentBoard`: `report(kind, x, z, y, key, who, n, now)` -> incident id (new or merged); `tick(dt, view)` (assign,
    ack, follow / escalate, close); output `BoardOut` (SEND with a mode, ACK with a "Copy." flag); `clear`, `slotOf`,
    `kindOf`, `investigatorsOf`, `openCount`, `escalations`.
  - `lightRingPoint` (the light check's ring), `lightInView` (sight field test for a light going out).
- Rules (design 3):
  - Merge: same kind (noise / shots / distraction) within 6 m (3D) and 10 s of the incident's last report; a light by its
    id (or circuit key); a plain noise within 6 m on the plan of a light that just went out merges into it (the pop at the
    fixture, the round's impact); a light reported where a noise still waits for its investigator takes it over. A merge
    adds perceivers, never a second investigator. Gunfire never merges into a plain noise.
  - Who goes: perceivers first (tier-2 hearers; for a light, those who saw it, else those who heard the pop), else for
    noise kinds a calm guard within 25 m; a light with no witness sends nobody and closes after 10 s. Eligible: level
    unaware / suspicious / cooldown, no task, not melee / dog, not held, not busy (alarm run, passive), not down. The 3
    nearest by straight 3D distance get a path query; score = path x officer 1.6 x cooldown 0.8; ties to the lower
    view index (= list order); no path for all 3: the straight-nearest (the old rule).
  - Count: noise / shots / distraction 1; light 2 when a second eligible witness is within 15 m of it, else 1 (M1).
  - Others who perceived it: ACK once each (look 3 s, keep task); the first 2 say "Copy." (radio).
  - Follow: an investigator off the task (back, in combat, on another search) is done; all done -> closed. Down (dead /
    KO: gone from the list) or held for 25 s -> a `missing` incident at where he was last: the 2 nearest calm guards (no
    radius) go, lead to the spot, cover 5 m short of it on his side. A missing incident does not escalate again (S4 / S6).
- `EnemyManager`:
  - `board` (one per manager: Sector Ops loads one section), `view`, `paths`, `incidentTable` (= `INCIDENT`, for e2e).
  - `boardStep(dt)`: fill the view, tick, apply the commands (`sendTo`, `checkLight`, `pairSearch`, `ack`). Runs at 5 Hz
    in `update` (coordinated only) and with dt 0 right after every report (assignment and acks are immediate).
  - `hear(pos, reach, kind = 'noise')`: every tier >= 1 hearer turns (`hearTurn`); tier-2 hearers' ids are reported
    (`noise`, or `distraction` from the noise maker and the sticky-cam ping). `noise()`: tier 3 / fighting -> combat
    as before; the rest turn; tier-2 reported as `shots` (LKP set when any was reported). `pickNearest` and `sendOne` are
    gone (`hearing.ts` no longer exports `pickNearest`).
  - `lightsOut(x, z, y?, lightId?, group?)`: coordinated -> a `light` incident (perceivers below); uncoordinated -> the old
    rule (`lightsOutOld`, unchanged). `clear()` also clears the board.
  - S1 check fix 1: held guards (`e.taken`) are skipped as hearers in `hear` (both modes) and in coordinated `noise`;
    the board never sends a held guard (and gives him no ack). Fix 4: `tierOf` with `coordinated = false` uses the exact
    old ray (`d > reach x MUFFLE`, muffled to `MUFFLE`).
  - 3D: incidents and candidate distances are 3D (S1 polish: 2D picked a guard on another floor).
- Lights (goal 4, `lightsOut`): perceivers = calm guards (not passive, held or melee) who SAW it go: a fixture of the
  light (or of the circuit) in the room they stand in (`roomAt`, the light probed 1.5 m under the fixture), else the
  first fixture inside the focused cone to 25 m or the peripheral field to 12 m gets one line-of-sight ray (eye to
  fixture, the last 0.4 m slack, `ballistics.clear`). The incident sits on the floor under the light (storey picked 2.5 m
  under the fixture). `StealthSystems.shotRay` now reports the light before the bulb's 5 m pop, so the pop and the 4 m
  impact merge into it; the wall switch passes its circuit (`sw.group`); the EMP passes its point. Every lights-out
  path goes through `EnemyManager.lightsOut` (the timed isolator on Menu Notes' branch must call it the same way).
- `Enemy` (`src/ai/enemy.ts`):
  - `incident` (the board's id, -1), `sendTo(x, z, id)` (bark `checking`, then the `heard` path: suspicious, investigates
    after `hearLook`, cooldown after), `checkLight(x, z, id, slot)` (straight to investigating, bark `lightOut`),
    `pairSearch(x, z, id, lead)` (`searchAt`; lead barks `missing`), `ack(x, z, copy)` (look 3 s unless busy; bark `ack`).
  - The light check (`lightStep`): walk at the investigate pace (a pair's second stops 2.2 m short; gives up walking
    after 30 s), look at the fixture 2 s, 4 ring points (2, 3.3, 4.7, 6 m, a quarter turn apart from the side he came
    from; the partner's ring an eighth on), 1.5 s at each looking outward (a point not reached in 8 s or without a path
    is skipped), then `standDown`: bark "Nothing here. Back to it.", unaware (cooldown only if he has fought), rejoin his
    route. A glimpse during it (meter >= 0.6) drops the check: he investigates what he saw.
  - The task is cleared on calm (bark `standDown` instead of "clear"), combat, or another search (a pair keeps its
    search). `hearTurn` never re-aims a guard with a task (S1 polish: it overwrote a sent guard's stimulus).
  - Paces (A9, `PACE`): patrol 1.1 (unchanged), investigate 0.85, search 0.75 when coordinated; parked modes keep 1.2 /
    1.3; the walk to wake a KO'd squadmate keeps 1.3 (until S4: the e2e 'revive' timing). Coordinated searching turns at
    the calm rate (`ENEMY_CALM_MOTION`); parked modes keep the aimed one. `AiContext.coordinated()` carries the mode.
- `AlertMachine` (`alertState.ts`): `AlertInput.hold` (the light check runs its own clock: investigating does not time
  out or end on arrival) and `AlertInput.turning` (looking at a sound he was not sent to: the linger rule waits). The
  second fixes S1 polish 2: continuous footsteps kept turners suspicious and the linger rule sent them all.
- Barks (`barks.ts`, design 10, text; radio chirp R): `ack` R "Copy." / "Copy, holding.", `checking` R "Checking it
  out.", `lightOut` "Light's gone. Taking a look.", `standDown` R "Nothing here. Back to it.", `missing` R "He's not
  answering. Two of us, go." Host HUD only (`onBark`); no wire change.
- `coordinated = false` (Hunter, Mission): no board, old hearing, old lights-out, old paces and turn rate.

## Numbers (`INCIDENT`)
perSection 16, mergeR 6 m, mergeT 10 s, lightPairR 15 m, callR 25 m, ackLook 3 s, maxCopy 2, silentEscalate 25 s,
lightCheck 8 s (= lightLook 2 + ringN 4 x ringLook 1.5), ringMin 2 m, ringMax 6 m, pathCandidates 3, officerMul 1.6,
cautiousMul 0.8, pairBack 5 m, maxPerceivers 16. Board tick 5 Hz. Paces x walkSpeed: investigate 0.85 (grunt 0.77 m/s),
search 0.75, patrol 1.1, revive 1.3.

## Behaviour changes
- A noise sends one guard; a second noise within 6 m and 10 s of the last one (footsteps, a pulsing noise maker) never
  sends another; the guard sent is not re-aimed by later noises. Others who heard it look 3 s; up to 2 say "Copy.".
- The sent guard is the nearest by walking distance (not straight), an officer is less likely to go, a guard in
  cooldown more likely.
- A light shot out or switched off: only guards who saw it go (their room goes dark, or the fixture in view with a clear
  line) react; before, the nearest calm guard within 25 m came through walls and everyone within 14 m looked. One goes,
  two if a second witness is within 15 m. They walk straight over (no 2.4 s look first), check the area and go back to
  their routes calm (no 45 s cooldown). A light nobody saw draws nobody (unless someone hears the pop and is sent by it).
- A held guard (grabbed, hostage, interrogated) no longer hears and no longer swallows the one investigator slot.
- An investigator taken out (or held) and silent 25 s: two calm guards come as a pair to where he was last.
- Investigating and searching guards walk slower than their patrol (were faster); searching guards turn at the calm rate.
- Walking close to turned guards no longer drags them over by the linger rule while they still face the steps.

### Fix 1 (blocking): the first of a light pair never stood down
- Root cause, two parts, both in the old `Enemy.lightStep` (was `src/ai/enemy.ts:1311-1376` at 661c258a):
  - The ring point was `lightRingPoint` snapped to the nearest walkable cell (`:1345-1351`) with no check that it is
    in the light's space. In the e2e the board makes b (nearer) slot 0 and a slot 1; a comes in from the NE and stops
    2.2 m short, so his third ring point (4.7 m at base + 270 deg) is (-4.7, -3): past the x -4 wall, in the racking
    hall. He walked an A* detour round the wall.
  - The ring walk timeout (`:1369-1374`): `const wp = lightT < RING_WALK ? goTo(...) : null; if (!wp &&
    routePath.length === 0) skip`. Past `RING_WALK` the path is still non-empty, so the skip never fires and he returns
    `{ point: null }` for good; `AlertInput.hold` (light check) stops the alert machine timing him out, so he stays
    investigating, his task stays on, the incident never closes. b's ring stays on the floor side, so he finished.
  - Also: `RING_WALK` 8 s was shorter than the 7.6 m walk from the 4.7 m point to the 6 m one at a grunt's investigate
    pace (0.77 m/s, ~10 s), so the last point was always cut.
- Fix: the light check is now a pure class, `src/ai/lightCheck.ts` (`LightCheck`; same stages and numbers), driven by
  `Enemy.lightStep` (`enemy.ts:1305`) through a walker made once (`goTo`, its path, drop path). Changes in behaviour:
  - A ring point must have a clear nav line from the light's floor cell (`lightCheck.ts:190`); else the point halfway
    in is tried; else it is skipped. The ring stays in the light's room.
  - The ring timeout fires whether or not a path is still being followed, and drops the path (`lightCheck.ts:146`);
    the walk in drops its path when it gives up too. Every stage is bounded: 30 + 2 + 4 x (12 + 1.5) = 86 s worst case.
  - `RING_WALK` 12 s (was 8). No test or script asserts it.
