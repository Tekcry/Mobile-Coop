# Walk card: 4.117.0 + 4.118.0 + 4.119.0 (10 minutes)

Items 1 and 2 are in the preview build: open https://tekcry.github.io/Mobile-Coop/preview/ and check the bottom of the
main menu reads **v4.119.0 PREVIEW** (or newer). Item 3 is in the live game: check it reads **v4.119.0** (or newer).
The preview keeps its own save, so stars and records there start empty. Riskiest first.

1. **Marlow Yard re-plan with two players** (Marlow Yard, preview build only; about 5 minutes)
   - The rebuilt Marlow Yard now plays for two: a player who drops and rejoins lands on the current step, a host left on
     their own sees the step line wait for the partner, a retry keeps who took which job, and "Resume hosting" reopens
     at the part of the yard you were in, not at the start. Several stuck spots and unclear step lines are fixed.
   - Menu: both players on the preview build (a second PC, or a second browser window). Host: Co-op > Host a room, set
     Mode to Campaign and Map to Marlow Yard (re-plan), then Start mission once the partner shows Ready. Partner: Co-op >
     Join with code, type the room code, then Ready. Play the gate and the carriage shed. Then the partner does Pause >
     Quit to menu and presses Rejoin (the code) on the main menu. Last, the host does Pause > Quit to menu and presses
     Resume hosting (the code).
   - Pass if each step line tells both players what to do, the rejoining partner lands on the current step's mark, and
     Resume hosting starts back in the carriage shed, not at the gate.
2. **Saltmarsh Park section 1 rebuilt** (Sector Ops, preview build only; about 3 minutes)
   - Section 1 is rebuilt to the agreed plan: a tall anti-climb steel fence all round, a new gatehouse with a glass band
     and three officers, the car park moved with a solar carport roof you can climb onto from the courier's van or the
     bike shelter, one big service yard with a storeman, a deeper plant room with a ladder to a roof hatch, a lit
     smoking shelter on the lawn, and 15 guards in all. The car park has the new real-world lighting too.
   - Menu: Sector Ops > Saltmarsh Park - Sector Ops > Start. Walk the fence line, climb onto the carport, look into the
     service yard and plant room.
   - Pass if you can't climb the fence, you can get onto the carport off the van, and nothing floats, blocks a door or
     leaves you stuck.
3. **Gatehouse window and car park light** (Sector Ops and Lighting, on for everyone; about 2 minutes)
   - In the live section 1 you can now vault through the gatehouse's open rear window both ways (the desk under it moved
     aside; the PC on it can still be downloaded from outside). The car park is now lit like a staffed site at night:
     twin lamps on each column, a new south row, a softer lane floodlight. Hiding there means behind cars, a cut circuit
     or a shot lamp.
   - Menu: live game, Sector Ops > Saltmarsh Park - Sector Ops > Start. Vault in and out of the gatehouse rear window,
     then walk the car park.
   - Pass if the window vault works both ways and the open car park has no dark patches to hide in.
