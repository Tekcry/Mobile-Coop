# Walk 4.71.0

Open /preview/. The version label (small text on the title screen and the main menu) must read v4.71.0 PREVIEW. Pad first. Note anything off with Pause > Report feedback.

## Settings
1. Main menu > Settings > Gameplay: "Mocap locomotion" is ON by default. You should see it switched on. Leave it on for the walk.

## Sector Ops, solo (Main menu > Sector Ops > Warehouse > Normal > Start)
2. Movement with mocap on: walk, jog, run and sprint with the stick, then crouch (B) and sneak. You should see smooth steps, planted feet, no foot snaps or hip drops. Try spin on the spot, quick turns, a hard stop from a run, crouching or drawing mid-walk.
3. Mocap off and on: finish or leave the run, set Settings > Gameplay > Mocap locomotion off, start again and walk. You should see the older hand-keyed walk, no breakage. Set it back on.
4. Guards: every Warehouse guard and every alarm reinforcement carries a rifle (no heavies, no melee-only). Dogs appear only with a handler. A palm strike (RT tap) never knocks a dog out.
5. Noise (throw a bottle with d-pad right): one guard walks over and says "Checking it out."; the others say "Copy." and only turn. Throw again at the same spot: no second guard comes. A noise behind a wall reaches fewer guards.
6. Patrols: investigating and searching guards walk slower than on patrol. No guard stands frozen at a patrol point.
7. Gunfire: a loud shot far off or behind walls sends one guard to look and the rest turn. Close gunfire (about 7 m in the open) puts nearby guards in combat.
8. Getting spotted: the spotter shouts "Contact!" and guards within a few metres join at once. About 2.5 s later he radios it in, then the rest of the section joins one at a time, nearest first. Retry and take the spotter down before his radio line: nobody beyond shouting range comes.
9. Shot lamp: shoot a lamp with guards in view. One or two check it, sweep round it, say "Nothing here. Back to it." and return calm. Shoot one out of sight of every guard: nobody reacts.
10. Bodies: knock out or kill a guard and leave him in view. The finder shouts "Man down!", searches the spot and radios it. Then the section looks harder, pauses less, walks slower and takes torches in the dark. Retry and take the finder out before his report: nobody else turns cautious.
11. Woken guard: leave a knocked-out guard where another guard finds him. He wakes, the two search for about a minute, stay cautious for three more, then go back to normal routes.
12. Cameras and bodies: leave a body in a camera's view (also in the dark). The camera calls it on the radio ("Camera N has a man down...") and the nearest calm guard walks straight over. Jam or shoot the camera first: nothing happens.
13. Camera reach: stand near the edge of a camera's zone. You should not be picked up at the far end, only well inside the zone.
14. Guards looking up: hang from a pipe or high ledge above a guard's eye line. He spots you very slowly or not at all.
15. Restart: get spotted, go down. After "Restarting from checkpoint" the lights (switched off or shot out), doors, guns, gadgets and guards are as at the section start, and the guards are unaware.
16. Traversal: shimmy a ledge to its end - the hands never stack on each other. Draw the rifle (X) from the sling: the gun clears her body.
17. Lights (S1 goods-in, workshop, girder): the strip-light haze is fixed. It should read darker.
18. Carry-over from 4.56 (still pending): vent grille - tap jump kicks it, holding jump alone unscrews it quietly. A landing roll needs about 1.5 m of room. Aimed into a wall, the gun tips muzzle-down. The weapon wheel opens on the vision slot (LB then A cycles goggles).

## Marlow Yard (Main menu > Co-op > Host a room > Campaign > Start mission; you can start alone)
19. Station 3 hallway camera: it covers the hallway but not the entry lobby. Standing in the lobby, you are not picked up. Standing in the hallway, you are picked up in about 3 s.
20. Station 3 security room: the console refuses while the duty guard stands; take him down, use it, the camera goes dark. Restart the bay: camera and guard are back.
21. Mocap on the course: walk, jog, crouch walk and turns through the stations look smooth, no foot snaps at doors or stairs.

## Co-op (two players: Co-op > Host a room, other player Join with code)
22. (co-op) Partner walk: when your partner first appears already walking, or after a restart teleport, their walk looks normal on your screen. No start-step pop.
23. (co-op) Throw onto a guard: one player offers the throw (RB standing). The other runs in about 3 m in front of a standing guard and is thrown. The guard drops knocked out as they land, on both screens. A guard a step past the landing stays standing. Try each player thrown.
24. (co-op) Hostage walk: the joiner grabs a guard and walks him. On the host's screen the hostage moves at a steady pace, stops when the joiner stops and does not flicker.
25. (co-op) Sector Ops light sync (Mode: Sector Ops): the host switches off a lamp and shoots one. The joiner sees them dark, the light meter and guards agree. The joiner shoots a lamp out: it goes dark for both.
26. (co-op) Sector Ops team doors: each locked door is bolted from your side; your partner unbolts it from the other side. "Unbolt" works only at the door itself - not through a wall from the corridor (S1 mess back door). Guards route round a bolted door. After a restart it is bolted again.
27. (co-op) Leg-up: boost your partner onto a high lip; they relay or drop the rope for you. Alone, the lip cannot be reached.
28. (co-op) Guard alerts agree on both screens: a "Contact!" shout, radio call and the alarm stage show on both. A guard's torch in the dark shows on the joiner's screen too.
