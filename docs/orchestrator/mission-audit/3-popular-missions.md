# Mission audit 3/3 - Popular missions and why players love them
2026-10-24. Sources: reviews, dev interviews, Steam/forum threads. Every claim carries a URL. Gaps flagged where no source was found.

## A. Case studies (asks / structure / rules / routes / guidance / co-op / why loved)

**1. Splinter Cell CT co-op (4 maps) + solo Bank.** Co-op: two agents, boost to high ledges, climb each other, throw over laser grids, hold the rope for a rappel, revive with adrenaline ([GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/review/32392-splinter-cell058-chaos-theory-review-2)). Praised as the most original feature but moves only work at set spots, so feel contrived, and only 4 maps ([Pocket-lint](https://www.pocket-lint.com/review/68164-splinter-cell-chaos-theory-xbox), [CheatCC](https://cheatcc.com/articles/tom-clancy-s-splinter-cell-chaos-theory-review-preview-for-xbox-xb-xb-rev/); exact attribution between the two not confirmed). Solo Bank: chain of objectives - find contact name, weapons-buy intel, vault access, steal bonds, optional records; two ways into the treasurer's office; a linear route works if you ghost all of it ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/13570/discussions/0/3776868105349554826)). Fans still post ghost runs (no knockouts, every PC hacked) ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/13570/discussions/0/3272435584436455451/?ctp=2)). No ranked "best CT level" list found. Lesson: co-op moves must be route choices, not keyholes.

**2. Blacklist co-op (4th Echelon).** Each handler gives a mission type: Briggs story co-op, Grim strict no-alarm intel, Kobin eliminate targets, Charlie 20 waves ([Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/review/1277/splinter-cell-blacklist-co-op-review.html), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/235600/discussions/0/522730075738076113)). bit-tech: lethal-stealth missions most fun in co-op; pure ghost missions best solo because a partner doubles detection risk ([bit-tech](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/gaming/pc/splinter-cell-blacklist-review/2/)). Impatient partner flagged as the main problem; some sets reset you to start on detection ([Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/review/1277/splinter-cell-blacklist-co-op-review.html), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/splinter-cell-blacklist-review)). Lesson: a ghost rule in co-op needs mutual-aid tools, or it punishes the pair.

**3. Conviction co-op + Deniable Ops.** Six-hour, four-level prequel with its own story; one death restarts ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2009/12/10/news-splinter-cell-conviction-co-op-details.aspx)). Co-Optimus: stands on its own in length and fun ([Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/review/454/page/3/splinter-cell-conviction-co-op-review.html)); Giant Bomb user: little interaction between agents beyond dialogue ([Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/user-reviews/id/111868/splinter-cell-conviction-review)). Modes: Hunter, Infiltration (detection = fail), Last Stand, Face-Off ([GamingNexus](https://www.gamingnexus.com/News/15527/First-details-for-Splinter-Cell-Conviction-Co-op-and-Multiplayer)).

**4. Hitman - Sapienza.** Brief was just "coastal town"; vertical "Swiss cheese" layout - "Every hole will bring you to your destination" ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/hitman-sapienza-level-analysis/)); main hit near the mansion, Elusive Targets and Escalations reuse the wider town ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-making-of-sapienza-hitmans-best-level/)). Lesson: dense loops beat long corridors; one map, many missions.

**5. Hitman - Paris Showstopper.** Two targets at a fashion show; beginner puzzle - get into a restricted area, split a roaming target from his guard; bartender poison, model swap, chandelier accident ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=545871)). NPCs react to the disguise you wear and their chatter reveals kill options ([Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/2016/05/review-hitman)).

**6. Hitman - Mission Stories (guidance).** Overheard leads open guided chains. Defenders: great for learning a map on run 1, optional, can be turned off ([EGM](https://egmnow.com/hitman-3-review/)). Critics: hand-holding turns discovery into a checklist ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/forum/viewthread.aspx?tid=978016), [Hitman Forum](https://hitmanforum.com/t/anyone-else-feel-displeased-about-the-lack-of-explicit-mission-stories-in-ambrose-island/21172)). Lesson: diegetic leads with a guidance toggle.

**7. Hitman - Whittleton Creek / Isle of Sgail.** Whittleton: suburb with gossip to overhear and secrets in every corner, but only about two streets deep ([Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/2018/11/hitman-2-review/), [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/review/1750-hitman-2/)). Sgail: masked party in a castle, two targets plus a VIP you must keep alive; hard at first, a playground once learned; only six stories ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/best/best-hitman-levels)).

**8. Hitman 3 - Dartmoor.** Take a detective's identity, solve a family murder; question suspects, break alibis, find hidden passages - all optional, only the hit is required ([Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hitman-3-review-murder-mystery)). One reviewer spent three hours on the mystery vs about 15 minutes direct (same). Called the level that best sums up the game (same).

**9. Hitman - Elusive Targets / Freelancer.** ET brief: "a snipe where your one shot matters"; one attempt, limited intel, time-limited ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-elusive-targets-system-in-2016-s-i-hitman-i-)). Downside per IOI: fear of failing makes players play safe and see little (same). Freelancer: deduce the leader among suspects; suspects flee if you cause chaos; fail and lose gear ([GameLuster](https://gameluster.com/review-hitman-freelancer-the-hitman-is-free-but-you-have-to-find-the-lance-yourself/), [KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/reviews/ps5-reviews/hitman-freelancer-review-carve-your-own-path-ps5/)).

**10. Dishonored - Lady Boyle's Last Party.** Three sisters in identical roles at a masked ball; find which is the target via clues and talk ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/the-making-of-dishonoreds-greatest-mission-lady-boyles-last-party/), [SegmentNext](https://segmentnext.com/dishonored-mission-5-lady-boyles-last-party-guide-no-kill-stealth-and-low-chaos/)). Guards ignore a masked guest, so stealth becomes about motive, not sightlines; front door, balcony or back entry; lethal or non-lethal ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/dishonoreds-party-level-rewrote-the-rules-of-stealth-ga-1613011624)). Kotaku: "The stealth genre is a playground, a stage for player creativity."

**11. Dishonored 2 - Clockwork Mansion.** Levers rebuild rooms; walk inside the walls behind the machinery; roof route ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/dishonored-2-hands-on-exquisite-stealth-in-a-reorganizing-clockwork-mansion), [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/dishonored-2-review/)). PCWorld calls it the game's high point ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3143714/dishonored-2-review-new-stealth-highs-hobbled-by-frustrating-pc-performance-lows.html)). Complaint: map system inadequate for the complexity ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/403640/discussions/0/3223871682624291065)).

**12. Deus Ex HR Detroit police station / MD Palisade Bank.** Station: talk past the desk sergeant (social check, XP bonus), or sewers, back alley, roof, vents, or fight; the talked-past sergeant reappears later ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/deusexhumanrevolution/walkthrough/detroitpolicestation.php), [SuperCheats](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/deus-ex-human-revolution/investigating-the-suicide-terrorist)). Palisade: web of security and shortcuts, called the game's highlight; social-engineer the receptionist, hack, punch a weak wall into ducts ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/making-deus-ex-mankind-divideds-best-level-the-palisade-bank), [GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/adam-jensen-2-0-deus-ex-mankind-divided-review/)).

**13. Thief - Life of the Party / Shalebridge Cradle.** Life of the Party: rooftop crossing, break-in for info and loot, return; inspired by Rainbow Six rooftops; guards trade insults ([Thief Wiki](https://thief.fandom.com/wiki/Life_of_the_Party), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/211740/discussions/0/1291817837627394080)). Cradle: often called the series' best level for sound, horror and found-text history ([TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/YMMV/ThiefDeadlyShadows), [Challenging Destiny](https://challengingdestiny.com/reviews/thief3.htm)); one reviewer finds its back-and-forth layout tedious (same).

**14. MGS V Ground Zeroes - Camp Omega.** Open base, multiple ways in (ride in a truck, find a side door); performance rank drives replay ([EIP](https://eip.gg/reviews/metal-gear-solid-v-ground-zeroes-review/)). Game Informer: side ops straightforward and unimaginative ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/games/metal_gear_solid_ground_zeroes/b/playstation4/archive/2014/03/17/metal-gear-solid-v-ground-zeroes-review)). Lesson: same map re-run at another time of day needs a new twist, not a new marker.

**15. Payday 2 - Big Bank / Shadow Raid / Framing Frame.** Stealth fails into loud rather than to a reload ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/1474222595302816655)). Big Bank: find the right computer, stealth skips drilling ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/540744934240105395)). Shadow Raid: enforced stealth, good practice map ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/135514402719919292)). Framing Frame: 3 days - steal paintings, sell, frame a senator; extra guard after the 4th kill; final day called bad on its own ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payday_2), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/3183345176710889222)). Randoms ruin stealth runs; players kick or go solo ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/540744475426292542)).

**16. GTA V heists / Cayo Perico.** Jewel Store: pick Loud or Smart; crew skill changes the take (bad driver loses jewels) ([GTA Wiki](https://gta.fandom.com/The_Jewel_Store_Job)). Cayo: scouting unlocks infiltration, escape and compound entry points (8 infiltration points); radio tower kills cameras; first heist doable solo end to end ([GTA BOOM](https://wiki.gtaboom.com/wiki/all-scope-out-locations-in-gta-onlines-cayo-perico-heist)).

Co-op team games (GTFO, DRG, Helldivers 2, Ready or Not, Siege).** GTFO: hack a console, relay the code to a teammate elsewhere; teamwork plus difficulty make it a top co-op game; weak guidance ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/gtfo-review), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/you-need-teamwork-stealth-and-steady-nerves-to-survive-co-op-horror-fps-gtfo)). Siege: one drones while another pushes on that intel ([Guild Order](https://guildorder.com/games/r6/wiki/drones-and-intel)).

**18. Systems that teach stealth (Invisible Inc, Mark of the Ninja, Keep Talking).** Invisible Inc alarm fills every turn and adds cameras and guards - each extra turn is more danger ([bit-tech](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/gaming/invisible-inc-review/2/), [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/reviews/review-of-invisible-inc-a-demanding-and-inventive-mix-of-strategy/z835)). Hazelight (A Way Out / It Takes Two).** Built for two from day one ([Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/it-takes-two-lovingly-marries-story-and-gameplay-together)); Fares: the story "happens on the couch" ([AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/josef-fares-it-takes-two-interview)). ## B. Synthesis

**Recurring ingredients of beloved missions**
1. One clear goal, many verbs: required objective is short; everything else is optional depth (Dartmoor, Sgail, Detroit station).
2. A social or puzzle hook beyond "don't be seen": find which of three (Boyle), solve a murder (Dartmoor), reshape rooms (Clockwork).
3. Dense, looping space with 3+ entries; no dead ends (Sapienza, Palisade, Camp Omega).
4. Intel changes routes: scouting or eavesdropping opens doors (Cayo scope-out, Hitman leads, Palisade receptionist).
5. Readable systems: players know why they were seen (Mark of the Ninja rules).
6. Escalation instead of instant fail: alarm meters, stealth failing into loud (Invisible Inc, Payday).
7. Consequences that persist: freed sergeant comes back (Deus Ex), lost take (Big Score, Jewel Store crew).
8. Replay hooks on the same map: ranks, challenges, other targets (Sapienza, Ground Zeroes).

**Mission rules that work (and the condition that keeps them fun)**
| Rule | Works when | Source pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Ghost / no alarms | Readable detection, partner can cover mistakes, short sections | Grim missions, CT Bank ghost runs |
| No kills | Strong non-lethal kit and rewards that don't punish the other style | Ready or Not S rank; Kotaku warns against forcing one style |
| Interrogate a specific person | Player must deduce who first; interrogation yields route intel | Lady Boyle, Freelancer, Dartmoor |
| Disguise / social | NPCs react; tells are learnable | Paris, Sgail, Boyle |
| Timed / one chance | Clear timer, intel up front, opt-in | Elusive Targets, Cayo escape window |
| Plant / frame | Plant site visible to guards so timing matters | Framing Frame, Whittleton |
| Retrieve under pressure | Alarm turns on at pickup; extraction is the climax | Big Score, Payday |
| Protect a VIP | VIP is static or self-moving on a schedule, not an escort | Sgail "keep alive" |
| No gadgets / lost kit | Short, atmospheric, gear recoverable | Cradle-style |
| Split-up | Each half has its own verbs and a sync point | GTFO code relay, Siege drone/push |

**Parallel 4-player objectives: fun vs chaos**
- Fun: each team has a distinct verb set and space, plus 1-2 hard sync points (code relay, simultaneous key turn) ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/gtfo-review)).
- Fun: one team's success visibly helps the other (radio tower kills cameras in Cayo) ([GTA BOOM](https://wiki.gtaboom.com/wiki/all-scope-out-locations-in-gta-onlines-cayo-perico-heist)).
- Chaos: one player's mistake ends everyone's run (randoms ruining stealth) ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/540744475426292542)). Fix: alarms scoped to a sector, shared meter rises but doesn't fail.
- Chaos: splitting needs voice comms; without it, groups scatter ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/553850/discussions/0/4302697906483231845)). Fix: pings, a shared objective board, auto-callouts.
- Solo path: objectives must be sequential-capable for one player (Cayo solo) with a time or rank cost, never impossible.

**What makes 2-player co-op special**
- Mutual dependence through moves that open routes (CT boost, rope, laser throw) - but at many spots, not fixed keyholes.
- Asymmetric information: one sees, one acts (Keep Talking, Siege drone). Swap roles mid-mission so nobody watches all night.
- Shared story moments (Hazelight) - but give both players something to do at once (A Way Out complaint).
- Revive/cover: partner can fix a mistake before it becomes an alarm.

**Pitfalls players hate (avoid)**
- Escort missions: slow, dumb NPC, you wait at triggers ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/protect-the-moron-), [Game-Wisdom](https://game-wisdom.com/critical/escort-mission)).
- Forced fail-on-detection: Star Wars Outlaws patched these out; Conviction criticised ([Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2024/09/star-wars-outlaws-instant-fail-stealth-sections-will-be-reworked-in-upcoming-ps5-patch), [Giant Bomb](https://www.giantbomb.com/user-articles/id/36296/where-splinter-cell-conviction-fails-and-succeeds)).
- Backtracking through the same rooms (Cradle critique) ([Challenging Destiny](https://challengingdestiny.com/reviews/thief3.htm)).
- Trial-and-error / no guidance (GTFO, Framing Frame luck restarts) ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/gtfo-review), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/218620/discussions/8/3183345176710889222)).
- Unclear map on complex levels (Clockwork complaint) ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/403640/discussions/0/3223871682624291065)).
- Waiting for teammates / idle partner (A Way Out) ([Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/a-way-out-review)).
- Over-guidance killing discovery (Mission Stories debate) ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/forum/viewthread.aspx?tid=978016)).
- One-chance fear making players play safe (Elusive Targets) ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-elusive-targets-system-in-2016-s-i-hitman-i-)).

## C. Mission concepts for Night Shift (original; no IP)
Format: title [players] - rule - steps - why fun - borrows from. Sector Ops (4P) rules can be global, per team, or split between teams; solo player inherits all rules of the teams they cover.

**Campaign (2-player)**
1. **Dead Air** [2] - Ghost: no alarms, no bodies found. Steps: climb a coastal relay mast (boost + rope hold), tap the uplink, follow the signal to a basement, wipe the logs, exfil by sea. Why: pure light/shadow test with co-op moves at many points; partner can catch a slip before it escalates. From: CT Lighthouse, Blacklist Grim.
2. **Three Grey Coats** [2] - Interrogate only the right courier; grab a wrong one and the real one bolts. Steps: three couriers in identical coats at a night market; eavesdrop, read ledgers, tail; deduce, isolate, interrogate in shadow; take the cipher. Why: deduction hook beyond sightlines. From: Lady Boyle, Freelancer showdown.
3. **Glass Floor** [2] - Asymmetric info: one in the camera room (sees all, cannot move), one on the floor (cannot see the feeds); swap at the midpoint vault. Steps: seize security room, guide partner to a dossier, swap, second player guides back out. Why: talking is the mechanic; nobody idles. From: Keep Talking, Siege drone phase.
4. **Two-Key Ledger** [2] - No kills; vault needs two keys turned at once in separate wings. Steps: lift a manager's keycard, social-engineer the night clerk for the code, reach both key posts, turn on a shared count, take bonds. Why: hard sync point, two routes per wing. From: CT Bank, Palisade, Payday Big Bank.
5. **The Turning House** [2] - Ghost. One player works the hidden gear room that shifts walls; the other moves through the rooms it opens. Steps: enter via chimney, find the gear room, rebuild rooms to reach the study, steal the plans, reverse the house to cover your tracks. Why: space as a co-op puzzle. From: Clockwork Mansion.
6. **The Last Witness** [2] - Interrogate one person; she lies unless you hold leverage found earlier. Steps: break into her flat for a letter, find her at a hotel bar, separate her from minders, interrogate (truth only with leverage), act on the real location. Why: intel changes the outcome; bluffing has a cost. From: Dartmoor, Detroit station.
7. **One Night Only** [2] - One attempt, no checkpoint; target leaves at 03:00 game time. Opt-in mission with briefing intel up front and higher pay. Why: tension and performance; opt-in avoids the fear tax. From: Elusive Targets.
8. **Paper Trail** [2] - Plant, don't take: frame a rival broker; not seen, no kills. Steps: photograph his signature, forge a transfer at an office, plant a burner in his car, leak to a guard's radio. Why: plants have to happen in watched places. From: Framing Frame, Whittleton Creek.
9. **Lights Out Ward** [2] - No gadgets: kit confiscated at entry; recover it piece by piece. Steps: enter a shut sanatorium, find gear lockers, read staff logs for the route, reach the records room, exfil as power fails. Why: atmosphere and scarcity, short enough to avoid tedium. From: Shalebridge Cradle.

**Sector Ops (4-player; two teams or one solo)**
10. **Split Shift** [4] - Split rules: Team Ghost (upper offices) must not be detected; Team Shadow (basement) may knock out but must keep the power cut alive on a timer. Steps: Shadow cuts power and holds it; Ghost downloads from three offices in the dark; both meet at the roof. Why: different verbs, same clock. From: Big Score, Payday.
11. **Code Relay** [4] - Team A interrogates a night manager for a rotating vault code; Team B at the vault must enter it within 30 s. Rule: no kills for both; interrogation fails if the manager sees a body. Why: hard sync and voice moment. From: GTFO terminal relays.
12. **Scout Night / Strike Night** [4] - Two-part op. Night 1: two scouts photograph entries; being seen closes that entry. Night 2: full squad picks routes from what was scouted. Why: intel-dependent routes; prep pays off. From: Cayo scope-out, GTA setups.
13. **Shared Fuse** [4] - Global rule: no kills. One alarm meter for the whole site, each team's noise feeds it; a spent gadget per team can lower it. Steps: three objectives across a harbour; meter thresholds add patrols, then lock doors. Why: teams care about each other without instant fail. From: Invisible Inc alarm, CT escalation.
14. **The Guest List** [4] - Team Mask works the gala in disguise (no weapons, no running); Team Night works the grounds (must stay out of lit areas). Steps: Night disables gate cameras; Mask identifies the host's aide; both isolate and interrogate the aide in the garden. Why: social stealth inside, classic stealth outside. From: Lady Boyle, Paris, Sgail.
15. **Harbour Lights** [4] - Three simultaneous objectives (plant tracker on a ship, steal manifest, sabotage a crane); solo can do all, ranks reward splitting. Per-objective optional rules shown on the board (ghost, no knockouts, under 5 min). Why: player-chosen challenge per team. From: Helldivers 2 side objectives, DRG roles.
16. **Mole Hunt** [4] - Split rule: Team A no kills, Team B never seen. Each team holds half the clues to an informant hidden among staff; combine, then one interrogation only. Why: both teams must share info to win. From: Freelancer, Dartmoor.
17. **Hot Extraction** [4] - Retrieve under pressure: objectives are ghosted, then the data pickup trips a lockdown on purpose; all four must exfil by two separate routes, one carrying the drive. Why: climax without fail-on-detection; stealth turns into an escape, not a reload. From: Payday stealth-to-loud, Big Score getaway.

**Design guardrails for all concepts:** readable detection; escalation over instant fail (except opt-in One Night Only); no escorts (VIPs move on their own schedule); 3+ entries per section; diegetic leads with a guidance toggle; both players always have a verb; section rating rewards rule-keeping.
