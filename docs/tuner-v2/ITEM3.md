**Stuck-foot cause (evidence, `tests/tuneStep.test.ts`).** CT MotionDriver run path at 60 Hz (4.0 m/s, 90 deg turn, down to the walk and back, 135 deg turn back; free movement, the rig's reach 0.505 m, CT quickStop). At the run the gait clock never lifts the trailing foot: 8 of 9 run toe-offs come from the replant rule, 1 from the clock. The plant is held by the clock's contact window (`wantContact = sinceStrike < i.duty`, then `holdOrStep`): duty 0.4 (the graph's clamp floor; the DASH clip is 0.38) x cycle 0.63 s = 0.25 s = 1.0 m of travel, but the landing is only 0.35 m ahead. So the foot stays locked until `holdOrStep`'s `dl > i.reach * 0.92` (0.465 m behind the hip) pulls it off. Worst planted distance 0.4646 m against the 0.4649 m limit, with the leg at full stretch. Crouched run: 0.596 of 0.600 m, 3 rule and 3 clock toe-offs. No drag (`slide` 0) and no foot planted past the limit: the foot is held too long, not slid.
**Fix (a tuning value, defaults unchanged).** Stepping > `run` > replant **0.8** (also `crouchRun` 0.8; `jog` too if it shows). The trailing foot lifts at 0.37 m instead of 0.465 m: 9 cm sooner (worst 0.377 m), still no drag, no foot past the limit. Crouched run worst 0.596 -> 0.473 m. A sweep showed 0.75 is the floor: at 0.65 both feet are in the air for up to 0.3 s (floaty). No CT Core diff is needed. The real cause is the graph's duty floor 0.4 against the run's long stride. That is a later option for CT Core (animGraph, not the planner), not proposed here.
