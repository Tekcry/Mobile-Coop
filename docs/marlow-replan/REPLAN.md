## What changes from the yard you walked (4.82.0)
- One way through every section. Side routes are closed with pallets, locked doors and screens. Only the last area, the
  Strongroom, keeps side routes, to practise against guards.
- Every co-op move is taught once, on its own, where it makes sense, including the five moves you added today. The Data
  Hall and the Strongroom then combine them.
