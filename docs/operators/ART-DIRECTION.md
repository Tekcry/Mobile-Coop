# Operators - art direction

Michael, 2026-10-15 (RUNBOOK step 3 brief). The standard for every operator asset from now on, alongside the detail
standard in `SPEC.md` Section 1. Where this disagrees with SPEC, the conflict is listed in `DECISIONS.md` (D13) and
SPEC is followed until Michael decides.

## 1. Core identity

- Elite covert infiltrators: alone or in small teams, at night, inside places they should not be.
- Heavily inspired by Splinter Cell, above all Chaos Theory. Quiet professionals, grounded military realism, near
  future but believable, nothing fantastical.
- Every item has a job. Kit with no purpose does not exist. Anything that would rattle, shine or snag is taped,
  tucked, matted or removed.
- Mood: dark, tactical, cold, controlled. Dangerous because they look competent, not because they look aggressive.
- Original, always:
  - no copy of Sam Fisher's suit or any Splinter Cell character;
  - no three-lens goggle cluster with three green lights;
  - no Third Echelon, Ubisoft, flag, unit or brand logos;
  - no recognisable trademarked equipment shapes.
- The inspiration is the tone, the function and the darkness, never a specific design.

## 2. Body and silhouette

- Lean, athletic, trained: climbers and swimmers, not bodybuilders. Defined shoulders and forearms, narrow waist,
  strong legs. Functional, not bulky. (Waist 0.93 of SPEC A4, D13.)
- Wren 1.68 m, Moth 1.78 m. Builds, roles and differences come from SPEC 4.4 and the joint export.
- Low profile: kit sits tight. Nothing sticks out past the shoulders or hips except what must (holster, pack).
- The silhouette reads instantly against a rim light: head with optic, shoulder line, harness straps, thigh holster,
  compact pack low on the back.
- Nothing loose or flowing: no capes, coats, scarves, dangling straps or loose cords. Every strap end is rolled,
  banded or tucked.

## 3. Layer system (head to toe)

| Layer | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Base layer | Thin compression top and leggings, close knit; seen only at the collar and cuffs |
| Combat shirt | Knit torso under armour, ripstop sleeves (Section 6) |
| Armour | Slick low-profile plate carrier cut high; laser-cut webbing, a few flat pouches; quick-release cable at the front; rounded edges that do not catch |
| Harness | Slim shoulder straps, narrow battle belt, low-profile buckle; gear where a crawling or climbing operative wants it |
| Trousers | Articulated knees with internal pad pockets, gusseted crotch, reinforced seat and knees, flat flapped cargo pockets, tapered ankles sealing over the boots |
| Thigh holster | Drop-leg platform for the pistol (its camera jammer is part of the pistol, not a separate gadget) |
| Gloves | Thin, close fitting; suede palm, stretch knit back, low-profile knuckle protection, no bulk at the fingertips |
| Boots | Low-profile tactical, soft quiet soles, matte leather and suede, short ankle |
| Head | Snug matte knit hood or balaclava; an original head-mounted multi-mode optic (night vision, thermal, camera feed) on a low-profile mount; slim earpiece with boom mic. Optic up: the face is in shadow. Optic down: it covers the eyes. Wren's housing is square (green), Moth's round twin lenses (amber) (SPEC 2) |
| Back | Compact flat pack, low: rope, tools, spare gear; nothing tied outside |
| Comms | Cables under straps, push-to-talk on the chest or shoulder, a small IR strobe for team identification |

## 4. Colour

- Near-black, deep charcoal, dark slate, dark olive. No pure black: albedo stays above about 0.02-0.03 linear so
  forms still read under a rim light.
- Value contrast comes from material, not colour: knit, ripstop, Cordura, leather, rubber and polymer are each
  distinguishable in near-darkness by texture and sheen alone.
- Light accents only where real kit has them: the IR strobe, status LEDs. Muted and small. No glowing suit lines, no
  neon, no decorative light.
- Exception (Michael, D13): the goggle lenses glow clearly in the dark, as SPEC 2's signature (Wren green, Moth amber).
- No bright colours, no camouflage, no flags or patches. Hook-and-loop identity panels stay blank.

## 5. Materials and wear

- Fabrics: matte nylon ripstop with a visible fine grid, soft knit torso, heavy Cordura reinforcements, stretch panels
  at the joints. Roughness about 0.7-0.95; only a faint fibre sheen at grazing angles.
- Hard parts: matte polymer buckles and clips, matte coated metal, rubberised edges. No chrome, no gloss.
- Construction holds up at close range: double-stitched seams, bar tacks at stress points, taped seam edges, zip
  garages, folded hems, hook-and-loop with a visible loop texture.
- Wear is subtle and earned: light dust at knees, elbows and boot toes; slight abrasion and fading on high-wear edges;
  a little grime in seams. Maintained professionals, not apocalypse survivors.

## 6. The combat shirt

- Torso: breathable knit, close fit, worn under armour, slight stretch at the sides.
- Sleeves: ripstop, raglan shoulder seams, articulated pre-bent elbows, gusseted underarms (arms reach overhead
  without lifting the hem).
- Collar: quarter-zip mock neck, snug to the neck, zip garage at the top.
- Upper arms: slanted pockets with flap closures and a blank hook-and-loop panel on each.
- Elbows: internal pad pockets with reinforced outer panels in heavier fabric.
- Cuffs: snug, with a hook-and-loop tab so they seal over the gloves.
- Colours: charcoal knit torso, near-black ripstop sleeves, slightly darker reinforcement panels.

## 7. How they read in the game

- In darkness: a silhouette. The rim light catches the shoulder line, the arms, the optic and kit edges; the body
  falls to near-black.
- Under a lamp or torch the material detail appears: seams, the fabric grid, wear, knit against ripstop.
- In a still frame they belong to a serious covert unit and could vanish into the shadow behind them.

## 8. Avoid

- Sci-fi power armour, bulky exosuits, cyberpunk, glowing suit lines, superhero styling.
- Mall-ninja gear: skulls, spikes, oversized knives, excess pouches, decorative straps.
- Parade or infantry looks: covered helmets with scrim, heavy pouch-laden carriers, camouflage.
- Fantasy and stylised cartoon proportions.

## Render check (every operator render, with SPEC 1's checklist)

1. Does the silhouette read in near-darkness?
2. Does the fabric stay matte?
3. Do knit and ripstop read as different materials without colour?
4. Do the folds look sewn, not sculpted?
5. Does anything break the covert-operative feel?
