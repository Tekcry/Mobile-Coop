# Wren concept A2 (D23, scratch)

The renders are A2-{front,back34,side,rim,chest,back,thigh-right,thigh-left,boot}.webp and A2-sheet.png (front / back34 / side / rim). They were rendered with Cycles OptiX on the RTX 4090 at 90 spp: full figure 1000x1500, close-ups 1200x1400. The light rig and AgX exposure (-1.9, Medium High Contrast) match the concepts pass. The script is work/stealth_a2.py (= stealth_a2_head.py, which is stealth.py's frames and helpers, plus a2_part.py). Rerun with: `blender -b work\base.blend --python work\stealth_a2.py -- --concept A2 --dest A2 --samples 90 --shots front,back34,side,rim,chest,back,thigh-right,thigh-left,boot`.

## Items (where it sits, why, how it is reached)
- **Suit.** A one-piece suit with a diagonal chest zip. Seams are bonded with tonal tape and there is no visible stitching (the stitch layer is hidden). The main panels have a tonal diamond micro-emboss, about 1.6 mm, which only shows close up. Laser-perforated vents (1.4 mm holes on a 3.2 mm pitch) sit at the underarm side panels and the backs of the knees. Rubberised micro-pebble grip panels cover the forearms and knees, for crawling, vents and wall contact. Abrasion panels are at the shoulder yokes, elbows and seat. Stretch knit is at the inner elbows and rear shoulders.
- **Thumb loops.** These sit under the glove cuffs, so they are not visible with gloves on.
- **Armour.** A composite chest plate with a neck scoop and three overlapping rib segments, plus a slim back plate. Edges are chamfered and rubber-trimmed, all from one corner-radius family. The finish is matte resin with a faint fibre texture.
- **Harness.** 22 mm side straps under the arms and slim shoulder straps. Every closure is a flush gunmetal magnetic quick-release, two halves on a rubber edge: no hook-and-loop and no metal that can rattle. This fits the silent-suit foley.
- **Rifle.**
  - A compact suppressed carbine with an original shape: skeletal stock with a rubber butt, enclosed low sight, short swept magazine, rounded handguard with an elastic sling keeper, and a plain cylindrical suppressor.
  - It lies diagonally on the back, stock up behind the left shoulder blade and muzzle down to the right hip. It is held at two points: a composite riser with a gunmetal clamp and release lever on the back plate at the receiver, and a moulded cup on the waist belt round the suppressor (the muzzle shows below it).
  - It is clear of the head (the butt top is at about 1.38 m, 14 cm off the spine) and clear of the pod.
  - To draw it, the left hand reaches over the left shoulder, flips the lever and lifts the stock. The suppressor slides up out of the cup and the right hand takes the grip as the gun comes past the shoulder.
- **Pistol.** A suppressed sidearm in a slim polymer holster with a retention hood (gunmetal release), at mid right thigh. It is held by two elastic leg bands with magnetic buckles and a short hanger from the waist belt. The right hand draws it straight down at the hand's natural reach.
- **Rifle magazines.** Two on the chest plate, just right of centre. They are open-top with shock cord and pull tabs, and their tops lean toward the left hand, which takes them across the chest.
- **Gadget slot.** A small flapped slot with a magnetic tab, upper right of the chest plate. It holds the EMP (1) and the noisemakers (2): flat, stick-on discs that are taken often.
- **Throwables.** Three slim double sleeves on a laminate left-thigh panel (two elastic bands, a hanger from the belt). Each sleeve holds two canisters, one gadget type per sleeve: frag, sleeping gas and flashbang, two of each, matching the carry counts in gadgets.ts. Each cap differs by touch: a knurled ring (frag), a dome (gas) and two ridges (flash). A shock-cord loop and pull tab sit over each cap. The left hand draws them, leaving the right hand on the weapon.
- **Pistol magazines.** Two in a slim open-top double pouch on the left front of the waist belt, at the support hand's natural reach.
- **Sticky cams and mines.** A small flat flapped pouch with a magnetic tab at the left hip (2 cams, 1 mine). The left hand reaches it.
- **Drone.** The folded tri-rotor and its battery ride in a small hard pod (rubber lid seal, gunmetal latch) low on the back, left of the rifle line. It hangs from the back plate on two short straps. Either hand reaches it behind the back, and its place is clear of the rifle's swing.
- **Medkit.** A flat tear-away pouch on the waist belt under the pod, with a webbing pull handle at its bottom edge and a visible tear seam. Either hand reaches it behind the back.
- **Knife.** A slim knife on the chest plate, left of centre, handle down, rubber handle with a gunmetal guard, pommel and release. The right hand draws it across the body, pulling down.
- **Comms.**
  - The radio is a slim module set into a recess in the upper left of the back plate, under the rifle stock, so it is protected. Its antenna is a short stub along the plate's top edge in a keeper.
  - The radio cable runs over the left shoulder along the strap edge, held by three elastic keepers, to the PTT at the left collarbone.
  - The step 07 earpiece cable plugs into the PTT there (it is shown again, routed up the neck to the left ear).
- **Waist belt.** A slim laminate belt with a flush magnetic front closure. One flush rappel ring sits at the back centre in a low tunnel: a gunmetal D folded flat, with only its bow showing. There is no sit harness, no leg loops and no gear loops.
- **Boots.** A lighter approach boot: the step 06 boot cut down to about 12-14 cm (from 16), a padded collar, a heel pull loop, a sticky rubber rand round the sole that rises over the toe, and lugs flattened to about half their height.
- **Kept.** Wren's body, the hood and balaclava, the original square goggle on its band, the strap-2 head harness and the gloves. There is no wrist computer, no three-lens goggle and no brands.

## Changes to the proposal, and why
- **Radio.** Moved into the upper left of the back plate, under the stock, instead of "under the pod". The pod has to sit low and left of the rifle line, so there is no plate under it. The shoulder-strap route to the left-collarbone PTT is also the shortest one there.
- **Pod.** Placed left of centre, not centred, so the diagonal rifle and its handguard stay clear of it.
- **Medkit.** Moved to the lower back left, on the belt under the pod, instead of lower right. The suppressor and its belt cup take the lower right.
- **Rifle magazine.** Short (about 9 cm) and swept toward the muzzle. A straight mag at right angles to the gun stuck about 5 cm out past the right side of the torso in the first test.
- **Gun mounting.** The rifle's lower mount is a cup on the waist belt, not on the plate. The slim back plate ends at the ribs, and the belt is exactly where the suppressor crosses it.
- **Throwables.** The three sleeves are double sleeves. The game carries 9 throwables (frag 2, gas 2, flash 2, EMP 1, noise 2), and three single sleeves could not hold them. The EMP and noisemakers are flat stick-on discs, so they go in the chest gadget slot.
- **Leg-band frames.** The thigh frames were rebuilt with a short ray reach. Inner-thigh rays found the other leg and bridged a band across the crotch.

## Known scratch limits
- A-pose only.
- The rifle stands about 3 cm off the back plate on average (see the side view), because it is a rigid straight gun over a curved back.
- The pod reads slightly shield-shaped from the waist curvature.
- The boot keeps the step 06 last and lacing, so it still reads as a short boot rather than a true approach shoe.
- The underarm vents and the micro-texture only read up close.
- The hand hides the pistol grip in the right-thigh close-up.
