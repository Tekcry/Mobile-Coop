# Night Shift - campaign outline (draft)
Owner: the orchestrator. Status: current (draft from the planning run, 2026-11-02; the ending is written in Phase 1,
N36; the rest of the story is decided with the missions, STORY-03).

The story spine from `docs/story.md` mapped onto the Target tier (N31: prologue + 7 missions; final at the Phase 7
slice), where the twist and the emotional turn land, and where each team move is taught and reused. Mission 1 is a
fresh design (N2): its setting is chosen in level planning, so "Dead Line" below is the story plan's candidate.

## Story spine (kept, P3)
- **Hook:** someone will switch off Hollowmere. A deniable two-operator unit, NIGHT SHIFT, hears one word on a tapped
  line: SUNDOWN, nine nights away. The team that lives in the dark has to keep the lights on.
- **Two temperaments** (story.md 4), same abilities (N34): **Wren**, team lead, ex-military, terse, carries the
  conscience ("when Wren talks at length it matters"); **Moth**, climber, ex-steeplejack, cheerful, curious, drawn to
  light. Each needs a clear arc (Hazelight: growth is what connects players), written with the ending in Phase 1.
  Banter is dry, reacts to what just happened, is silent on alert and never spells out a solution.
- **The radio crew:** LANTERN (Peg, the handler), Tally (signals), Sexton (surveillance). Text only (D16), one line
  at a time for both players.
- **Midpoint reveal:** in Lantern Out, intel proves the Office's own director is the Client (story.md 5).
- **Late turn:** SUNDOWN starts early; the city goes dark mid-mission (Substation Zero).
- **The ending** (written in Phase 1, N36): the team turns the lights back on; the last image is Hollowmere lighting up
  block by block, seen from a rooftop. Co-operative to the last scene (no player against player, G9); both players get
  the same story text (no secret knowledge for one).
- **The emotional turn uses the co-op bond through the controls** (Brothers' model, not A Way Out's fight):
  **"Two switches"** (N41, `ENDING-DRAFT.md` A). With the radio jammed, Wren holds the control room and Moth the roof
  generator, each the other's eyes by pings; the last press is the two-key "both ready" action from night 1; Moth,
  drawn to light, turns the city back on. Earlier, Lantern Out (the radio dies) rehearses it.
  Hints planted from Mission 1 on (`ENDING-DRAFT.md`).

The midpoint reveal falls at about 70% of the run; the blackout sits just before the finale. The Minimum tier (5)
folds Glass House into High Water and the Strongroom, and Substation Zero into the finale; the Stretch tier (9) gives
Cold Storage and The Cutting their own nights.

**Length risk:** about 4 hours is half of A Way Out (6-8 hours), and CT's top complaint was a short campaign. D13's
final call at the slice weighs mission length, price and the real cost per mission together.

## Each night also has (Hazelight research, N37)
- One short optional quiet beat (1-2 minutes): a rooftop wait, the van, the exit; a both-press moment with banter.
- 1-3 unarmed staff with routines; at least one overheard conversation that opens an opportunity.
- At most one joint approach choice: "Wren's way" (patient, low, on the ground) or "Moth's way" (bold, high, over the
  roofs); both must agree; it changes the route and play, and echoes next night.

## Team moves: taught, then reused
The kept list comes from Phase 4; this is the plan for the moves most likely to survive. Teaching order (Marlow Yard,
interdependence rising; each move taught in four steps: safe solo use, one handover, a hold, both under time
pressure): leg-up, jam alternation, partner throw, leg-up and ledge relay, hang-down, a two-key action, rope.

| Move | Taught | Reused (rising difficulty) | Combined with | Frequency per mission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leg-up (boost) | Prologue 1 | Every mission | Ledge relay, throw | 3-5 |
| Jam / light sharing | Prologue 2 | 1, 2, 3 (motion lights), 6 (the blackout flips it) | Distract-and-act | 2-4 |
| Partner throw, far-side release | Prologue 3 | 1, 2, 4 | Lure and ambush | 0-2 |
| Ledge relay | Prologue 4 | 1, 3, 5 | Hang-down, rescue | 1-2 |
| Hang-down | Prologue 5 | 2, 4, 5 | Overwatch | about 1 |
| Two-key action (relaxed sync) | Prologue 6 | 1 (the cage), 4 (the vault), finale | Protect-while-act | 1-2 |
| Rope / belay | Prologue 7 or Mission 2 | 2, 6 | Escape route | 0-1 |
| Double drop (joint takedown) | Mission 1 | Every mission with guard pairs | Lure | 1+ |
