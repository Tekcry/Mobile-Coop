# Mission rules: Three Grey Coats and campaign limits
kind: minor
- Three Grey Coats: knocking Teale out before he talks restarts the section, with him back at his table. No break is counted.
- Three Grey Coats: killing Teale before he talks, or questioning a decoy, breaks the rule. He moves to the clock-mender's back loft and the section restarts.
- Campaign missions: at most 32 guards alive at once, no optional rewards, no shield lead in response teams. A solo campaign start goes back to the menu.
- Tallow Wharf: no dogs. Their posts are held by armed guards instead.
- All of this ships dark behind the `missions` flag (`?missions=1`).
