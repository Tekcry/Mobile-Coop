# New interaction prompts in co-op and the knife scene (preview only)
kind: minor
- In the preview, the new prompts now cover co-op and the special moments: hold A on a downed partner to revive them; beside your partner's held guard, A is the team takedown and Y bluffs; hanging head-first, A grabs the guard below or uses what is in reach, with "Pull me up" on your partner; while you offer a leg-up, A still uses the switch or panel beside you, with its prompt on it; carrying a body, A hides it, opens a door or puts it down where the prompt shows; in the knife scene A or the attack button is the knife takedown. Team moves are named shortly on your partner ("Take the leg-up"), with any waiting text on a small second line. The live game is unchanged.
