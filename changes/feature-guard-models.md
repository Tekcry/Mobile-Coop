# Guard models
kind: minor
- Guards can draw as realistic figures from the operator pipeline (guard-models G7, behind `?guardmodels=1` while the
  budget is measured; `?guardmodels=0` keeps today's merged body): each guard's look (head, skin, hair, headwear, kit,
  colours) comes from his look seed, so host and client see the same man and a section restart gives every slot the
  same look. Helmets, vests and the heavy plate show exactly what stops a round; a knocked-off helmet hides on him and
  drops at his feet; carried bodies keep their look. Until the baked guard files land, Wren's figure stands in.
