# Progression and cosmetics removed
kind: minor
- Clean-up I: progression is gone: XP, levels, credits, weapon upgrades, unlocks, rewards, the suit and its armour / gloves / boots / pouches, the HQ upgrades (supply drops, field medic training), challenges, weapon mastery and camos, the profile badge and the results screen's rewards slot. `src/progression/` is deleted; the attachment data moved to `src/weapons/attachments.ts` and the loadout reads to `src/weapons/loadout.ts`. Everything runs at the issued values (no armour, noise, hands or gadget-carry modifiers; the revive takes its normal time). Nothing is bought or unlocked; the Loadout row is still the read-only Kit screen.
- Clean-up I: cosmetics are gone: `src/cosmetics/` is deleted (the avatar catalogue, patterns, camos, tags and emblems) with the saved look and the player tag. Wren in the main menu and every player in a match wear the one default look (the operator suit and comms headset, charcoal with a dark accent) and the factory weapon colours. A guard kind keeps its own look from `config/enemies.json`. A player's pings and lobby dot use one fixed colour (the old default tag colour, orange); the lobby no longer shows a title.
- The save is now version 11. A v10 profile loads: the name, the attachments on each weapon, the loadout and the presets are kept; XP, credits, unlocks, upgrades, kill counts, camos, the look, the tag, lifetime stats, the per-mission bests of the old Infiltration, the suit, HQ and challenges are dropped (nothing is refunded). Older saves (v1 to v9) still load. Settings, the campaign, course, Sector Ops and rating records and the animation tuner's presets are not touched. Settings > Data now says "Reset profile".
- The co-op version is now 90 (`hello` and the lobby carry no `tag` or `look`; the end-of-match stats carry no `weaponKills`), so an older build cannot join.
- Tests: the e2e suites `e2e-progression` and `e2e-cosmetics` and the unit tests for progression, the economy and the cosmetics catalogue are deleted; `e2e-offline` and `e2e-park` check the v11 profile, `e2e-weapons-carry` no longer sets a backpack look. New unit tests cover the 10 -> 11 migration, a stored v10 save beside the other stores, and the v1 to v10 steps.
