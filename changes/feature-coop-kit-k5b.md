# Co-op kit: Plank Bridge, laying the board and crossing
kind: minor
- Co-op (on in /preview/, not yet in the live game): Plank Bridge is playable. Two players carry the scaffold board to
  the edge of a gap and lay it across together: it slides out and rests on both sides, then both can walk over it. It
  reaches across gaps of 1 to 3.3 m with both sides about level; when it cannot be laid, a short line says why (too
  wide, not level, something in the way). A guard who sees a laid board comes to look, as with a held-open shutter. A
  laid board can be lifted again from either end and carried on, and a restart puts it back on its stack. At Marlow Yard
  this is station 5's way over the pit once the station uses it.
