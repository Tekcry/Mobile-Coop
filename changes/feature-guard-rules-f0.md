# Guard barks and pairs
kind: minor
- A guard who rises to investigate now says what drove it: a sound gives a "heard" line, a sight gives a "saw" line,
  and a light or body check gives a neutral line. A guard who saw you no longer says "I heard something."
- A guard who stays calm forgets an old glimpse or noise, so a later noise is never reported as a sighting.
- (dark, `?guardpairs=1`) Guards who patrol together notice within about 3 seconds when their partner goes missing,
  and two posted guards close together chat every 40 seconds and check on each other the same way.
